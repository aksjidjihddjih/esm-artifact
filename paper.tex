\noindent\textbf{\large Abstract}

\noindent Evaluating the epistemic contribution of scientific models, algorithms, and reusable abstractions in decentralized environments faces two fundamental obstacles: subjective peer evaluation is vulnerable to validator weight-copying and collusion, whereas objective one-shot compression contests lack composable citation attribution and enable free-riding on foundational theories. This paper introduces the \textbf{Epistemic State Machine (ESM)}, a deterministic cryptographic and cryptoeconomic protocol that combines Verifiable Delay Functions (VDFs), zero-knowledge STARK execution proofs, collision-resistant Counted Merkle Sum Tree (MST) commitments, and two-phase commit-reveal schemes to adjudicate Minimum Description Length (MDL) lossless compression over a bounded Binary Lambda Calculus universal machine ($U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$) with deductive citation and syntactic reduction attribution. First, ESM replaces subjective scoring with a telescoping integer escrow allocation rule governed by strict MDL frontier reductions. Second, to prevent single-dataset lookup-table (pre-embedding) gaming while rewarding reusable foundational theories, this study derives a \textbf{Burn-Backed Amortization} law in which an axiom's creditable description-length exemption is upper-bounded in exact proportion to its prior irreversible token burn. Third, to deter plagiarism and minor syntactic cloning of low-burn or dormant axioms, the protocol introduces a \textbf{Syntactic Reduction Attribution} transition ($\mathcal{T}_6$) evaluated via $T_{\max}$-fuel-bounded coinductive B\"ohm-tree $\beta\eta$-equivalence to measure conditional constructive description length between programs, coupled with a debt-following two-stage upfront reservation waterfall and symmetric puncture release. Fourth, we formulate an explicit extensive-form game model establishing dominant-strategy and subgame-perfect deterrence equilibria, validated by concrete Krivine Abstract Machine (KAM) compression benchmarks, a multi-dataset cash-flow trajectory analysis, and a five-mechanism subgame-perfect equilibrium ablation with three-axis sensitivity boundary verification and multi-agent no-regret (Hedge) learning dynamics (open-source artifact at \url{https://github.com/aksjidjihddjih/esm-artifact}). All accounting and state-transition equations are formulated over discrete natural numbers ($\mathbb{N}$). The accompanying formalization in \textbf{Lean 4.22.0} (\texttt{ESM\_verified.lean}, 1,111 lines using only the standard \texttt{Init} core and the three foundational axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound} with zero \texttt{sorryAx}) verifies the arithmetic consistency of the \textbf{discrete natural-number accounting, escrow allocation, two-stage reservation waterfall, symmetric puncture release, arithmetic supply conservation across 18 ledger transaction schemas (formalized via 12 branch theorems instantiating \texttt{global\_supply\_invariant\_preserved}), and dominance model across 53 lemmas and theorems}---confirming the absence of rounding or underflow bugs across the mechanized accounting equations under floor division and saturating subtraction (while the $\mathcal{T}_5$ dictionary sorting algorithm, the $\mathcal{T}_6$ KAM evaluator, and Kraft's inequality remain analytical specifications outside the Lean model)---and classifies all guarantees into \textbf{Class I} (algebraic invariants), \textbf{Class II} (computational soundness), and \textbf{Class III} (game-theoretic equilibria and structural limits). The complete Lean 4.22.0 formalization (53 theorems), reference BLC/KAM evaluators, and automated verification suites are publicly available at \url{https://github.com/aksjidjihddjih/esm-artifact}.

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{Motivation: Subjectivity, Free-Riding, and Single-Output Verification Limits}
The proliferation of machine learning models, automated theorem provers, and algorithmic discovery pipelines has created an urgent need for decentralized mechanisms capable of pricing informational and explanatory contributions without relying on centralized committees. Existing mechanisms fall into three architectural paradigms, each exhibiting a fundamental structural limitation:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{One-Shot Compression Contests Lack Composable Citation Attribution:} Classical Minimum Description Length (MDL) and algorithmic probability frameworks \cite{solomonoff1964,kolmogorov1965,rissanen1978,li2019} establish that compression is mathematically dual to inductive law discovery. However, operational compression benchmarks such as the Hutter Prize \cite{hutter2005} evaluate monolithic, standalone decompressor-plus-archive pairs against a single static corpus. They provide no state machine for modularly citing previously discovered programs (``axioms''), nor any mechanism to route royalties back to foundational libraries when downstream researchers synthesize new models.
  \item \textbf{Subjective Peer-Weight Protocols Suffer from Validator Collusion:} Decentralized AI networks such as Bittensor \cite{rao2021} replace objective compression metrics with continuous peer-assigned weight matrices. Because ground-truth evaluation is expensive while copying consensus weights is virtually free, rational validators face a strong incentive to copy the weights of large stake-holders rather than executing independent evaluations, leading to collusion and subjective capture.
  \item \textbf{Optimistic Single-Output Verifiers Cannot Price Generalization or Citation:} Verifiable computation protocols such as TrueBit \cite{teutsch2019} and zero-knowledge succinct transparent arguments of knowledge (STARKs) \cite{bensasson2018} verify that a specific program $p$ produces a specific output $y = p(x)$. While essential for execution integrity, they do not measure whether $p$ captures reusable structural regularities across diverse datasets, nor do they prevent an adversary from copying an open-source program $p_s$, applying a trivial wrapper $q$, and claiming full economic credit as a newly registered module $p_i = q(p_s)$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Explicit Definition of ``Oracle-Free'' Adjudication}
To maintain strict academic integrity and avoid exaggerated claims common in cryptoeconomic literature, the scope of \textbf{oracle-freeness} in ESM is explicitly defined at the outset.

\begin{definition}[Oracle-Free Epistemic Adjudication]
\label{def:oracle_free}
In the Epistemic State Machine (ESM), the term \textbf{Oracle-Free} does \textbf{not} guarantee the physical authenticity, sensor veracity, or real-world truth of external off-chain data prior to its registration. Rather, \textbf{Oracle-Free} is strictly defined as the property that once a dataset $D_k$ has been cryptographically committed on-chain, the evaluation of compression claims against $D_k$, the attribution of epistemic value to cited or syntactically reduced ancestor axioms, and the settlement of token rewards and slashing penalties are adjudicated deterministically by the state machine without human peer reviewers, subjective voting committees, or external off-chain arbiters (oracles).

\end{definition}

\subsection{Core Contributions}
This paper makes four concrete contributions to cryptoeconomics, algorithmic information theory, and formal verification:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Deterministic Epistemic State Machine (ESM) Integrating MDL Compression and Deductive Citation:} The specification formulates a complete state machine over a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) of Data Nodes ($v_k$), reusable Axiom Nodes ($v_u$), and Recipe Edges ($e_{k \leftarrow i}$) evaluated on a step-bounded Binary Lambda Calculus Krivine machine $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$ ($T_{\max} = 2^{20}$). Bounties are released via a superadditive integer telescoping escrow function $\text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta)$ synchronized across 60-block serial commit-delay-reveal batches and 9,960-block Shannon dictionary epochs.
  \item \textbf{Burn-Backed Amortization Law ($\Delta_{\text{amort}}$) Against Single-Dataset Pre-Embedding:} A central vulnerability of modular MDL markets is the lookup-table (pre-embedding) attack: an attacker embeds raw target data $D_k$ inside a ``general'' axiom $v_u$ prior to submitting a tiny wrapper recipe that cites $v_u$, thereby artificially inflating compression $\Delta$ to drain $B_k^{(0)}$. This work proves unconditionally over $\mathbb{N}$ (\leanref{single\_data\_pre\_embedding\_unprofitable}) that capping an axiom's creditable bit-length exemption $\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k)$ via prior irreversible self-burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u, < t_k)$ bounds the maximum net single-dataset reward increment to $\Delta R_{\text{net}} \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u, < t_k)$, rendering single-dataset pre-embedding strictly unprofitable once data-availability (DA) burn, gas, and lockup opportunity cost are included, while allowing honest foundational axioms to achieve positive return when cited across $N_{\text{cite}} \ge 6$ comparable datasets.
  \item \textbf{Syntactic Reduction Attribution ($\mathcal{T}_6$) with Upfront Two-Stage Reservation and Symmetric Release:} Because Burn-Backed Amortization caps direct citation royalties by prior burn, an under-capitalized or dormant foundational axiom $v_s$ could otherwise be cloned or lightly wrapped by a well-funded plagiarist $v_i$. The protocol introduces a permissionless transition $\mathcal{T}_6$ allowing any third party to prove $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}(q(p_s)) =_{\beta\eta} p_i$ for an earlier root axiom $v_s$ via $T_{\max}$-fuel-bounded coinductive B\"ohm-tree $\beta\eta$-equivalence, establishing conditional syntactic reduction length $L_{\text{red}}(v_i) \equiv |p_i| \ominus (|q| + \ell_\omega(\text{idx}(v_s)))$. Combined with a two-stage upfront reservation waterfall at tranche creation and symmetric reservation release under boundary puncture (\leanref{release\_symmetric, old\_rule\_leaks}), this prevents both derivative Sybil cap-overflow and temporary reservation griefing.
  \item \textbf{Endogenous Game-Theoretic Equilibria, Empirical Validation, and 53-Lemma Mechanized Accounting Model:} Every division, logarithm, basis-point fee, escrow slice, puncture unrolling, and debt-following royalty waterfall in ESM is formulated over $\mathbb{N}$ using floor/ceiling integer division and saturating subtraction ($\ominus$). We establish a multi-player game model proving dominant-strategy deterrence against pre-embedding, self-reduction RBF evasion, reservation griefing, and unamortized cloning, alongside an endogenous mixed-strategy watchtower inspection equilibrium addressing the Verifier's Dilemma \cite{luu2015}. Rather than claiming full-stack implementation verification, our Lean 4.22.0 artifact (\texttt{ESM\_verified.lean}, 1,111 lines, \textbf{53 lemmas and theorems} with zero \texttt{sorryAx}) mechanically verifies the arithmetic consistency of the natural-number accounting, escrow, reservation waterfall, and supply conservation model---confirming the absence of rounding or underflow bugs across the mechanized accounting equations \cite{moura2021}---complemented by concrete BLC/KAM compression benchmarks, a multi-dataset cash-flow trajectory analysis, and a five-mechanism subgame-perfect equilibrium ablation with three-axis sensitivity boundary verification and multi-agent no-regret (Hedge) learning dynamics.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work \& Positioning}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Algorithmic Information Theory and Minimum Description Length}
Solomonoff's theory of universal inductive inference \cite{solomonoff1964} and Kolmogorov complexity \cite{kolmogorov1965} formalize the epistemic principle that the optimal explanation of an observation string $x \in \{0,1\}^*$ is the shortest program $p$ on a prefix-free universal Turing machine $U$ that outputs $x$. Because exact Kolmogorov complexity $K_U(x) = \min\{|p| : U(p) = x\}$ is uncomputable due to the halting problem \cite{li2019}, Rissanen's Minimum Description Length (MDL) principle \cite{rissanen1978} restricts evaluation to computable two-part codes consisting of a resource-bounded model description length $L(M)$ plus residual data encoding length $L(D \mid M)$.

To achieve machine-independent conciseness without arbitrary instruction-set bloat, Tromp \cite{tromp2007} introduced Binary Lambda Calculus (BLC), providing a minimal self-delimiting binary encoding of untyped $\lambda$-terms alongside a compact universal machine. For prefix-free encoding of positive integers (such as step budgets, residual magnitudes, and dictionary indices), Elias $\omega$ coding \cite{elias1975} maps any integer $n \ge 1$ to a recursively length-prefixed codeword whose exact bit-length is defined recursively by $\ell_\omega(1) = 1$ and $\ell_\omega(n) = 1 + \lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + \ell_\omega(\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor)$ for $n \ge 2$ (e.g., $\ell_\omega(2) = 3\text{ bits}$, $\ell_\omega(3) = 3\text{ bits}$, and $\ell_\omega(4) = 1 + \lfloor\log_2 4\rfloor + \ell_\omega(2) = 1 + 2 + 3 = 6\text{ bits}$ for codeword \texttt{101000}). While the Hutter Prize \cite{hutter2005} operationalized resource-bounded compression as an off-chain human-administered contest over a 100 MB (and subsequently 1 GB) Wikipedia snapshot, it lacks an on-chain state machine for continuous frontier updates, composable multi-author citations, or anti-plagiarism attribution.

\subsection{Verifiable Computation and Decentralized Knowledge / AI Protocols}
On the cryptographic verification side, Ben-Sasson et al.\ \cite{bensasson2018} introduced Scalable Transparent Arguments of Knowledge (STARKs), enabling a prover to convince a verifier in polylogarithmic time $O(\text{polylog } T)$ that a deterministic transition relation executed correctly for $T$ steps using only collision-resistant hash functions and Reed-Solomon proximity testing (FRI), requiring no trusted setup. Boneh et al.\ \cite{boneh2018} formalized Verifiable Delay Functions (VDFs), guaranteeing that a pseudorandom challenge seed cannot be predicted or biased before a sequential delay of $N_{\text{delay}}$ blocks has elapsed.

In decentralized compute and AI markets, Luu et al.\ \cite{luu2015} identified the \emph{Verifier's Dilemma}, showing that rational nodes skip expensive deterministic verification unless protocol incentives explicitly make auditing profitable in equilibrium. TrueBit \cite{teutsch2019} and optimistic rollup dispute protocols such as Arbitrum \cite{kalodner2018} address single-execution verification via interactive bisection games, while Ball et al.\ \cite{ball2017} formalized Proofs of Useful Work (PoUW) based on fine-grained worst-case hardness assumptions, and Bittensor \cite{rao2021} proposed a stake-weighted peer-ranking mechanism (Yuma Consensus) for neural network subnets. Unlike PoUW schemes that reward one-shot evaluation of pre-specified polynomial functions \cite{ball2017} or optimistic dispute games that verify a single isolated state transition \cite{teutsch2019,kalodner2018}, ESM prices open-ended inductive compression across composable program DAGs while resolving the Verifier's Dilemma via a two-tier VDF spot-check and endogenous watchtower inspection game.

\subsection{Data Valuation, Peer Prediction, and Smart Contract Formal Verification}
A rich literature in algorithmic game theory studies information elicitation, data valuation, and formal contract verification:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Decision Markets and Shapley Data Valuation:} Decision markets and proper scoring rules \cite{chen2012} incentivize truthful probabilistic forecasts conditional on an observable future ground-truth outcome. In machine learning data markets, Ghorbani and Zou \cite{ghorbani2019} introduced Data Shapley to equitably attribute model performance across training contributors, and Agarwal et al.\ \cite{agarwal2019} designed an algorithmic marketplace for pricing data streams via marginal utility gains. However, exact Shapley attribution requires $O(2^N)$ marginal retraining evaluations (or noisy Monte Carlo sampling) and relies on an external validation oracle to score model accuracy. By contrast, ESM prices reusable \emph{program abstractions (axioms)} entirely without an external test oracle, replacing exponential coalitional retraining with an $O(|\mathcal{C}_i|)$ deterministic two-stage integer reservation waterfall governed by exact MDL bit reductions.
  \item \textbf{Peer Prediction and Oracle-Free Elicitation:} When objective ground truth is unavailable, peer-prediction mechanisms \cite{miller2005} and endogenous-proficiency crowdsourcing protocols \cite{dasgupta2013} reward agents based on statistical correlation between their reports. While theoretically elegant, peer-prediction systems are susceptible to uninformative symmetric equilibria in which colluding participants report identical low-effort signals---a failure mode mirrored by validator weight-copying in subjective AI networks \cite{rao2021}. ESM eliminates subjective peer correlation by requiring every compression claim and syntactic reduction to be backed by a constructive BLC program witness verified deterministically on $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$.
  \item \textbf{Smart Contract Formal Verification:} Beginning with Bhargavan et al.\ \cite{bhargavan2016}, formal verification of smart contracts has sought to eliminate arithmetic overflow, reentrancy, and accounting bugs prior to deployment. Building on the Lean 4 theorem prover and kernel architecture of de Moura and Ullrich \cite{moura2021}, ESM mechanizes all 53 theorems of its natural-number accounting core, state-transition invariants, and game-theoretic dominance lemmas directly inside the Lean 4.22.0 \texttt{Init} kernel without external library dependencies.
\end{itemize}

\section{The ESM Core Model (Exact Integer Specification)}
\label{sec:core_model}

\subsection{Base Universal Machine of BLC, Elias Coding, and Merkle Sum Trees}
All computation in ESM is defined relative to a canonical, resource-bounded Binary Lambda Calculus universal machine $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$ \cite{tromp2007} evaluated on the deterministic Krivine Abstract Machine (KAM) with a hard step ceiling of $T_{\max} = 2^{20} = 1,048,576$ reduction steps per record invocation. Every program $p \in \{0,1\}^*$ is a self-delimiting binary encoding of a closed $\lambda$-term. To ensure deterministic consensus across implementations, ESM specifies a canonical execution and I/O semantics: (i) \textbf{Scott-Encoded LSB-First Bit-List I/O:} each record index $j \in \{0, \dots, n_k - 1\}$ is passed as a width-$b_k$ (baseline $16\text{ bits}$) LSB-first Scott-encoded bit-list ($\text{B0} = \lambda a.\lambda b.\, a$, $\text{B1} = \lambda a.\lambda b.\, b$, $\text{nil} = \text{B1}$, $\text{cons } h\, t = \lambda c.\lambda n.\, c\, h\, t$), and the output closure is lazily decoded bit-by-bit (\texttt{decode\_bits}) across its first $b_k$ elements to reconstruct the predicted integer $\hat{y}_j \in \{0, \dots, 2^{b_k} - 1\}$ (specifically, the decoder applies each list element $b$ to two opaque markers $(\texttt{MK0}, \texttt{MK1})$ and classifies it as bit $0$ if it reduces to $\texttt{MK0}$ or bit $1$ if it reduces to $\texttt{MK1}$, while passing an opaque end-of-list marker $\texttt{MKEND}$ as the list's \texttt{nil} argument so that any output list terminating in fewer than $b_k$ elements or yielding a non-marker term is strictly detected as a non-bit output); and (ii) \textbf{KAM Step Accounting \& Totalized Fallback:} the call-by-name Krivine Abstract Machine (KAM) counts $1\text{ step}$ for each application push (\texttt{App}), $\beta$-reduction pop (\texttt{Lam}), and de Bruijn environment lookup (\texttt{Var(idx)}), summing the WHNF reduction steps and the $b_k$-bit decoding steps as the per-record step count ($1 \le \delta \le T_{\max}$). If evaluation exceeds the proposer's declared per-record step ceiling $\delta \le T_{\max}$ ($\text{steps} > \delta$), encounters an open/unparseable term, or reaches a non-bit output, the evaluator deterministically falls back to $\hat{y}_j \equiv 0^{b_k}$ and charges the fallback record the full declared step ceiling $\delta$ (rendering the evaluation function total over all binary strings, while any mismatch against the committed MST leaf is deterministically rejected or slashed under $\mathcal{T}_1 / \mathcal{T}_2$).

To avoid floating-point non-determinism across heterogeneous hardware, all arithmetic in ESM is restricted to $\mathbb{N} = \{0, 1, 2, \dots\}$. Saturating subtraction is denoted $a \ominus b \equiv \max(a - b, 0)$ (implemented as \texttt{a - b} on \texttt{Nat} in Lean 4), floor division as $\lfloor a / b \rfloor$ (with $\lfloor a / 0 \rfloor \equiv 0$ in totalized arithmetic), and ceiling division for $b \ge 1$ as:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ceildiv}
\text{ceilDiv}(a, b) \equiv \left\lceil \frac{a}{b} \right\rceil \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{a + b - 1}{b} \right\rfloor, \qquad
\text{clog2}(x) \equiv \min\big\{k \in \mathbb{N} : 2^k \ge \max(x, 1)\big\}.
\end{equation}
Here, $\text{clog2}(x)$ provides the exact integer definition of both the binary Merkle tree depth $\text{clog2}(n_k)$ and the integer floor logarithm $\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor \equiv \text{clog2}(n + 1) - 1$ for $n \ge 1$. Positive integers $n \ge 1$ (including execution step counts $\delta \ge 1$ and the sequential registration index $\text{idx}(v_s) \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$ assigned to each axiom $v_s$ in order of reveal adoption) are encoded via Elias $\omega$ coding \cite{elias1975} with recursive bit-length $\ell_\omega(1) = 1$ and $\ell_\omega(n) = 1 + \lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + \ell_\omega(\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor)$ for $n \ge 2$. When a recipe predicts $\hat{y}_j = U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}(p_{\text{rec}}(j))$ for record index $j \in \{0, \dots, n_k - 1\}$ of dataset $D_k = (y_0, \dots, y_{n_k - 1}) \in (\{0,1\}^{b_k})^{n_k}$, the residual error $r_j \equiv y_j \oplus \hat{y}_j$ (mapped bijectively to $\mathbb{N}_{\ge 0}$ via unsigned integer interpretation or ZigZag encoding with $\text{biject}(0) = 0$, so that $\text{biject}(r_j) + 1 \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$) is committed into a binary \textbf{Merkle Sum Tree (MST)}. Here, $p_{\text{rec}}$ denotes the composite recipe evaluation term binding the cited axioms $\mathcal{C}_i$ and the local wrapper/witness parameters $w_i$ (and the general formulation incorporating an explicit validity domain specification $\Omega_{k,i}$ or non-zero tolerance thresholds reduces identically to the same MST residual bit-length sum $L_{\text{res}}$). Each internal node of the MST commits both the cryptographic hash of its children and the exact integer sum of their residual bit-lengths, making the total residual bit-length $L_{\text{res}} = \sum_{j=0}^{n_k-1} \ell_{\text{res}}(r_j)$ verifiable at the root and enabling $O(\log_2 n_k)$ range-sum proofs during spot-checking and boundary punctures. Specifically, for a dataset with $k_{\text{err}} \in \{0, \dots, n_k\}$ non-zero residual leaves at sorted record indices $0 \le j_1 < j_2 < \dots < j_{k_{\text{err}}} < n_k$, the sparse MST root encoding defines the total residual description length as $L_{\text{res}} \equiv \ell_\omega(k_{\text{err}} + 1) + c_{\text{hdr}} + \sum_{m=1}^{k_{\text{err}}} \big(\ell_\omega(\Delta j_m) + b_k\big)$, where $\ell_\omega(k_{\text{err}} + 1)$ is the Elias $\omega$ outlier-count header ($1\text{ bit}$ when $k_{\text{err}} = 0$), $c_{\text{hdr}} = 33\text{ bits}$ is the fixed metadata header ($1\text{ bit}$ sparse/dense mode flag $+ 16\text{ bits}$ tree-depth and record-count framing $+ 16\text{ bits}$ residual bit-width descriptor $= 33\text{ bits}$, yielding $L_{\text{res}} = 1 + 33 = 34\text{ bits}$ when $k_{\text{err}} = 0$), $\Delta j_m \ge 1$ ($\Delta j_1 \equiv j_1 + 1$ and $\Delta j_m \equiv j_m - j_{m-1}$ for $m \ge 2$) is the index gap encoded via Elias $\omega$, and $b_k$ is the residual amplitude width. Furthermore, because each internal MST node also commits the exact integer count of non-zero residual leaves (outlier count) in its subtree as a \textbf{Counted Merkle Sum Tree}, the index gap $\Delta j_m$ to the preceding non-zero leaf is deterministically verifiable in $O(\log_2 n_k)$ Merkle path steps during Mode~II-A and Mode~III single-leaf verification. Conversely, in the dense mode ($1\text{ bit}$ mode flag $= 1$, used when outliers are frequent), index-gap codes are omitted and all $n_k$ residuals are encoded sequentially as $L_{\text{res}}^{\text{dense}} \equiv c_{\text{hdr}} + \sum_{j=0}^{n_k-1} \ell_\omega(\text{biject}(r_j) + 1)$, allowing each recipe to select the mode achieving $L_{\text{res}} = \min(L_{\text{res}}^{\text{sparse}}, L_{\text{res}}^{\text{dense}})$.

\subsection{Knowledge DAG: Data Nodes, Axiom Nodes, Recipe Edges, and Escrow Allocation}
The global state of ESM maintains a typed Directed Acyclic Graph $\mathcal{G} = (\mathcal{V}_D \cup \mathcal{V}_A, \mathcal{E})$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Data Nodes ($v_k \in \mathcal{V}_D$):} Created via transition $\mathcal{T}_0$, a data node commits a dataset $D_k$ consisting of $n_k \ge 1$ records of $b_k \ge 1$ bits each, establishing the initial trivial (uncompressed) description length $\Phi_k^{(0)} \equiv n_k b_k \ge 1$, an initial bounty pool $B_k^{(0)} \ge 1$, and an initial compression frontier $\Phi_k^{\text{best}} \leftarrow \Phi_k^{(0)}$. For domains supporting out-of-sample falsification (Mode~III, Section~\ref{sec:extensions}), $\mathcal{T}_0$ also commits a $T_{\max}$-step-bounded domain indicator BLC term $\Omega_k : \{0,1\}^{a_k} \to \{0,1\}$ and an algebraic/symmetry invariant checker $V_{\text{inv}} \in \{0,1\}^*$.
  \item \textbf{Axiom Nodes ($v_u \in \mathcal{V}_A$):} Created via transition $\mathcal{T}_1$ (Recipe / Axiom commit-reveal \& batch adoption), an axiom node registers a reusable BLC program $p_u \in \{0,1\}^*$ of raw length $L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) \equiv |p_u| \ge 1$, assigned a monotonic commit sequence number $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_u) \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$, a sequential reveal-adoption registry index $\text{idx}(v_u) \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$, commit block height $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{blk}}(v_u)$, root origin pointer $\text{origin}(v_u)$ (initialized to $v_u$), syntactic reduction length $L_{\text{red}}(v_u)$ (initialized to $0$), and cumulative self-burn ledger $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u)$.
  \item \textbf{Recipe Edges ($e_{k \leftarrow i} \in \mathcal{E}$):} Submitted via transition $\mathcal{T}_1$ to compress dataset $D_k$ by citing a finite subset of prior axioms $\mathcal{C}_i \subseteq \mathcal{V}_A$. A recipe specifies a wrapper program $w_i$, step-limit codeword length $\ell_\delta \ge 1$, citation list header length $\ell_{\text{ref}} \equiv \vert{}\text{Ref}(v_i)\vert{}$ (distinct from the Elias $\omega$ coding function $\ell_\omega(\cdot)$), MST residual length $L_{\text{res}}$, accumulated puncture penalty $d_{\text{punct}}$, and cited axiom contributions consisting of unamortized length $L_{\text{unamortized}}(v_u, D_k) \equiv L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) - \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k)$ plus indicator-gated dictionary code length $\mathbf{1}_{[\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) > 0]} \cdot \ell_E(v_u)$.
\end{itemize}
Across all chapters of this specification, the protocol state transitions $\mathcal{T}_0 \sim \mathcal{T}_6$ are unified as follows:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\mathcal{T}_0$: Dataset registration;
  \item $\mathcal{T}_1$: Recipe / Axiom commit-reveal \& batch adoption;
  \item $\mathcal{T}_2$: Post-adoption boundary puncture (Mode II-A \& Mode III);
  \item $\mathcal{T}_3$: Priority claim hold window expiration \& underwriter absorption;
  \item $\mathcal{T}_4$: Tranche maturity settlement \& dataset closure;
  \item $\mathcal{T}_5$: Epoch weight decay, Shannon dictionary update, \& delayed $\phi_u^{\text{dict}}$ activation;
  \item $\mathcal{T}_6$: Syntactic reduction attribution.
\end{itemize}

\begin{definition}[Total Local Description Length and Telescoping Escrow Allocation]
\label{def:escrow_alloc}
For any recipe $e_{k \leftarrow i}$ on dataset $D_k$, its local wrapper overhead $L_{\text{local}}$ and total effective description length $\Phi_{\text{local}}$ are defined over $\mathbb{N}$ as:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:phi_local}
\begin{aligned}
L_{\text{local}}(e_{k \leftarrow i}) &\equiv |w_i| + \ell_{\text{ref}} + \ell_\delta, \\
\Phi_{\text{local}}(e_{k \leftarrow i}) &\equiv L_{\text{local}}(e_{k \leftarrow i}) + L_{\text{res}} + d_{\text{punct}} + \sum_{v_u \in \mathcal{C}_i} \Big(\big(L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) - \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k)\big) + \mathbf{1}_{[\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) > 0]} \cdot \ell_E(v_u)\Big).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Here, $L_{\text{unamortized}}(v_u, D_k) \equiv L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) - \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k)$ is the unamortized axiom length and $\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) \le L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u)$ is the burn-backed description-length exemption defined in Section~\ref{sec:burn_and_reduction} (Definition~\ref{def:burn_amort}), so that an unamortized axiom ($\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) = 0$) pays its full raw length $L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u)$ without dictionary pointer overhead while a partially or fully amortized axiom ($\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) > 0$) replaces $\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k)$ bits with its epoch dictionary pointer $\ell_E(v_u)$. In Equation~\eqref{eq:phi_local}, $\ell_\delta \equiv \ell_\omega(\delta) \ge 1$ is the Elias $\omega$ codeword length of the proposer's declared per-record step ceiling $\delta \in \{1, \dots, T_{\max}\}$, and the citation list header length $\ell_{\text{ref}}$ for $m = |\mathcal{C}_i|$ cited axioms is defined as $\ell_{\text{ref}} = 2\text{ bits}$ when $m = 0$ and $\ell_{\text{ref}} = \ell_\omega(m + 1)$ when $m \ge 1$ (e.g., $\ell_{\text{ref}} = \ell_\omega(2) = 3\text{ bits}$ for $m = 1$). Since $\ell_\delta \ge 1$ for any valid Elias codeword, $\Phi_{\text{local}}(e_{k \leftarrow i}) \ge 1$ holds unconditionally (\leanref{local\_desc\_ge\_one}). When a winning recipe reduces the dataset frontier from $\Phi_k^{\text{best}}$ to $\Phi_{\text{new}} = \Phi_{\text{local}}(e_{k \leftarrow i}) < \Phi_k^{\text{best}}$, achieving bit reduction $\Delta \equiv \Phi_k^{\text{best}} - \Phi_{\text{new}} \ge 1$, the maximum escrow slice carved from $B_k^{\text{rem}}$ is determined by the telescoping allocation function:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:escrow_alloc}
\text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta) \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{B_k^{(0)} \cdot \Delta}{\Phi_k^{(0)}} \right\rfloor \quad \big(\text{where } \Phi_k^{(0)} \equiv n_k b_k \ge 1\big).
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Monotonicity and Superadditivity of Escrow Allocation]
\label{lem:escrow_props}
\leanref{div\_le\_div\_right, add\_div\_le\_div\_add, escrowAlloc\_mono, escrowAlloc\_superadd}
For all $B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_1, \Delta_2 \in \mathbb{N}$:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item If $\Delta_1 \le \Delta_2$, then $\text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_1) \le \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_2)$.
  \item Superadditivity holds unconditionally:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:superadd}
  \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_1) + \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_2) \le \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_1 + \Delta_2).
  \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}[Proof Sketch]
By integer division properties, $\lfloor x / c \rfloor \cdot c \le x$ and $\lfloor y / c \rfloor \cdot c \le y$. Summing both yields $(\lfloor x/c \rfloor + \lfloor y/c \rfloor) \cdot c \le x + y$, which implies $\lfloor x/c \rfloor + \lfloor y/c \rfloor \le \lfloor (x+y)/c \rfloor$ (\texttt{add\_div\_le\_div\_add}). Substituting $x = B_k^{(0)} \Delta_1$, $y = B_k^{(0)} \Delta_2$, and $c = \Phi_k^{(0)}$ with distributivity $B_k^{(0)}\Delta_1 + B_k^{(0)}\Delta_2 = B_k^{(0)}(\Delta_1 + \Delta_2)$ immediately yields Equation~\eqref{eq:superadd}. Specifically, superadditivity guarantees that splitting a compression improvement $\Delta_1 + \Delta_2$ across multiple sequential recipes never extracts more from $B_k^{(0)}$ than a single combined jump, completely eliminating tranche-splitting arbitrage.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Serial Batch Structure and Epoch Synchronization}
To eliminate front-running (where a validator inspects an unconfirmed compression recipe in the mempool and submits it under their own identity) and prevent intra-batch manipulation of frontier or dictionary parameters, ESM enforces a strict two-timescale temporal hierarchy:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Serial Batch Cycle ($N_{\text{batch}} = 60\text{ blocks}$):} Time is partitioned into non-overlapping serial batches of length:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:batch_cycle}
  N_{\text{batch}} = N_{\text{com}}(20) + N_{\text{delay}}(10) + N_{\text{rev}}(30) = 60\text{ blocks}.
  \end{equation}
  During the $N_{\text{com}} = 20$ block commit phase (Phase 1), proposers submit blinded salted hashes $h_i = \text{Hash}(\text{proposer\_id} \parallel D_k \parallel \text{root}_{\text{MST}} \parallel p_{\text{rec}} \parallel \text{salt})$ along with creator self-collateral $S_c \equiv S_{k,1}^{\text{creator}}$, which strictly caps the maximum gross tranche escrow $E_{\text{tr}}$ that can be adopted for that commit in Phase 2 to $E_{\text{tr}} \le \lfloor 10000 (1 + \Lambda) S_c / \sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}} \rfloor$ (where $\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}}$ is the recipe collateral requirement ratio, baseline $10000\text{ bps} = 100\%$, and $\Lambda \in \{0, 1\}$ is the underwriter leverage ratio). During the $N_{\text{delay}} = 10$ block delay phase, no new commits for that batch are accepted and a Verifiable Delay Function (VDF) \cite{boneh2018} finalizes the unpredictable spot-check seed $\rho_{\text{batch}}$. During the $N_{\text{rev}} = 30$ block reveal phase (Phase 2), committed proposers reveal their recipes and STARK proofs for the $K_{\text{spot}} = 32$ VDF-selected indices. At batch end, only the valid revealed recipe achieving the strictly lowest $\Phi_{\text{local}} < \Phi_k^{\text{best}}$ (tie-broken among recipes achieving the same $\Phi_{\text{local}}$---such as when citing axioms of equal dictionary code length $\ell_E$---first by smaller cited-axiom commit-sequence sum $\sum_{v_u \in \mathcal{C}_i} t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_u)$, prioritizing direct citation of earlier original axioms over derivative clones, and then by recipe commit order) is adopted; non-winning valid reveals receive a full refund of $S_c$ (\leanref{branch04\_reject\_preserves}), while unrevealed commits forfeit $70\%$ of $S_c$ to permanent burn and receive a $30\%$ refund (\leanref{branch05\_unrevealed\_preserves}).
  \item \textbf{Epoch Synchronization ($N_{\text{epoch}} = 9,960\text{ blocks}$, Transition $\mathcal{T}_5$):} Epoch weight decay, canonical Shannon dictionary updates $\ell_E(v_u)$, and delayed dictionary fee rate $\phi_u^{\text{dict}}$ activations are executed via transition $\mathcal{T}_5$ only at epoch boundaries spaced every:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:epoch_sync}
  N_{\text{epoch}} = 9,960 \equiv 166 \times N_{\text{batch}}\text{ blocks}.
  \end{equation}
  Because $N_{\text{epoch}}$ is an exact integer multiple ($166\times$) of $N_{\text{batch}}$, no batch ever straddles an epoch boundary. Consequently, both the reference frontier $\Phi_k^{\text{best}}$ and the dictionary code lengths $\ell_E(v_u)$ are strictly constant throughout the entirety of every batch.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Burn-Backed Amortization \& Syntactic Reduction Attribution}
\label{sec:burn_and_reduction}

\subsection{Burn-Backed Amortization and Single-Dataset Pre-Embedding Bound}
Suppose an adversary wishes to attack dataset $D_k$ (with raw size $\Phi_k^{(0)}$ and bounty $B_k^{(0)}$) by registering a fake ``axiom'' $v_u$ whose code $p_u$ simply hardcodes a compressed lookup table for $D_k$, and then submitting a recipe $e_{k \leftarrow i}$ that cites $v_u$ using only a short dictionary pointer $\ell_E(v_u)$. If the protocol naively exempted the entire raw length $L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u)$ upon first citation, the attacker would extract nearly the entire bounty $B_k^{(0)}$ at negligible cost.

ESM neutralizes this attack without any human committee by requiring that an axiom's creditable description-length exemption $\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k)$ be backed by prior irreversible token burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u, < t_k)$ committed at least $N_{\text{lock}}$ blocks before dataset $D_k$'s creation block $t_k$.

\begin{definition}[Burn-Backed Amortization and Net Citation Savings]
\label{def:burn_amort}
Let $v_u \in \mathcal{V}_A$ be an axiom committed at block $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{blk}}(v_u) \le t_k - N_{\text{lock}}$ (where the pre-embedding lockup maturation window is $N_{\text{lock}} = 10,000\text{ blocks}$), and let $D_k$ be a dataset with initial bounty $B_k^{(0)} \ge 1$ and initial description length $\Phi_k^{(0)} \ge 1$. Given protocol settlement burn rate $\epsilon_{\text{bps}} < 10000$ (baseline $\epsilon_{\text{bps}} = 500\text{ bps}$), the amortized description length $\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k)$ and net epoch savings $\text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_u, D_k)$ are defined as:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:delta_amort}
\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) \equiv \min\left(L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u), \; \left\lfloor \frac{10000 \cdot C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u, < t_k) \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)}}{(10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}}) \cdot B_k^{(0)}} \right\rfloor\right),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:saved_net}
\text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_u, D_k) \equiv \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) \ominus \ell_E(v_u).
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Unconditional Unprofitability of Single-Dataset Pre-Embedding]
\label{lem:single_data_pre_embed}
\leanref{single\_data\_pre\_embedding\_unprofitable}
For any dataset $D_k$ with $\Phi_k^{(0)} > 0$, any axiom $v_u$, any dictionary code length $\ell_E(v_u) \in \mathbb{N}$, and any net payout rate $\text{net\_bps} = 10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}} \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\Delta_{\text{saved}} \equiv \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) \ominus \ell_E(v_u)$. The maximum net reward increment $\Delta R_{\text{net}}$ obtainable on dataset $D_k$ from the exemption $\Delta_{\text{saved}}$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:delta_r_net}
\Delta R_{\text{net}} \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{(10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}}) \cdot \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_{\text{saved}})}{10000} \right\rfloor,
\end{equation}
satisfies the unconditional algebraic upper bound:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:pre_embed_bound}
\Delta R_{\text{net}} \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u, < t_k).
\end{equation}
Note that the difference between $\Delta R_{\text{net}}$ in Equation~\eqref{eq:delta_r_net} and the ceiling $\mathcal{T}_4$ net settlement payout $E_{\text{net}} \equiv E_{\text{tr}} - \lfloor \epsilon_{\text{bps}} E_{\text{tr}} / 10000 \rfloor \le \Delta R_{\text{net}} + 1 \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u, < t_k) + 1$ (for $E_{\text{tr}} = \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_{\text{saved}})$) is at most $1\text{ token unit}$ due to integer floor rounding. Consequently, whenever mandatory Data Availability (DA) registration burn and transaction gas satisfy $\epsilon_{\text{DA}} L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) + g_{\text{tx}} \ge 2\text{ token units}$ (plus capital opportunity cost over $N_{\text{lock}} = 10,000\text{ blocks}$), the net profit of pre-embedding data for any single dataset $D_k$ is unconditionally strictly negative ($< 0$) under both floor and ceiling settlement forms.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}[Proof Sketch]
By definition of saturating subtraction and minimum, $\Delta_{\text{saved}} \le \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) \le \lfloor 10000 \cdot C_{\text{amort\_burn}} \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)} / (\text{net\_bps} \cdot B_k^{(0)}) \rfloor$. Multiplying by $\text{net\_bps} \cdot B_k^{(0)}$ and applying $\lfloor X / Y \rfloor \cdot Y \le X$ yields $\Delta_{\text{saved}} \cdot \text{net\_bps} \cdot B_k^{(0)} \le 10000 \cdot C_{\text{amort\_burn}} \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)}$. Since $\text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_{\text{saved}}) \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)} \le B_k^{(0)} \cdot \Delta_{\text{saved}}$, multiplying by $\text{net\_bps}$ and dividing by $\Phi_k^{(0)} > 0$ via \texttt{div\_le\_div\_right} gives $\text{net\_bps} \cdot \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_{\text{saved}}) \le 10000 \cdot C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$. Dividing both sides by $10000$ yields $\Delta R_{\text{net}} \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Multi-Axiom Split Pre-Embedding Bound]
\label{cor:multi_axiom_pre_embed}
Suppose an adversary partitions a lookup table for dataset $D_k$ across $m \ge 1$ distinct axioms $v_{u_1}, \dots, v_{u_m} \in \mathcal{C}_i$ with individual net exemptions $\Delta_{\text{saved},j} \equiv \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_{u_j}, D_k) \ominus \ell_E(v_{u_j})$ and prior burns $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_{u_j}, < t_k)$. Then the combined net reward increment from citing all $m$ axioms satisfies:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:multi_pre_embed}
\Delta R_{\text{net}}\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^m \Delta_{\text{saved},j}\right) \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{(10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}}) \cdot \text{EscrowAlloc}\big(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \sum_{j=1}^m \Delta_{\text{saved},j}\big)}{10000} \right\rfloor \le \sum_{j=1}^m C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_{u_j}, < t_k).
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}[Proof Sketch]
For each $j \in \{1, \dots, m\}$, $\Delta_{\text{saved},j} \cdot \text{net\_bps} \cdot B_k^{(0)} \le 10000 \cdot C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_{u_j}, < t_k) \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)}$. Summing over $j = 1, \dots, m$ yields $(\sum_{j=1}^m \Delta_{\text{saved},j}) \cdot \text{net\_bps} \cdot B_k^{(0)} \le 10000 \cdot (\sum_{j=1}^m C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_{u_j}, < t_k)) \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)}$. Applying $\text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Sigma_\Delta) \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)} \le B_k^{(0)} \Sigma_\Delta$ and dividing by $10000 \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)} > 0$ via \texttt{div\_le\_div\_right} yields Equation~\eqref{eq:multi_pre_embed}, while splitting across $m$ axioms strictly increases pointer overhead $\sum_{j=1}^m \ell_E(v_{u_j}) \ge m$ and gas fees.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Canonical Shannon Dictionary Code Length Over Natural Numbers}
To reward axioms that achieve widespread reuse across the knowledge DAG with shorter citation pointers (mirroring Shannon-Fano-Elias coding in MDL \cite{rissanen1978,li2019}) while preventing Sybil self-citation from artificially inflating an axiom's dictionary weight, ESM caps each axiom's empirical citation weight $W(v_u)$ using continuously locked dictionary stake.

\begin{definition}[Stake-Capped Canonical Shannon Dictionary Code Length]
\label{def:shannon_dict}
Let $N_{\text{epoch}} = 9,960 \equiv 166 \times N_{\text{batch}}$ ($N_{\text{batch}} = 60$). For each axiom $v_u \in \mathcal{V}_A^{(E)}$ active at the start of epoch $E$, let $\overline{S}_{u,E}^{\text{dict,c}}$ denote the minimum dictionary collateral of the axiom creator and $\overline{S}_{u,s,E}^{\text{dict,st}}$ denote the minimum dictionary stake of external guarantor $s$ that remained continuously locked without any withdrawal request throughout the entire preceding epoch $E-1$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:dict_stake_min}
\overline{S}_{u,E}^{\text{dict}} \equiv \overline{S}_{u,E}^{\text{dict,c}} + \sum_{s} \overline{S}_{u,s,E}^{\text{dict,st}}.
\end{equation}
Given the dictionary stake-to-weight cap parameter $\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{dict}} \ge 1$ (baseline $\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{dict}} = 10000\text{ bps} = 100\%$, distinct from the recipe collateral ratio $\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}}$) and Laplace smoothing floor $\epsilon_0 \ge 1$, the stake-capped weight $W_E(v_u)$ and exact integer Shannon code length $\ell_E(v_u)$ are defined as:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:weight_cap}
W_E(v_u) \equiv \min\left(W(v_u), \; \left\lfloor \frac{10000 \cdot \overline{S}_{u,E}^{\text{dict}}}{\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{dict}}} \right\rfloor\right),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:shannon_len}
\ell_E(v_u) \equiv \min\left\{k \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1} : 2^k \big(W_E(v_u) + \epsilon_0\big) \ge \sum_{v_m \in \mathcal{V}_A^{(E)}} \big(W_E(v_m) + \epsilon_0\big) + \epsilon_0\right\}.
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

Defining the total smoothed dictionary weight $\Sigma_W \equiv \sum_{v_m \in \mathcal{V}_A^{(E)}} (W_E(v_m) + \epsilon_0)$, note that Equation~\eqref{eq:shannon_len} requires zero floating-point logarithms: $k$ is simply the smallest positive integer for which $2^k (W_E(v_u) + \epsilon_0)$ meets or exceeds $\Sigma_W + \epsilon_0$. Moreover, summing $2^{-\ell_E(v_u)} \le \frac{W_E(v_u) + \epsilon_0}{\Sigma_W + \epsilon_0}$ over all $v_u \in \mathcal{V}_A^{(E)}$ yields $\sum_{v_u} 2^{-\ell_E(v_u)} \le \frac{\Sigma_W}{\Sigma_W + \epsilon_0} < 1$, strictly satisfying Kraft's inequality for prefix-free decodability.

\subsection{Syntactic Reduction Transition (\texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{T}_6$}{T6})}
While Burn-Backed Amortization prevents single-dataset pre-embedding, it introduces a subtle secondary vulnerability if deployed alone: suppose a researcher publishes a foundational axiom $v_s$ (program $p_s$) with modest initial burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_s)$, or suppose $v_s$'s burn cap is temporarily small relative to a massive new dataset $D_k$. A well-capitalized plagiarist could copy $p_s$ (or apply a trivial identity/refactoring wrapper to produce $p_i$), burn a large $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_i)$, and capture all downstream citation royalties on $D_k$, bypassing $v_s$ entirely.

To close this vector deterministically without human arbitration, ESM introduces transition $\mathcal{T}_6$ (\textbf{Syntactic Reduction Attribution}), which measures the conditional constructive description length of $p_i$ given an earlier root axiom $p_s$.

\begin{definition}[Syntactic Reduction Transition $\mathcal{T}_6$ Acceptance Conditions]
\label{def:t6_reduction}
A transaction $\mathcal{T}_6(v_i, v_s, q, \text{fuel}_{\max})$ claiming that axiom $v_i$ (with program $p_i$) is syntactically reducible to an earlier axiom $v_s$ (with program $p_s$) via transformation/adapter program $q \in \{0,1\}^*$ within a submitter-declared total verification fuel ceiling $\text{fuel}_{\max} \in \{1, \dots, T_{\max}\}$ is accepted if and only if all four conditions hold:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Strict Temporal Precedence:} $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_s) < t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_i)$.
  \item \textbf{Root Axiom Condition:} $\text{origin}(v_s) = v_s$ (i.e., $v_s$ is currently an unreduced root axiom, ensuring a flat depth-1 attribution forest).
  \item \textbf{Bounded Constructive Synthesis (B\"ohm-Tree $\beta\eta$-Equivalence):} The adapter application $q(p_s)$ denotes the function application $\text{App}(q, p_s)$ of two closed BLC terms (the root program $p_s$ is supplied to $q$ as a closed $\lambda$-term, not as a bit-string). Evaluating $\text{App}(q, p_s)$ to WHNF on the bounded universal machine and comparing the result with $p_i$ within the declared verification budget $\text{fuel}_{\max} \le T_{\max}$ must establish:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:blc_synthesis}
  U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}(q(p_s)) =_{\beta\eta} p_i.
  \end{equation}
  Because recursive axioms containing fixed-point combinators such as the Y-combinator (including the root axiom $v_s$ of Section~\ref{sec:empirical}) have no $\beta$-normal form (their literal full $\beta$-normal form unfolds infinitely), the symbol $=_{\beta\eta}$ in Equation~\eqref{eq:blc_synthesis} is not decided by comparing normal forms: it denotes \emph{coinductive B\"ohm-tree equality}, which is strictly broader than finite $\beta\eta$-conversion and certifies observational equivalence. It is decided by a deterministic procedure with an explicit stack (no recursion): at each queued pair of terms, both are reduced in normal order to head normal form (HNF), their leading $\lambda$-binder prefixes are $\eta$-aligned, head variables and argument arities are compared, the argument sub-terms are queued for comparison, and any re-encountered term pair $(a, b)$ along the traversal is coinductively accepted (greatest fixed point). To preclude denial-of-service by adversarial terms (including terms with tiny KAM WHNF step counts but heavy structural comparison trees), the verifier charges the KAM steps $\text{kam}$ required to evaluate $\text{App}(q, p_s)$ to WHNF and the bounded B\"ohm-tree comparison operations to a single joint budget $\text{fuel}_{\text{total}} = \text{kam} + \text{beta} + \text{nodes} + \text{work} \le \text{fuel}_{\max} \le T_{\max} = 2^{20}$ (where $\text{beta}$ counts $\beta$-reduction steps to HNF, $\text{nodes}$ counts sub-term pair comparisons, and $\text{work}$ counts the AST nodes traversed by substitution, $\eta$-shifting, and structural comparison), aborting as soon as $\text{fuel}_{\text{total}}$ exceeds $\text{fuel}_{\max}$. An aborted check is treated as \emph{not established} (the procedure returns \texttt{None}, never \texttt{True}) and the transaction is rejected. Hence the check always halts, is sound (a pair is accepted only if its B\"ohm trees coincide), and is necessarily incomplete (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}, Item~1).
  \item \textbf{Syntactic Reduction Length \& Pointer Overwrite Rule:} Define the candidate syntactic reduction length:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:l_red_new}
  L_{\text{red}}^{\text{new}} \equiv \vert{}p_i\vert{} \ominus \big(\vert{}q\vert{} + \ell_\omega(\text{idx}(v_s))\big).
  \end{equation}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Case A (Current origin is a valid root axiom):} If $\text{origin}(\text{origin}(v_i)) = \text{origin}(v_i)$, the transaction requires $L_{\text{red}}^{\text{new}} > L_{\text{red}}(v_i)$ (or $L_{\text{red}}^{\text{new}} = L_{\text{red}}(v_i) \ge 1$ with strictly earlier root precedence $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_s) < t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(\text{origin}(v_i))$).
    \item \textbf{Case B (Orphaned non-root origin recovery):} If the previous reduction target $\text{origin}(v_i)$ was itself subsequently reduced to another axiom so that it is no longer a root axiom ($\text{origin}(\text{origin}(v_i)) \neq \text{origin}(v_i)$), the transaction accepts a direct overwrite to the root axiom $v_s$ whenever $L_{\text{red}}^{\text{new}} \ge 1$.
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
Upon acceptance, the state machine updates $\text{origin}(v_i) \leftarrow v_s$ and $L_{\text{red}}(v_i) \leftarrow L_{\text{red}}^{\text{new}}$ in $O(1)$ storage writes, with deterministic two-transaction (commit plus reveal) gas cost $g_{\text{T6}} = 2 g_{\text{tx}} + c_{\text{step}} \cdot \text{fuel}_{\max}$ charged in proportion to the declared verification fuel ceiling $\text{fuel}_{\max}$. Analogous to $\mathcal{T}_1$, third-party $\mathcal{T}_6$ submissions are executed via a two-phase commit-reveal scheme: a watchtower $h$ commits the salted hash $\text{Hash}(h \parallel v_i \parallel v_s \parallel q \parallel \text{salt})$ at block $t$ and reveals $(q, \text{fuel}_{\max}, \text{salt})$ within the commit validity window $1 \le \Delta t \le N_{\text{rev,T6}}$ (baseline $N_{\text{rev,T6}} = 30\text{ blocks}$), where the first submitter $h$ to complete a valid reveal satisfying Case~A or Case~B claims $B_{\text{finder}}$ while unrevealed commits expire after $N_{\text{rev,T6}}$ blocks (and because $h$ is cryptographically bound inside the commit hash, no mempool observer can copy or hijack a revealed adapter).
\end{definition}

\textbf{One-Time Deposit-Backed $\mathcal{T}_6$ Finder's Bounty ($B_{\text{finder}}$) \& Sybil-Resistant Universal Debt-Following Waterfall (\leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}):} Because on-chain wallet addresses are pseudonymous in a permissionless environment, conditioning waterfall priority on address equality ($\text{owner}(v_u) = \text{owner}(v_s)$ vs.\ $\text{owner}(v_u) \neq \text{owner}(v_s)$) would be vulnerable to trivial two-wallet Sybil splitting, while paying a perpetual royalty share to the $\mathcal{T}_6$ finder would both delay watchtower incentives until future citations occur and allow a clone creator to self-submit $\mathcal{T}_6$ as their own finder. Instead, ESM achieves address-independent protection via two complementary rules:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{One-Time Deposit-Backed $\mathcal{T}_6$ Finder's Bounty ($B_{\text{finder}}$):} Upon reveal adoption ($\mathcal{T}_1$, Branch~3), each registered axiom $v_i$ locks a syntactic-audit deposit $S_{\text{audit}}$ (baseline $S_{\text{audit}} = 20\text{ tokens}$, included in the Branch~3 locked stake $S^{\text{st}}$ and refunded in full to $\text{owner}(v_i)$ if $v_i$ remains unreduced at dataset closure or maturity). When a watchtower $h$ submits the first valid $\mathcal{T}_6(v_i, v_s, q, \text{fuel}_{\max})$ transaction reducing $v_i$ to root $v_s$, $h$ does not receive a perpetual cut of future royalties; instead, $S_{\text{audit}}$ is immediately slashed using the exact same integer split structure as Branches~6/7 and~14, paying $h$ a one-time fixed cap bounty $B_{\text{finder}} \equiv \lfloor (10000 - \kappa_{\text{bps}}) S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens}$ (under the parameter regime $c_{\text{search}} + g_{\text{T6}} \le 5$ over $\mathbb{N}$, so that $B_{\text{finder}} = 6\text{ tokens} > c_{\text{search}} + g_{\text{T6}}$) and permanently burning the remaining $S_{\text{audit}} - B_{\text{finder}} = 14\text{ tokens}$ ($70\%$). This design yields two immediate game-theoretic guarantees: (a) \emph{Subgame-Perfect Pre-Lockup Deterrence:} because $B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens} > c_{\text{search}} + g_{\text{T6}}$ is paid immediately upon $\mathcal{T}_6$ acceptance regardless of whether $v_i$ is ever cited, third-party watchtowers have a strict best response to reduce any plagiarized clone $v_i$ during the $N_{\text{lock}} = 10,000\text{ block}$ inspection window before $v_i$ can open RBF sponsorship; and (b) \emph{Elimination of Self-Finder Arbitrage:} if the clone creator self-submits $\mathcal{T}_6$ as finder, they merely recover $B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens}$ of their own locked $S_{\text{audit}}$ while permanently burning $S_{\text{audit}} - B_{\text{finder}} = 14\text{ tokens}$ (net loss $-(S_{\text{audit}} - B_{\text{finder}}) - g_{\text{T6}} = -14 - g_{\text{T6}} < 0$) and capture zero tokens from the redirected royalty stream.
  \item \textbf{Sybil-Resistant Two-Regime Rule (Pre-Lockup RBF Eligibility Gate \& Universal $2.0\times$ Debt-Following Waterfall, \leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}):}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{Regime 1 (Pre-Lockup RBF Eligibility Gate):} An axiom $v_u$ is permitted to open external RBF sponsorship ($B_{\text{target}}(v_u) > 0$) only after surviving the $N_{\text{lock}} = 10,000\text{ block}$ post-reveal inspection window without any $\mathcal{T}_6$ reduction ($\text{origin}(v_u) = v_u$). Any plagiarized clone reduced during $N_{\text{lock}}$ by a watchtower (incentivized by the immediate bounty $B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens}$) has $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u) = 0$, so $100\%$ of redirected post-anchor royalties on $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ flow directly to root $v_s$'s waterfall.
    \item \emph{Regime 2 (Identity-Free Post-Lockup Debt-Following Waterfall):} If $v_u$ survives $N_{\text{lock}}$, raises RBF burn $b_{u,s}$ ($D_{\text{tot}}(v_u) > 0$ under the $M_{\text{cap,bps}} = 20000$, i.e., $2.0\times$ repayment cap), and is subsequently reduced via $\mathcal{T}_6$ to an earlier root $v_s$ ($t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_s) < t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_u)$), the protocol applies the \textbf{Universal Debt-Following Waterfall} (\leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}) on $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ \emph{unconditionally and without referencing address identity}: $R^{\text{net}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}})$ repays $v_u$'s remaining approved RBF debt $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u)$ in full first ($\text{paid}_{u,p}$), then $v_s$'s RBF debt $D_{\text{tot}}(v_s)$ ($\text{paid}_{s,q}$), and distributes the residual $R^{\text{rem}}$ to dictionary guarantors (prioritizing any locked dictionary guarantors $\sum_s \overline{S}_{u,s,E}^{\text{dict,st}} > 0$ of $v_u$ during the $2\text{--}3$ epoch withdrawal grace window $t_{\text{eff}}(t_{\text{req}})$) and $\text{owner}(v_s)$. Once $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u)$ is retired, all subsequent perpetuity royalties on the redirected slot $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ accrue exclusively to $v_s$'s waterfall ($\text{owner}(v_s)$ and $v_s$'s guarantors), while only the residual on the non-reduced retained slot $x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ (corresponding to genuine non-reduced wrapper/extension code) remains with $\text{owner}(v_u)$.
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\begin{proposition}[Obfuscation-Compression Duality and Plagiarist Self-Citation Penalty]
\label{prop:obfuscation_duality}
Under Condition~(3) of Definition~\ref{def:t6_reduction} (Equation~\eqref{eq:blc_synthesis}), bounded $\beta\eta$-normalization within $T_{\max}$ KAM steps strips trivial $\beta/\eta$ redex padding, dead-code wrappers, and mechanical continuation-passing style (CPS) transforms via an $O(1)$-bit normalizer adapter $q$. Suppose an adversary instead performs non-trivial obfuscation or clean-room re-implementation that cannot be bridged by $T_{\max}$-step $\beta\eta$-normalization and a short adapter $q$, producing a program $p_i$ of inflated bit-length $|p_i| = |p_s| + K$ ($K \ge 1$):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Obfuscation-Compression Trade-Off:} If $v_i$ is cited without amortization ($\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_i, D_k) = 0$), Equation~\eqref{eq:phi_local} charges the full inflated length $|p_i| = |p_s| + K$, penalizing the recipe's description length $\Phi_{\text{local}}$ by $+K$ bits and causing it to lose batch adoption to any honest recipe citing $v_s$. Conversely, amortizing the inflated $K$ bits forces the plagiarist to pay an additional irreversible burn $\Delta C_{\text{amort\_burn}} = \lceil (10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}}) B_k^{(0)} K / (10000 \Phi_k^{(0)}) \rceil$ plus DA burn $\epsilon_{\text{DA}} K$.
  \item \textbf{Plagiarist Self-Citation Penalty:} Suppose a plagiarist registers and burns $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_i)$ for a clone $v_i$ and submits winning recipes citing $v_i$ themselves. Once $\mathcal{T}_6$ reduces $v_i \to v_s$ with redirected slot fraction $\rho \equiv x_{i \to s}^{\text{tr}} / a_{i \to i}^{\text{tr}} \in (0, 1]$ and tranche citation share $f_{\text{cite}} \equiv a_{i \to i}^{\text{tr}} / \Delta_{\text{tr}} \in (0, 1]$, a fraction $(\gamma_{\text{bps}}/10000) \rho f_{\text{cite}}$ of net tranche escrow is redirected to $v_s$'s waterfall. Compared to the hypothetical baseline in which $v_i$ remained an unreduced independent axiom retaining $100\%$ of its self-citation payouts, the plagiarist requires a dataset recovery multiplier of $\frac{1}{1 - (\gamma_{\text{bps}}/10000) \rho f_{\text{cite}}} \in (1, \; 1.25]$ under $\gamma_{\text{bps}} = 2000\text{ bps}$ (reaching the maximum $1 / (1 - 0.20) = 1.25\times$ under full reduction $\rho = f_{\text{cite}} = 1$, and $1 / (1 - 0.20 \times 0.90) \approx 1.22\times$ when $\rho = 0.90, f_{\text{cite}} = 1$ as in Strategy~5 of Section~\ref{sec:agent_sim}) to recover their sunk burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_i)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Identity-Free Financing--Attribution Trilemma \& Pre-Lockup Equilibrium Resolution]
\label{prop:trilemma}
\textbf{[Class III Structural Trilemma \& Subgame-Perfect Pre-Lockup Resolution]} In a permissionless environment where agents can freely create Sybil wallet addresses, suppose a derivative axiom $v_u$ survives $N_{\text{lock}}$ unreduced, incurs RBF repayment debt $D_0 = M_{\text{cap}} b$ with multiplier $M_{\text{cap}} > 1$ (baseline $M_{\text{cap}} = 2.0$) from sponsor burn $b > 0$, and is reduced via $\mathcal{T}_6$ to an earlier root axiom $v_s$ in the post-$N_{\text{lock}}$ subgame in the high-reduction regime ($\rho \to 1$, where the retained slot $x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ alone is insufficient to service $D_0 = M_{\text{cap}} b$). Then no ex-post royalty allocation rule on the redirected slot $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ can simultaneously satisfy all three of the following properties:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{(P1) Identity-Freeness:} The allocation rule depends only on on-chain state and never inspects wallet address equality among $\text{owner}(v_u)$, $\text{owner}(v_s)$, or RBF sponsors.
  \item \textbf{(P2) Sybil-Proof Sponsor Covenant:} Bona fide external RBF sponsors of $v_u$ cannot have their contracted repayment cap $D_0 = M_{\text{cap}} b$ reduced or diluted if $\text{owner}(v_u)$ executes a single-address or Sybil-split $\mathcal{T}_6$ reduction to a controlled root $v_s$.
  \item \textbf{(P3) Post-Lockup Self-Sponsor Premium Elimination:} A plagiarist who uses a second Sybil wallet to self-sponsor a clone $v_u$ of an honest author's root $v_s$ cannot extract more than principal $1.0b$ from the redirected slot $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ if $\mathcal{T}_6$ occurs after RBF opening.
\end{enumerate}
\emph{Proof \& Two-Stage Equilibrium Resolution:} Under (P1), a post-lockup state with an honest axiom owner $\text{owner}(v_u)$ funded by an independent RBF sponsor $s$ who later self-reduces $v_u \to v_s$ is state-indistinguishable from a plagiarist $\text{owner}(v_u)$ who self-sponsors $v_u$ via a Sybil wallet $s'$ and is later reduced to an honest root $v_s$. Satisfying (P2) requires paying $v_u$'s sponsors up to $M_{\text{cap}} b$ from $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$, whereas satisfying (P3) requires capping payouts to $v_u$'s sponsors at $\le b$, which is impossible whenever $M_{\text{cap}} b > b$ ($M_{\text{cap}} > 1$). ESM resolves this trilemma by separating prevention and debt protection across two temporal stages: (i) on the equilibrium path, because the one-time deposit-backed bounty $B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens} > c_{\text{search}} + g_{\text{T6}}$ makes watchtower reduction strictly dominant during Regime~1 ($N_{\text{lock}} = 10,000\text{ blocks}$ prior to RBF eligibility), any clone $v_u$ of an existing public axiom $v_s$ is reduced before RBF sponsorship can open, enforcing $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u) = 0$ in subgame-perfect equilibrium; and (ii) off the equilibrium path (Regime~2, after $N_{\text{lock}}$), the Universal Debt-Following Waterfall strictly enforces (P1) and (P2) (\leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}), guaranteeing that bona fide RBF sponsors can never be defaulted on via self-reduction. \qed
\end{proposition}

Why does Condition (4) include Case B? Suppose $v_3$ was initially reduced to $v_2$ ($\text{origin}(v_3) = v_2$), and later someone reduces $v_2$ to an even earlier root axiom $v_1$ ($\text{origin}(v_2) = v_1 \neq v_2$). To guarantee $O(1)$ gas complexity per transaction and avoid unbounded recursive pointer-chasing loops on-chain, $\mathcal{T}_6$ does not recursively traverse all descendants of $v_2$ when $v_2$ is reduced. Instead, Stage 2 attribution only redirects royalties to $\text{origin}(v_u)$ if $\text{origin}(\text{origin}(v_u)) = \text{origin}(v_u)$, and Case B allows any watchtower or $v_1$'s owner to re-point $v_3$ directly to root $v_1$ whenever $L_{\text{red}}^{\text{new}} \ge 1$, even if the two-hop composition adapter $q_{1 \to 3}$ is slightly longer than the single-hop adapter $q_{2 \to 3}$.

\subsection{Two-Stage Upfront Attribution at Tranche Creation \& \texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{T}_4$}{T4} Royalty Waterfall}
A critical subtle attack against citation attribution is Sybil derivative cloning: an attacker registers $m$ distinct clones $v_{u_1}, \dots, v_{u_m}$ of a root axiom $v_s$, cites all $m$ clones in a recipe on $D_k$, and attempts either to over-allocate royalties beyond $v_s$'s legitimate cap $\text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_s, D_k)$ or to lock out honest citations. ESM prevents this via a \textbf{Two-Stage Upfront Reservation Waterfall} executed atomically when a winning tranche $\text{tr}$ with bit reduction $\Delta_{\text{tr}} \ge 1$ is created.

\begin{definition}[Two-Stage Upfront Tranche Attribution]
\label{def:two_stage_attrib}
Let recipe $e_{k \leftarrow i}$ cite axiom set $\mathcal{C}_i$ to create a tranche with reduction $\Delta_{\text{tr}}$ on dataset $D_k$ in epoch $E$:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Stage 1: Direct Marginal Reservation (\leanref{stage1\_single\_bounds, stage1\_list\_sum\_le\_delta}).} For every cited axiom $v_u \in \mathcal{C}_i$, compute its unreserved marginal savings on $D_k$:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:stage1_marg}
  \text{Saved}_{i \to u}^{\text{marg}}(D_k) \equiv \text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_u, D_k) \ominus S_{\text{paid}}(v_u, D_k), \qquad
  \Sigma_{\text{marg}} \equiv \sum_{v_u \in \mathcal{C}_i} \text{Saved}_{i \to u}^{\text{marg}}(D_k).
  \end{equation}
  If $\Sigma_{\text{marg}} = 0$, set $a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}} \equiv 0$; otherwise compute the direct tranche reservation:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:stage1_alloc}
  a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}} \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Saved}_{i \to u}^{\text{marg}}(D_k) \cdot \min(\Sigma_{\text{marg}}, \Delta_{\text{tr}})}{\Sigma_{\text{marg}}} \right\rfloor,
  \end{equation}
  and immediately reserve the full direct allocation on $v_u$'s per-dataset counter regardless of whether $v_u$ is an independent root or a derivative axiom:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:stage1_update}
  S_{\text{paid}}(v_u, D_k) \mathrel{+}= a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}}.
  \end{equation}
  \item \textbf{Stage 2: Shared Sequential Residual Cap Redirect (\leanref{capStep\_conserves, capFold\_conserves, capFold\_general\_bound}).} Partition $\mathcal{C}_i$ into independent axioms (where $\text{origin}(v_u) = v_u$ or $\text{origin}(\text{origin}(v_u)) \neq \text{origin}(v_u)$, which retain $x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}} \equiv a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ and $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}} \equiv 0$) and active derivative groups pointing to a valid root axiom $v_s = \text{origin}(v_u) \neq v_u$ with $\text{origin}(v_s) = v_s$. For each such root axiom $v_s$ (after any direct Stage 1 reservation if $v_s \in \mathcal{C}_i$), initialize its shared residual cap:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:stage2_init_cap}
  R_{\text{cap}}(v_s) \leftarrow \text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_s, D_k) \ominus S_{\text{paid}}(v_s, D_k).
  \end{equation}
  Sorting the cited derivatives $\{v_{u_1}, \dots, v_{u_m}\}$ of $v_s$ in ascending order of $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_{u_j})$, iterate sequentially for $j = 1, \dots, m$:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:stage2_redirect}
  x_{u_j \to s}^{\text{tr}} \equiv \min\Big(a_{i \to u_j}^{\text{tr}}, \; \min\big(L_{\text{red}}(v_{u_j}), \; R_{\text{cap}}(v_s)\big)\Big), \qquad
  x_{u_j \to u_j}^{\text{tr}} \equiv a_{i \to u_j}^{\text{tr}} - x_{u_j \to s}^{\text{tr}},
  \end{equation}
  updating the residual cap and root reservation counter at each step:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:stage2_state_update}
  R_{\text{cap}}(v_s) \mathrel{-}= x_{u_j \to s}^{\text{tr}}, \qquad
  S_{\text{paid}}(v_s, D_k) \mathrel{+}= x_{u_j \to s}^{\text{tr}}.
  \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

When a tranche of escrow $E_{\text{tr}}$ and bit reduction $\Delta_{\text{tr}} > 0$ matures past the challenge window and executes settlement transition $\mathcal{T}_4$, protocol burn $E_{\text{burn}} \equiv \lfloor \epsilon_{\text{bps}} E_{\text{tr}} / 10000 \rfloor$ is deducted first, leaving net distributable escrow $E_{\text{net}} \equiv E_{\text{tr}} - E_{\text{burn}}$. Each attribution slot $x \in \{x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}, x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}\}$ with beneficiary axiom $v_m$ receives gross slot royalty:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:slot_royalty}
R(x) \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{\gamma_{\text{bps}} E_{\text{net}} x}{10000 \cdot \Delta_{\text{tr}}} \right\rfloor,
\end{equation}
where $\gamma_{\text{bps}} \le 10000$ (baseline $\gamma_{\text{bps}} = 2000\text{ bps} = 20\%$) is the ancestor citation royalty rate. Inside each slot $x$, $R(x)$ is distributed in four sequential priority steps (\leanref{slot\_four\_way\_split\_conserves}):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Data Anchor Rebate:} $R^{\text{anchor}}(x) \equiv \lfloor \beta_{D,\text{bps}} R(x) / 10000 \rfloor$ (baseline $\beta_{D,\text{bps}} = 1000\text{ bps} = 10\%$) is rebated to the anchor of $D_k$, leaving $R^{\text{net}}(x) \equiv R(x) - R^{\text{anchor}}(x)$.
  \item \textbf{RBF Burn-Sponsor Priority Repayment (Universal Debt-Following Waterfall, \leanref{rbf\_lifetime\_cap, rbf\_prorata\_le\_list, m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}):} For a direct (non-redirected) slot $x = x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ on beneficiary $v_m = v_u$, the protocol first deducts the aggregate RBF repayment $R_{\text{repay}}(x) \equiv \min(R^{\text{net}}(x), D_{\text{tot}}(v_m))$ into $v_m$'s RBF repayment pool, fixing the post-RBF residual at $R^{\text{rem}}(x) \equiv R^{\text{net}}(x) - R_{\text{repay}}(x)$ (matching \texttt{rbf\_lifetime\_cap} and \texttt{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves} with zero rounding leakage to the axiom owner). Inside the pool, each approved burn sponsor $s$ with remaining unpaid cap $D_{m,s}$ (where $D_{\text{tot}}(v_m) = \sum_s D_{m,s}$ and initial debt cap is $M_{\text{cap,bps}} = 20000$, i.e., $2.0\times$ sponsor burn $b_{m,s}$ within $B_{\text{target}}(v_m)$) receives the base pro-rata share:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:rbf_paid}
  \text{paid}_s(x) \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{R_{\text{repay}}(x) \cdot D_{m,s}}{D_{\text{tot}}(v_m)} \right\rfloor,
  \end{equation}
  and any integer floor remainder $R_{\text{repay}}(x) - \sum_s \text{paid}_s(x)$ (at most the number of active sponsors) is allocated in $1\text{-token}$ units in commit-priority order (FIFO / Largest-Remainder) to sponsors with remaining debt $D_{m,s} > 0$, exhausting $R_{\text{repay}}(x)$ in full (to bound on-chain iteration gas to $O(1)$, each axiom is capped at $K_{\text{rbf}}^{\max} = 10$ sponsor slots with minimum contribution $b_{u,s} \ge \lceil B_{\text{target}}(v_u) / 10 \rceil$). For a redirected slot $x = x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ transferred from derivative axiom $v_u$ to root axiom $v_s$, under the Sybil-resistant two-regime rule of Section~4.3, the \textbf{Universal Debt-Following Waterfall} applies this exact aggregate deduction in two address-independent priority tiers (\leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}): first, $R^{\text{net}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}})$ deducts and repays $v_u$'s remaining approved RBF debt $\text{paid}_u = \min(R^{\text{net}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}), D_{\text{tot}}(v_u))$ in full (which equals $0$ whenever $v_u$ was reduced during $N_{\text{lock}}$ before RBF eligibility), leaving $R^{\text{mid}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}) \equiv R^{\text{net}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}) - \text{paid}_u$; second, $R^{\text{mid}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}})$ deducts and repays $v_s$'s RBF debt $\text{paid}_s = \min(R^{\text{mid}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}), D_{\text{tot}}(v_s))$, leaving $R^{\text{rem}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}) \equiv R^{\text{mid}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}) - \text{paid}_s$.
  \item \textbf{Dictionary Guarantor Dividend:} External stakers who locked $\overline{S}_{m,s,E}^{\text{dict,st}}$ continuously across epoch $E-1$ receive:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:dict_div}
  \text{div}_s(x) \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{\phi_{m,\text{bps}}^{\text{dict}} \cdot \overline{S}_{m,s,E}^{\text{dict,st}} \cdot R^{\text{rem}}(x)}{10000 \cdot \overline{S}_{m,E}^{\text{dict}}} \right\rfloor.
  \end{equation}
  By the total integer division convention ($\lfloor X / 0 \rfloor \equiv 0$) established in Section~3.1, $\text{paid}_s(x) = 0$ whenever $D_{\text{tot}}(v_m) = 0$ in Equation~\eqref{eq:rbf_paid}, and $\text{div}_s(x) = 0$ whenever $\overline{S}_{m,E}^{\text{dict}} = 0$ (as well as in the initial epoch $E = 0$) in Equation~\eqref{eq:dict_div}. Under any post-lockup $\mathcal{T}_6$ reduction where external dictionary guarantor stake $\sum_s \overline{S}_{u,s,E}^{\text{dict,st}} > 0$ was locked on $v_u$, Step~3 dividends on $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ continue to prioritize $v_u$'s dictionary guarantors until the withdrawal grace window $t_{\text{eff}}(t_{\text{req}})$ ($2\text{--}3$ epochs, Extension~3) has elapsed.
  \item \textbf{Axiom Owner Residual:} The post-dividend residual is $R^{\text{owner}}(x) \equiv R^{\text{rem}}(x) - \sum_s \text{div}_s(x)$, which accrues to $\text{owner}(v_u)$ on a retained slot $x = x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ and in full to $\text{owner}(v_s)$ on a redirected slot $x = x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ (since the $\mathcal{T}_6$ finder $h$ is compensated via the one-time deposit-backed bounty $B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens}$ upon $\mathcal{T}_6$ execution rather than diluting ongoing royalties), conserving $R(x)$ identically.
\end{enumerate}
Summing across all slots $R_{\text{anc}} \equiv \sum_x R(x)$, the remaining tranche escrow $R_{\text{rem}} \equiv E_{\text{net}} - R_{\text{anc}}$ is split between recipe underwriters/guarantors $R_{\text{guar}} \equiv \lfloor \phi_{i,\text{bps}}^{\text{rec,eff}} R_{\text{rem}} / 10000 \rfloor$ and the recipe proposer $R_{\text{proposer}} \equiv R_{\text{rem}} - R_{\text{guar}}$, where the guarantor share rate $\phi_{i,\text{bps}}^{\text{rec,eff}}$ (unified with Sections~6.2 and~6.3; baseline $\phi_{i,\text{bps}}^{\text{rec}} = 1000\text{ bps} = 10\%$) equals $\phi_{i,\text{bps}}^{\text{rec}}$ on an initial tranche (distributed pro-rata to the guarantor stake ratio $S_{k,1}^{\text{uw}} / S_{k,1}^{\text{tot}}$) and $\max(\phi_{i,\text{bps}}^{\text{rec}}, \phi_{\text{floor,bps}})$ on an absorbed tranche.

\takeaway{Key Structural Invariant of $\mathcal{T}_4$ Settlement (\texttt{settle\_general\_list\_conserves} \& \texttt{slot\_four\_way\_split\_conserves})}{
Every single integer division rounding dust in the slot waterfall remains inside $R^{\text{owner}}(x)$, and every integer rounding dust across ancestor slots remains inside $R_{\text{proposer}}$. Thus, for any arbitrary list of attribution slots $\text{xs}$ satisfying $\sum_{x \in \text{xs}} x \le \Delta_{\text{tr}}$, exact token conservation holds identically without a single unit of deficit or excess: $R^{\text{anchor}}(x) + \sum_s \text{paid}_s(x) + \sum_s \text{div}_s(x) + R^{\text{owner}}(x) \equiv R(x)$ and $E_{\text{burn}} + R_{\text{anc}} + R_{\text{guar}} + R_{\text{proposer}} \equiv E_{\text{tr}}$.
}

\subsection{Theorem 1: Pool Conservation, Frontier Invariant, and Amortization Bounds}

\begin{theorem}[Pool Conservation, Frontier Invariant, and Amortization Bounds]
\label{thm:main_theorem_1}
\textbf{[Class I Mechanized Invariants (Parts 1, 2, 4) \& Class III Analytical Threshold (Part 3)]} Under the exact integer state machine of ESM, the following four properties hold:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Universal Pool Lower Bound} \leanref{pool\_lower\_bound\_ceil, DataInv.pool\_lower, stepAdopt\_preserves\_inv}: Whenever the dataset state satisfies the frontier partition invariant
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:front_inv}
  \Phi_k^{\text{best}} + (d_{\text{init}} + d_{\text{abs}}) + d_{\text{hold}} + d_{\text{settled}} = \Phi_k^{(0)}
  \end{equation}
  and each escrow bucket is bounded by $\text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, d_{\bullet})$ with total pool conservation $B_k^{\text{rem}} + e_{\text{hold}} + (e_{\text{init}} + e_{\text{abs}}) + e_{\text{settled}} = B_k^{(0)}$, the remaining unallocated bounty pool $B_k^{\text{rem}}$ always satisfies:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:pool_lower_ceil}
  B_k^{\text{rem}} \ge \left\lceil \frac{B_k^{(0)} \cdot \Phi_k^{\text{best}}}{\Phi_k^{(0)}} \right\rceil \equiv \text{ceilDiv}\big(B_k^{(0)} \cdot \Phi_k^{\text{best}}, \; \Phi_k^{(0)}\big).
  \end{equation}
  \item \textbf{Single-Dataset Pre-Embedding Unprofitability} \leanref{single\_data\_pre\_embedding\_unprofitable}: For any axiom $v_u$ and dataset $D_k$, the maximum net reward increment $\Delta R_{\text{net}}$ generated by $v_u$'s amortized description-length exemption $\Delta_{\text{saved}} = \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) \ominus \ell_E(v_u)$ satisfies $\Delta R_{\text{net}} \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u, < t_k)$.
  \item \textbf{Multi-Dataset Citation Recovery Threshold (\textbf{[Class III Analytical Threshold]}):} Under baseline parameters ($\epsilon_{\text{bps}} = 500$, $\gamma_{\text{bps}} = 2000$, $\beta_{D,\text{bps}} = 1000$), an honest foundational axiom $v_u$ that achieves full amortization utilization across $N_{\text{cite}}$ datasets recovers its upfront burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u)$ from net citation royalties alone when:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:n_cite_threshold}
  N_{\text{cite}} \ge \left\lceil \frac{10000^2}{\gamma_{\text{bps}} (10000 - \beta_{D,\text{bps}})} \right\rceil = \left\lceil \frac{10^8}{2000 \times 9000} \right\rceil = \lceil 5.555\dots \rceil = 6.
  \end{equation}
  \item \textbf{Syntactic Reduction Waterfall Conservation and Cumulative Cap Bound} \leanref{syntactic\_reduction\_waterfall\_valid, capFold\_general\_bound, settle\_general\_list\_conserves}: For any derivative axiom $v_u$ reduced to root $v_s$, the Stage 2 split $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}} + x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}} = a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ never increases total royalty payout ($R(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}) + R(x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}) \le R(a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}})$), and across any number of cited derivatives $\{v_{u_1}, \dots, v_{u_m}\}$, the cumulative reserved savings on root $v_s$ never exceeds its amortized cap:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:cap_fold_bound}
  S_{\text{paid}}^{\text{after}}(v_s, D_k) \le \max\big(S_{\text{paid}}^{\text{before}}(v_s, D_k), \; \text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_s, D_k)\big) \le \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_s, D_k).
  \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof Sketch]
For Part (1), let $d_{\text{used}} \equiv (d_{\text{init}} + d_{\text{abs}}) + d_{\text{hold}} + d_{\text{settled}}$ and $e_{\text{used}} \equiv (e_{\text{init}} + e_{\text{abs}}) + e_{\text{hold}} + e_{\text{settled}}$. Repeated application of \texttt{escrowAlloc\_superadd} proves $e_{\text{used}} \le \lfloor B_k^{(0)} d_{\text{used}} / \Phi_k^{(0)} \rfloor$, so $e_{\text{used}} \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)} \le B_k^{(0)} \cdot d_{\text{used}}$. Since $B_k^{\text{rem}} \Phi_k^{(0)} + e_{\text{used}} \Phi_k^{(0)} = B_k^{(0)} \Phi_k^{(0)} = B_k^{(0)} \Phi_k^{\text{best}} + B_k^{(0)} d_{\text{used}}$, subtracting the inequality gives $B_k^{(0)} \Phi_k^{\text{best}} \le B_k^{\text{rem}} \Phi_k^{(0)}$. Adding $\Phi_k^{(0)} - 1$ and dividing by $\Phi_k^{(0)} > 0$ yields $\text{ceilDiv}(B_k^{(0)} \Phi_k^{\text{best}}, \Phi_k^{(0)}) \le B_k^{\text{rem}}$ (\texttt{pool\_lower\_bound\_ceil}). Moreover, because $\Phi_k^{\text{best}} \ge 1$ (\texttt{local\_desc\_ge\_one}), $B_k^{\text{rem}} \ge 1$ whenever $B_k^{(0)} \ge 1$, ensuring the bounty pool can never be prematurely exhausted to zero while any compression potential remains. Part (2) follows from Lemma~\ref{lem:single_data_pre_embed}. For Part (3), each full-cap citation on a dataset $D_k$ where $v_u$ is cited by a third-party proposer yields net royalty $(1 - \beta_{D,\text{bps}}/10000)(\gamma_{\text{bps}}/10000)(1 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}}/10000) \frac{B_k^{(0)} \Delta_{\text{amort}}}{\Phi_k^{(0)}} \approx 0.90 \times 0.20 \times C_{\text{amort\_burn}} = 0.18 C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$, requiring $\lceil 1 / 0.18 \rceil = 6$ datasets to exceed $1.0 C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$. Part (4) follows by structural induction on the derivative list $l$ using \texttt{capStep\_conserves} ($(R - x) + (\text{acc} + x) = R + \text{acc}$) and subadditivity of floor division $R(x_s) + R(x_u) \le R(x_s + x_u)$ (\texttt{syntactic\_reduction\_waterfall\_valid}).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Theorem 2: Wash-Trading Deficit \& Incentive Compatibility under Explicit Assumptions}

\begin{theorem}[Wash-Trading Deficit \& Incentive Compatibility]
\label{thm:main_theorem_2}
\textbf{[Class I \& Class III]} Consider a coalition controlling a data anchor, axiom authors, and recipe proposers that registers a synthetic dataset $D_k$ with bounty $B_k^{(0)}$ and compresses it using pre-known private axioms to recapture its own bounty:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Deterministic Wash-Trading Deficit (\textbf{[Class I]}):} For every settled tranche $E_{\text{tr}} \le B_k^{(0)}$, the coalition's gross receipts across proposer payout, guarantor payout, ancestor royalties, RBF repayments, dictionary dividends, and data anchor rebates are bounded above by $\le E_{\text{tr}} - E_{\text{burn}}$ (strictly less if external RBF sponsors or external guarantors capture a share of the waterfall, and equal to $E_{\text{tr}} - E_{\text{burn}}$ in the worst case where the coalition monopolizes every role) \leanref{settle\_general\_list\_conserves, slot\_four\_way\_split\_conserves}. Under the standard positive registration/transaction cost condition $\epsilon_{\text{DA}} \Phi_k^{(0)} + g_{\text{tx}} > 0$ (or whenever $E_{\text{burn}} = \lfloor \epsilon_{\text{bps}} E_{\text{tr}} / 10000 \rfloor > 0$), the coalition's direct token profit is strictly negative even when no axiom burn is used ($C_{\text{amort\_burn}} = 0$):
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:wash_trade_deficit}
  \begin{aligned}
  \Pi_{\text{wash}} &\le (E_{\text{tr}} - E_{\text{burn}}) - E_{\text{tr}} - \epsilon_{\text{DA}} \Phi_k^{(0)} - C_{\text{amort\_burn}} - g_{\text{tx}} \\
  &= -\left(\left\lfloor \frac{\epsilon_{\text{bps}} E_{\text{tr}}}{10000} \right\rfloor + \epsilon_{\text{DA}} \Phi_k^{(0)} + C_{\text{amort\_burn}} + g_{\text{tx}}\right) < 0.
  \end{aligned}
  \end{equation}
  Moreover, because effective dictionary weight $W_E(v_u)$ is upper-bounded by continuously locked dictionary stake $\overline{S}_{u,E}^{\text{dict}}$ in Equation~\eqref{eq:weight_cap}, synthetic wash-trading citations cannot shorten an axiom's dictionary code length $\ell_E(v_u)$ by even a single bit without locking commensurate real stake $\overline{S}_{u,E}^{\text{dict}}$.
  \item \textbf{Individual Rationality (IR) under Explicit Class III Assumptions:}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Data Anchor IR:} A data anchor registering $D_k$ with true external utility $V_{\text{ext}}(\Delta_k) \ge B_k^{(0)}$ and receiving anchor rebates $\sum_x R^{\text{anchor}}(x) = \sum_x \lfloor \beta_{D,\text{bps}} R(x) / 10000 \rfloor$ achieves positive net utility whenever $V_{\text{ext}}(\Delta_k) + \sum_x R^{\text{anchor}}(x) > B_k^{(0)} + \epsilon_{\text{DA}} \Phi_k^{(0)} + g_{\text{tx}}$.
    \item \textbf{Foundational Axiom Author IR:} An author of a general axiom $p_u$ cited to capacity across $N_{\text{cite}} \ge 6$ datasets achieves positive expected profit when $(0.18 N_{\text{cite}} - 1) C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u) > \epsilon_{\text{DA}} L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) + g_{\text{tx}} + \text{Cost}_{\text{lock}}(N_{\text{lock}})$.
    \item \textbf{Recipe Proposer IR:} An honest recipe proposer achieving compression $\Delta_{\text{tr}}$ without ancestor citations receives $(1 - \phi_i^{\text{rec}}) (1 - \epsilon) \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_{\text{tr}})$ (where $\phi_i^{\text{rec}} \equiv \phi_{i,\text{bps}}^{\text{rec}} / 10000$), which strictly exceeds proving cost $C_{\text{STARK}}$ and gas $g_{\text{tx}}$---even after accounting for the at most $1\text{-token}$ floor rounding in $\text{EscrowAlloc}$---whenever $\Delta_{\text{tr}} \ge \left\lceil \frac{\Phi_k^{(0)} (C_{\text{STARK}} + g_{\text{tx}} + 1)}{(1 - \phi_i^{\text{rec}})(1 - \epsilon) B_k^{(0)}} \right\rceil$.
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{example}[Analytical Numerical Example: Pre-Embedding vs.\ Multi-Dataset Reuse]
\label{ex:numerical_amort}
Consider baseline parameters $\epsilon_{\text{bps}} = 500$ ($5\%$), $\gamma_{\text{bps}} = 2000$ ($20\%$), $\beta_{D,\text{bps}} = 1000$ ($10\%$), and a dataset $D_k$ with $\Phi_k^{(0)} = 10^6\text{ bits}$ and $B_k^{(0)} = 10,000\text{ tokens}$.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Case 1 (Single-Dataset Pre-Embedding Attack):} An attacker embeds $L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) = 10^5\text{ bits}$ of $D_k$-specific lookup data and burns $C_{\text{amort\_burn}} = 950\text{ tokens}$. By Equation~\eqref{eq:delta_amort}, $\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) = \min\big(10^5, \lfloor \frac{10000 \times 950 \times 10^6}{9500 \times 10000} \rfloor\big) = 10^5\text{ bits}$. With dictionary code $\ell_E(v_u) = 10\text{ bits}$, $\Delta_{\text{saved}} = 99,990\text{ bits}$. The escrow carved out is $E_{\text{tr}} = \text{EscrowAlloc}(10000, 10^6, 99990) = 999\text{ tokens}$, and after the $5\%$ settlement burn, the net payout increment is $\Delta R_{\text{net}} = \lfloor 0.95 \times 999 \rfloor = 949\text{ tokens}$ (and even under the ceiling settlement form $E_{\text{tr}} - \lfloor 0.05 \times 999 \rfloor = 999 - 49 = 950\text{ tokens} \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}} = 950\text{ tokens}$, it never exceeds $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$ in either case). Subtracting DA registration burn $\epsilon_{\text{DA}} L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u)$ and gas fees $g_{\text{tx}}$ leaves the attacker with a strict net deficit.
  \item \textbf{Case 2 (Honest Universal Axiom Cited Across $N_{\text{cite}} = 8$ Datasets):} An honest researcher publishes a general scientific parser $v_s$ of length $10^5\text{ bits}$ backed by $C_{\text{amort\_burn}} = 950\text{ tokens}$ (possibly co-financed via RBF sponsors). Across each of $N_{\text{cite}} = 8$ independent datasets of comparable reward density ($E_{\text{net}} = 950\text{ tokens}$ per dataset), $v_s$ earns gross royalty $R = \lfloor 0.20 \times 950 \rfloor = 190\text{ tokens}$ and net royalty after the $10\%$ anchor rebate of $R^{\text{net}} = 190 - 19 = 171\text{ tokens}$ per dataset. Over $8$ datasets, cumulative net royalties total $8 \times 171 = 1,368\text{ tokens} > 950\text{ tokens}$, yielding a $+44\%$ net return above burn.
\end{itemize}
\end{example}

\subsection{Formal Game-Theoretic Model \& Equilibrium Analysis}
\label{sec:game_theory}
To elevate the Class III economic analysis beyond standalone threshold inequalities, we formalize the strategic interaction among protocol participants as a multi-stage extensive-form game $\Gamma_{\text{ESM}} = \big(\mathcal{N}, (\Sigma_i)_{i \in \mathcal{N}}, (u_i)_{i \in \mathcal{N}}\big)$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Player Set ($\mathcal{N}$):} The game comprises five strategic agent classes:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:player_set}
  \mathcal{N} = \{\text{Anchor}, \; \text{AxiomAuthor}, \; \text{Proposer}, \; \text{Watchtower}, \; \text{Sponsor}\}.
  \end{equation}
  \item \textbf{Strategy Spaces ($\Sigma_i$):}
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item $\Sigma_{\text{Anchor}}$: Register an authentic dataset $D_k$ with external utility $V_{\text{ext}}(\Delta_k) \ge B_k^{(0)}$, or execute a synthetic wash-trading cycle with colluding proposers/axioms.
    \item $\Sigma_{\text{AxiomAuthor}}$: Publish a reusable foundational axiom $v_s$ funded via self-burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$ or RBF sponsorship; register a single-dataset pre-embedded lookup table $v_{\text{pre}}$; or attempt a self-reduction $\mathcal{T}_6$ attack to evade RBF debt $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u) > 0$.
    \item $\Sigma_{\text{Proposer}}$: Submit an honest compression recipe citing amortized axioms $v_s$; inline an unamortized clone of $p_s$ to evade ancestor royalty $\gamma_{\text{bps}}$; submit a corrupted MST with error fraction $f \in (0,1)$ (Mode II-A grinding, with reveal/abort choice after observing VDF seed $\rho_{\text{batch}}$); or execute a two-recipe temporary reservation griefing attack $(e_1, e_2)$.
    \item $\Sigma_{\text{Watchtower}}$: Select post-adoption auditing effort determining the detection probability $q_{\text{detect}} \in [0,1]$ for Mode II-A / Mode III falsification witnesses and $\mathcal{T}_6$ reductions.
    \item $\Sigma_{\text{Sponsor}}$: Contribute RBF burn $b_{u,s} \le B_{\text{target}}(v_u)$ or underwriter collateral $S_{k,1}^{\text{uw}} \le \Lambda S_c$.
  \end{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Payoff Functions ($u_i(\sigma)$):} Each player $i \in \mathcal{N}$ maximizes expected net token payoff $u_i(\sigma)$ evaluated under the exact integer state transitions $\mathcal{T}_0 \sim \mathcal{T}_6$, net of protocol settlement burn $\epsilon_{\text{bps}}$, DA registration burn $\epsilon_{\text{DA}} > 0$ per bit, transaction gas $g_{\text{tx}} > 0$, and slashing penalties.
\end{itemize}

\begin{theorem}[Subgame-Perfect \& Dominant-Strategy Deterrence Equilibria]
\label{thm:game_equilibria}
\textbf{[Class I Algebraic Payoff-Comparison Lemmas \& Class III Equilibrium Analysis]} In the extensive-form game $\Gamma_{\text{ESM}}$ under positive DA/gas costs ($\epsilon_{\text{DA}} L_{\text{axiom}} + g_{\text{tx}} > 0$) and baseline protocol parameters, the following five equilibrium and dominance properties hold (where Theorems 49--53 in \texttt{ESM\_verified.lean} mechanize the underlying algebraic payoff-comparison and waterfall conservation lemmas over $\mathbb{N}$, from which the strategic dominance claims follow):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Strict Dominance of Direct Submission over Single-Dataset Pre-Embedding} \leanref{game\_pre\_embedding\_strictly\_dominated}: For any single dataset $D_k$, the net payout increment from pre-embedding $\Delta_{\text{saved}}$ bits inside an axiom $v_u$ is strictly less than the required burn and registration cost:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:game_pre_embed_dom}
  \Delta R_{\text{net}} \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u, < t_k) < C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u, < t_k) + \epsilon_{\text{DA}} L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) + g_{\text{tx}},
  \end{equation}
  making single-dataset pre-embedding a strictly dominated strategy ($u_{\text{pre}} < u_{\text{direct}}$; under the ceiling settlement form $E_{\text{tr}} - \lfloor \epsilon_{\text{bps}} E_{\text{tr}} / 10000 \rfloor \le \Delta R_{\text{net}} + 1 \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}} + 1$, strict dominance holds unconditionally whenever $\epsilon_{\text{DA}} L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) + g_{\text{tx}} \ge 2\text{ token units}$).
  \item \textbf{Address-Independent Invariance of RBF Priority \& Pre-Lockup Clone Deterrence Under $\mathcal{T}_6$ Reduction} \leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}: Under the Universal Debt-Following Waterfall of Sections~4.3 and~4.4, for any post-lockup redirected slot $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ (regardless of whether $\text{owner}(v_u)$ and $\text{owner}(v_s)$ use a single address or Sybil-split addresses), the priority repayment to $v_u$'s RBF sponsors is $\min(R^{\text{net}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}), D_{\text{tot}}(v_u))$, identical to an unreduced slot while the attacker incurs additional gas cost $g_{\text{T6}} > 0$ (and if self-submitting $\mathcal{T}_6$, forfeits $S_{\text{audit}} - B_{\text{finder}} = 14\text{ tokens}$ of their audit deposit); conversely, under the Regime~1 equilibrium path where a 1-of-$N$ honest watchtower is active during $N_{\text{lock}}$, for any plagiarized clone $v_u$ of an existing axiom $v_s$, the one-time deposit-backed bounty $B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens} > c_{\text{search}} + g_{\text{T6}}$ makes watchtower reduction strictly dominant during $N_{\text{lock}}$ before RBF sponsorship can open ($D_{\text{tot}}(v_u) = 0$, Proposition~\ref{prop:trilemma}). Thus, both $\mathcal{T}_6$ RBF evasion and self-finder/self-sponsor clone arbitrage are strictly dominated.
  \item \textbf{Strict Dominance Against Temporary Reservation Griefing} \leanref{game\_griefing\_strictly\_dominated}: Whenever the parameter inequality $\frac{\kappa_{\text{bps}} \sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}}}{10000 + \Lambda_{\text{bps}}} > \frac{\gamma_{\text{bps}}(10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}})}{10000}$ holds, for every positive bogus tranche escrow $E_{\text{tr},1} > 0$, the permanent slash burn loss $S_{\text{burn}}^c$ on the bogus recipe $e_1$ strictly exceeds the maximum ancestor royalty $R_{\text{evade}}^{\max}$ that a colluding recipe $e_2$ can evade ($S_{\text{burn}}^c > R_{\text{evade}}^{\max}$), rendering reservation griefing strictly unprofitable regardless of watchtower identity.
  \item \textbf{Watchtower Inspection Deterrence Equilibrium} \leanref{game\_watchtower\_deterrence\_equilibrium}:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{Regime A (Macroscopic corruption $f \ge 2.65\%$ [exact: $2.644\%$] at $\Lambda=0$; $f \ge 4.02\%$ [exact: $4.018\%$] at $\Lambda=1$):} Even at zero post-adoption watchtower inspection ($q_{\text{detect}} = 0$), the Phase~1 commit collateral bound $E_{\text{tr}} \le \lfloor 10000(1+\Lambda)S_c / \sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}} \rfloor$ ensures that the expected VDF spot-check forfeiture $(1 - (1-f)^{32}) \cdot 0.70 S_c$ outweighs the pass-through reward $(1-f)^{32} \cdot 0.95 E_{\text{tr}}$, making honest MST reporting dominant.
    \item \emph{Regime B (Microscopic corruption $f \to 0^+$):} In the post-reveal subgame, honest MST reporting is a best response for all $f > 0$ even against worst-case full-tranche fraud ($\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} = 0.95 R_{\text{gross}}$) and worst-case self-front-running ($\alpha \to 1$, net loss $\kappa S_c = 0.70 S_c$) whenever watchtower inspection probability satisfies $q_{\text{detect}} \ge \frac{(1 - \epsilon)(1 + \Lambda)}{\kappa + (1 - \epsilon)(1 + \Lambda)}$ ($0.576$ for $\Lambda=0$; $0.731$ for $\Lambda=1$).
  \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Honest Citation Dominance over Unamortized Cloning} \leanref{game\_honest\_citation\_dominates\_unamortized\_clone}: For any amortized root axiom $v_s$ generating positive marginal net escrow $\Delta E_{\text{net}} > 0$ from its net code savings $\text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_s, D_k) = \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_s, D_k) - \ell_E(v_s) > 0$, a proposer who cites $v_s$ via its $\ell_E(v_s)$-bit pointer and pays ancestor royalty $\lfloor \gamma_{\text{bps}} \Delta E_{\text{net}} / 10000 \rfloor$ (with $\gamma_{\text{bps}} < 10000$, baseline $2000\text{ bps}$) earns a strictly positive net gain $\Delta E_{\text{net}} - \lfloor \gamma_{\text{bps}} \Delta E_{\text{net}} / 10000 \rfloor > 0$, whereas inlining $p_s$ as raw unamortized code ($\Delta_{\text{amort}} = 0$) forfeits $\Delta E_{\text{net}}$ entirely (and registering an amortized clone incurs $\mathcal{T}_6$ reduction back to $v_s$). Hence, honest citation strictly dominates unamortized inlining.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof Sketch]
Theorems 49--53 in \texttt{ESM\_verified.lean} establish the exact natural-number payoff inequalities underlying each game-theoretic claim: Part (1) follows from \texttt{single\_data\_pre\_embedding\_unprofitable} ($\Delta R_{\text{net}} \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$) and \texttt{game\_pre\_embedding\_strictly\_dominated} ($\Delta R_{\text{net}} < C_{\text{amort\_burn}} + \text{burn\_da} + g_{\text{tx}}$ for $\text{burn\_da} + g_{\text{tx}} > 0$). Part (2) follows from the conservation and priority identity $\text{paid}_u = \min(R^{\text{net}}, D_{\text{tot}}(v_u))$ in \texttt{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves} (where the priority ordering is specified via sequential \texttt{let}-bindings in the waterfall definition and Lean mechanically proves exact sum conservation $R^{\text{anchor}} + \text{paid}_u + \text{paid}_s + \text{div} + R^{\text{owner}} = R$ under that specification). Part (3) follows from the cross-multiplied numerator inequality in \texttt{game\_griefing\_strictly\_dominated}. Part (4) combines the binomial pass probability $(1-f)^{32}$ with the scaled payoff inequality $((10000 - q_{\text{bps}})\text{net\_bps}) R_{\text{gross}} \le (q_{\text{bps}} \kappa_{\text{bps}}) S_c$ in \texttt{game\_watchtower\_deterrence\_equilibrium}. Part (5) follows from \texttt{game\_honest\_citation\_dominates\_unamortized\_clone}, which proves $\Delta E_{\text{net}} - \lfloor \gamma_{\text{bps}} \Delta E_{\text{net}} / 10000 \rfloor > 0$ for $\gamma_{\text{bps}} < 10000$ and $\Delta E_{\text{net}} > 0$.
\end{proof}

To address the \emph{Verifier's Dilemma} \cite{luu2015}---in which watchtower inspection $q_{\text{detect}}$ is an endogenous strategic choice by rational auditors facing off-chain verification cost $c_{\text{audit}} > 0$---we model the post-reveal auditing subgame without double-counting slash payouts. When an independent watchtower detects a Mode~II-A fraud, the proposer forfeits $100\%$ of creator collateral ($1.00 S_c$), of which $\kappa S_c = 0.70 S_c$ ($\kappa \equiv \kappa_{\text{bps}}/10000 = 0.70$) is permanently burned and $(1 - \kappa) S_c = 0.30 S_c$ is paid to the watchtower as hunter reward. More generally, if a cheating proposer who observes an incoming watchtower challenge can front-run their own puncture transaction with probability $\alpha \in [0, 1)$ (where $\alpha = 0$ under commit-reveal/VDF ordering and $\alpha \to 1$ is the worst-case bound), the proposer's expected net collateral loss upon detection and the independent watchtower's expected hunter reward are:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:slash_alpha_accounting}
L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha) \equiv \big(1 - (1 - \kappa)\alpha\big) S_c \in [0.70 S_c, \; 1.00 S_c], \qquad
R_{\text{hunter}}(\alpha) \equiv (1 - \alpha)(1 - \kappa) S_c = (1 - \alpha) 0.30 S_c.
\end{equation}
Furthermore, denoting the Phase~1 pre-reveal grinding threshold for general leverage $\Lambda \in \{0, 1\}$ by $f_{\text{grind}}(\Lambda)$ ($f_{\text{grind}}(0) = 2.65\%$ and $f_{\text{grind}}(1) = 4.02\%$), because the microscopic corruption fraction $f \in (0, f_{\text{grind}}(\Lambda))$ is the proposer's private choice prior to auditing, the watchtower's inspection strategy $q_{\text{detect}} \in [0, 1]$ cannot be conditioned on $f$. Instead, a cheating proposer selects an optimal private fraud scale $f^* \in \arg\max_{f \in (0, f_{\text{grind}}(\Lambda)]} \Delta R_{\text{fraud}}(f)$ (yielding target incremental fraudulent gain $\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} \equiv \Delta R_{\text{fraud}}(f^*) \le 0.95 R_{\text{gross}}$), while Theorem~\ref{thm:game_equilibria}(4) Regime~B characterizes the conservative sufficient deterrence condition under an exogenous minimum inspection floor $q_{\text{detect}} \ge 0.576$ ($\Lambda=0$) or $0.731$ ($\Lambda=1$) in the worst-case limit $\alpha \to 1$:

\begin{proposition}[Endogenous Mixed-Strategy Inspection Equilibrium]
\label{prop:endogenous_watchtower}
\textbf{[Class III Endogenous Auditing Game]} In the post-reveal inspection subgame with target fraudulent gain $\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} > 0$, self-front-running parameter $\alpha \in [0, 1)$, and non-degeneracy auditing cost condition $c_{\text{audit}} < R_{\text{hunter}}(\alpha) = (1 - \alpha) 0.30 S_c$ (equivalently, $\alpha < 1 - \frac{c_{\text{audit}}}{0.30 S_c}$), no pure-strategy Nash equilibrium exists, and the unique mixed-strategy Nash equilibrium $(p_{\text{cheat}}^*, q_{\text{detect}}^*)$ is given by:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:mixed_nash_watchtower}
p_{\text{cheat}}^* = \frac{c_{\text{audit}}}{R_{\text{hunter}}(\alpha)} = \frac{c_{\text{audit}}}{(1 - \alpha) 0.30 S_c}, \qquad
q_{\text{detect}}^* = \frac{\Delta R_{\text{fraud}}}{\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} + L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha)}.
\end{equation}
In particular, when self-front-running is prevented ($\alpha = 0$), $L_{\text{slash}}(0) = 1.00 S_c$ and $R_{\text{hunter}}(0) = 0.30 S_c$; even in the worst-case limit $\alpha \to 1$, $L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha) \ge 0.70 S_c = \kappa S_c$ holds unconditionally (matching the conservative bound mechanized in \texttt{game\_watchtower\_deterrence\_equilibrium}), while for small incremental fraud $\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} \ll S_c$, the required equilibrium inspection rate satisfies $q_{\text{detect}}^* = O(\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} / S_c)$ and the equilibrium cheating rate decays as $p_{\text{cheat}}^* = O(c_{\text{audit}} / S_c)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof Sketch]
An independent watchtower is indifferent between auditing (expected net payoff $p_{\text{cheat}} R_{\text{hunter}}(\alpha) - c_{\text{audit}}$) and not auditing ($0$) if and only if $p_{\text{cheat}}^* = c_{\text{audit}} / R_{\text{hunter}}(\alpha)$. Given $q_{\text{detect}}$, a cheating proposer selects $f^*$ to maximize incremental gain $\Delta R_{\text{fraud}}$ and is indifferent between cheating at scale $\Delta R_{\text{fraud}}$ (expected incremental payoff $(1 - q_{\text{detect}}) \Delta R_{\text{fraud}} - q_{\text{detect}} L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha)$ over honest reporting) and reporting honestly ($0$) if and only if $q_{\text{detect}}^* = \Delta R_{\text{fraud}} / (\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} + L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha))$. Substituting $\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} \le 0.95 R_{\text{gross}}$, $L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha) \ge 0.70 S_c$, and $S_c \ge R_{\text{gross}} / (1 + \Lambda)$ recovers the worst-case upper bound $q_{\text{detect}}^* \le 0.95(1+\Lambda)/(0.70 + 0.95(1+\Lambda))$.
\end{proof}

\section{Scalability, Falsification, and Financing Extensions}
\label{sec:extensions}

\subsection{Extension 1: Optimistic Spot-Check \& Boundary Puncture (\texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{T}_2$}{T2})}
For massive datasets (e.g., $n_k = 10^6$ to $10^9$ records), proving $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$ evaluation across all $n_k$ records inside a single STARK at reveal time would impose prohibitive prover latency. ESM resolves this via a hybrid \textbf{Optimistic VDF Spot-Check + Post-Adoption Boundary Puncture ($\mathcal{T}_2$)} architecture:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Reveal-Time VDF Spot-Check ($K_{\text{spot}} = 32$):} During the reveal window $N_{\text{rev}}$, the proposer submits a single STARK proof verifying that (i) the MST root is well-formed with total residual length $L_{\text{res}}$, and (ii) for $K_{\text{spot}} = 32$ record indices $j_1, \dots, j_{32} \in \{0, \dots, n_k - 1\}$ pseudorandomly derived from the post-commit VDF output $\rho_{\text{batch}}$, the revealed program $p_{\text{rec}}$ executes within $T_{\max}$ steps and its residual $y_{j_m} \oplus U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}(p_{\text{rec}}(j_m))$ exactly matches the $j_m$-th leaf of the committed MST.
  \item \textbf{Post-Adoption Falsification Modes ($\mathcal{T}_2$):} During the challenge lockup period following adoption, any watchtower can submit a concise STARK/Merkle falsification witness under two distinct modes:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Mode II-A (In-Distribution / Training Data Fraud):} The proposer's committed MST leaf at some index $j \in \{0, \dots, n_k-1\}$ does not match the actual residual bit-length of $y_j \oplus U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}(p_{\text{rec}}(j))$ (i.e., the proposer cheated on a record that missed the $K_{\text{spot}}=32$ sample). Mode II-A triggers \textbf{$100\%$ full slashing} of both creator collateral $S_{k,1}^{\text{creator}}$ and underwriter collateral $S_{k,1}^{\text{uw}}$, rolling back the entire recipe.
    \item \textbf{Mode III (Out-of-Sample Invariant / Symmetry Puncture via Committed $V_{\text{inv}}$):} The recipe was honest on committed training leaves, but violates the dataset's on-chain invariant checker $V_{\text{inv}} \in \{0,1\}^*$ over the validity domain $\Omega_k : \{0,1\}^{a_k} \to \{0,1\}$ committed at $\mathcal{T}_0$ (Section~3.2). Because $V_{\text{inv}}$ and $\Omega_k$ are publicly committed on-chain by the Data Anchor at $\mathcal{T}_0$, proposers can audit the well-formedness of $V_{\text{inv}}$ off-chain before submitting any recipe. Specifically, a valid Mode~III witness submits a set of distinct counterexample inputs $Z_{\text{punct}} = \{z_1, \dots, z_m\}$ with $1 \le |Z_{\text{punct}}| \le K_{\text{punct}}^{\max} = 32$ satisfying $\Omega_k(z) = 1$ and $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}(V_{\text{inv}}(p_{\text{rec}}, z)) = 0$ for each $z \in Z_{\text{punct}}$, which deterministically increments the recipe's description length by the protocol-defined patch-table penalty $\delta \Phi \equiv |Z_{\text{punct}}| \cdot (a_k + b_k) \ge 1$ (the exact bit-length required to hardcode each falsified input $z \in \{0,1\}^{a_k}$ and its $b_k$-bit output as an exception patch, or $|Z_{\text{punct}}| \cdot b_k$ when input coordinates are implicit). Puncture on any given recipe is a \textbf{single-shot resolution} (once punctured, the recipe's surviving reduction $d_{\text{rem}} \equiv \Delta_{k,1}^{\text{tot}} \ominus \delta \Phi \le d_{\text{init}} + d_{\text{abs}}$ is finalized and the same recipe is not repeatedly re-punctured). Under Mode III, the proportional slashing rate $r_{\text{bps}} \in \{1, \dots, 10000\}$ and unrolled escrow are priced \textbf{time-invariantly across tranche maturity using the adoption-time initial total reduction $\Delta_{k,1}^{\text{tot}} \equiv d_{\text{init}} + d_{\text{abs}}$ (rather than remaining unsettled balance) as the denominator}, ensuring that hunter rewards and slashing penalties do not vary depending on whether a puncture occurs before or after the initial tranche settles \leanref{puncture\_invariant\_preserved, tranche\_unroll\_conserves\_and\_bounds, stepPuncture\_concrete\_preserves\_inv}. Specifically, the surviving initial and absorbed escrows are scaled via:
    \begin{equation}
    \label{eq:safe_rem_escrow}
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{safeRemEscrow}&(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, E_{\text{tr}}, d_{\text{tr}}, d_{\text{rem,tr}}) \\
    &\equiv \min\!\left(\lfloor (E_{\text{tr}} \cdot d_{\text{rem,tr}}) / d_{\text{tr}} \rfloor, \; \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, d_{\text{rem,tr}})\right),
    \end{aligned}
    \end{equation}
    while the unrolled escrow $E_{\text{unroll}} \equiv \min(E_{\text{total}} - E_{\text{rem}}, \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, d_{\text{unroll}}))$ is transferred to the Priority Claim Hold pool $e_{\text{hold}}$ (whose expiration and underwriter absorption are governed by transition $\mathcal{T}_3$) and any rounding dust $E_{\text{dust}} \equiv E_{\text{total}} - E_{\text{rem}} - E_{\text{unroll}}$ returns directly to $B_k^{\text{rem}}$, preserving $E_{\text{rem}} + E_{\text{unroll}} + E_{\text{dust}} = E_{\text{total}}$ (\leanref{tranche\_unroll\_conserves\_and\_bounds}).
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{Theoretical Grinding Boundaries and Watchtower Detection Thresholds}
Suppose an adversary attempts a grinding attack under Mode II-A: submitting a corrupted MST that under-reports residuals on a fraction $f \in (0,1)$ of the $n_k$ records, hoping all $K_{\text{spot}} = 32$ VDF-selected indices land on the $(1-f)$ honest records (which occurs with probability $P_{\text{pass}}(f) = (1 - f)^{32}$). Let $\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}} = 10000$ ($100\%$ total recipe collateral requirement relative to gross reward $R_{\text{gross}} \equiv E_{\text{tr}}$), settlement burn $\epsilon_{\text{bps}} = 500$ ($5\%$, so maximum net reward is $0.95 R_{\text{gross}}$), and unrevealed commit forfeiture rate $70\%$ ($c_{\text{forfeit}} = 0.70 S_c$) if the VDF seed catches a corrupted leaf during the reveal window and the attacker aborts reveal. With underwriter leverage ratio $\Lambda \equiv S_{k,1}^{\text{uw}} / S_{k,1}^{\text{creator}} \in \{0, 1\}$ (or in basis points $\Lambda_{\text{bps}} \equiv 10000 \Lambda$), because the Phase 1 commit collateral $S_c \equiv S_{k,1}^{\text{creator}}$ caps the maximum claimable Phase 2 gross escrow at $R_{\text{gross}} \le \lfloor 10000 (1 + \Lambda) S_c / \sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}} \rfloor$, the attacker's locked creator collateral satisfies $S_c \ge R_{\text{gross}} / (1 + \Lambda)$ (and thus unrevealed forfeiture $0.70 S_c \ge 0.70 R_{\text{gross}} / (1 + \Lambda)$):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Pre-Reveal Grinding Threshold ($q_{\text{detect}} = 0$ worst case):} If the attacker aborts whenever the $K_{\text{spot}}=32$ sample hits a corrupted record, expected profit is non-positive whenever $P_{\text{pass}}(f) \cdot 0.95 R_{\text{gross}} - (1 - P_{\text{pass}}(f)) \cdot 0.70 \frac{R_{\text{gross}}}{1 + \Lambda} \le 0$, i.e., $(1 - f)^{32} \le \frac{0.70}{0.70 + 0.95(1 + \Lambda)}$.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item For $\Lambda = 0$ (no underwriter): $(1 - f)^{32} \le \frac{0.70}{1.65} = 0.42424\dots \implies \mathbf{f \ge 2.65\%}$ \text{(exact: $2.644\%$)}.
    \item For $\Lambda = 1$ (1:1 underwriter leverage): $(1 - f)^{32} \le \frac{0.70}{2.60} = 0.26923\dots \implies \mathbf{f \ge 4.02\%}$ \text{(exact: $4.018\%$)}.
  \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Post-Reveal Watchtower Detection Condition for Microscopic Errors ($f \to 0^+$):} If $f$ is arbitrarily small ($P_{\text{pass}}(f) \approx 1$), the attacker must reveal on-chain to claim the reward, exposing the full recipe to post-adoption Mode II-A verification where detection slashes $100\%$ of $S_{k,1}^{\text{creator}} = R_{\text{gross}} / (1 + \Lambda)$. Accounting for the worst-case scenario in which the attacker front-runs their own puncture as hunter to recover the $1 - \kappa_{\text{bps}}/10000 = 30\%$ hunter reward (net slash $0.70 S_{k,1}^{\text{creator}}$), expected profit $(1 - q_{\text{detect}}) \cdot 0.95 R_{\text{gross}} - q_{\text{detect}} \cdot 0.70 \frac{R_{\text{gross}}}{1 + \Lambda} \le 0$ holds for all $f > 0$ whenever the 1-of-$N$ watchtower detection probability $q_{\text{detect}}$ satisfies:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:q_detect_bounds}
  q_{\text{detect}} \ge \frac{0.95(1 + \Lambda)}{0.70 + 0.95(1 + \Lambda)} =
  \begin{cases}
  \frac{0.95}{1.65} \approx \mathbf{0.576} & (\Lambda = 0), \\[4pt]
  \frac{1.90}{2.60} \approx \mathbf{0.731} & (\Lambda = 1),
  \end{cases}
  \end{equation}
  which represents the worst-case ceiling when $\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} = 0.95 R_{\text{gross}}$ and $\alpha \to 1$ ($L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha) = 0.70 S_c$; for $\alpha = 0$ where self-front-running is prevented, $L_{\text{slash}}(0) = 1.00 S_c$ lowers these ceilings to $0.95/1.95 \approx 0.487$ at $\Lambda=0$ and $1.90/2.90 \approx 0.655$ at $\Lambda=1$). For smaller target fraud $\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} \ll R_{\text{gross}}$, the endogenous equilibrium inspection rate $q_{\text{detect}}^* = \Delta R_{\text{fraud}} / (\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} + L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha))$ of Proposition~\ref{prop:endogenous_watchtower} scales down as $O(\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} / S_c)$.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Symmetric Reservation Release on Puncture and Griefing Defense}
When a tranche is punctured under $\mathcal{T}_2$ from initial reduction $\Delta_{k,1}^{\text{tr}} > 0$ down to surviving reduction $\Delta_{\text{rem}}^{\text{tr}} \le \Delta_{k,1}^{\text{tr}}$, each redirected root slot $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ and retained derivative slot $x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ scales down proportionally:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:puncture_slot_scale}
x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr,new}} \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}} \Delta_{\text{rem}}^{\text{tr}}}{\Delta_{k,1}^{\text{tr}}} \right\rfloor, \qquad
x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr,new}} \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}} \Delta_{\text{rem}}^{\text{tr}}}{\Delta_{k,1}^{\text{tr}}} \right\rfloor.
\end{equation}
Let the slot reductions be $\delta x_s \equiv x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}} - x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr,new}}$ and $\delta x_u \equiv x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}} - x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr,new}}$. Because Stage 1 added the full $a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}} = x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}} + x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ to $S_{\text{paid}}(v_u, D_k)$ while Stage 2 added $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ to $S_{\text{paid}}(v_s, D_k)$, ESM executes \textbf{symmetric release}:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:symmetric_release}
S_{\text{paid}}(v_s, D_k) \mathrel{-}= \delta x_s, \qquad
S_{\text{paid}}(v_u, D_k) \mathrel{-}= (\delta x_s + \delta x_u).
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Exactness of Symmetric Puncture Release vs.\ Leakage of Asymmetric Release]
\label{lem:release_symmetric}
\leanref{release\_symmetric, puncture\_slot\_scale\_valid, old\_rule\_leaks}
For any prior reservation $\text{base} \in \mathbb{N}$, slot allocations $x_s, x_u \in \mathbb{N}$, and reductions $r = \Delta_{\text{rem}}^{\text{tr}} \le d = \Delta_{k,1}^{\text{tr}}$ with $d > 0$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:release_sym_id}
(\text{base} + x_s + x_u) - \Big(\big(x_s - \lfloor x_s r / d \rfloor\big) + \big(x_u - \lfloor x_u r / d \rfloor\big)\Big) = \text{base} + \lfloor x_s r / d \rfloor + \lfloor x_u r / d \rfloor.
\end{equation}
Conversely, subtracting only $\delta x_u$ from $S_{\text{paid}}(v_u, D_k)$ strictly leaks reservation capacity whenever $\lfloor x_s r / d \rfloor < x_s$ (mechanized counterexample in \texttt{old\_rule\_leaks}: for $\text{base}=0, x_s=10, x_u=10, r=1, d=2$, $(0+10+10)-(10-5) = 15 \neq 10$).
\end{lemma}

Finally, to prevent \textbf{Temporary Reservation Griefing} --- where an attacker submits a bogus first recipe $e_1$ (with tranche reduction $\Delta_{\text{tr},1}$ and escrow $E_{\text{tr},1}$) citing ancestor $v_s$ to temporarily reserve $x \le \Delta_{\text{tr},1}$ bits of $S_{\text{paid}}(v_s, D_k)$, immediately submits a colluding second recipe $e_2$ that cites $v_s$ at zero marginal royalty on those $x$ bits while $S_{\text{paid}}(v_s, D_k)$ is saturated, and then self-punctures $e_1$ to recover the hunter reward --- ESM slashes the proposer's creator stake at slashing rate $r_{\text{bps}} \in \{1, \dots, 10000\}$ ($r_{\text{bps}} = 10000$ for full puncture of the bogus reservation) according to:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:slash_split}
S_{\text{slash}}^c \equiv \left\lceil \frac{r_{\text{bps}} S_{k,1}^{\text{creator}}}{10000} \right\rceil, \quad
S_{\text{hunter}} \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{(10000 - \kappa_{\text{bps}}) S_{\text{slash}}^c}{10000} \right\rfloor, \quad
S_{\text{burn}}^c \equiv S_{\text{slash}}^c - S_{\text{hunter}} \ge \left\lceil \frac{\kappa_{\text{bps}} S_{\text{slash}}^c}{10000} \right\rceil,
\end{equation}
where $\kappa_{\text{bps}} \in \{0, \dots, 10000\}$ (baseline $\kappa_{\text{bps}} = 7000\text{ bps} = 70\%$) is the slash burn fraction and $\Lambda_{\text{bps}} \equiv 10000 \Lambda$. Because the bogus recipe $e_1$ can temporarily lock at most $x \le \Delta_{\text{tr},1}$ bits of $v_s$'s reservation cap, the maximum ancestor royalty that the colluding recipe $e_2$ can evade across those $x$ bits is bounded by $\frac{\gamma_{\text{bps}}(10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}})}{10000^2} E_{\text{tr},1}$, whereas even if the attacker acts as their own hunter on $e_1$ to recapture $S_{\text{hunter}}$, they permanently lose $S_{\text{burn}}^c \ge \frac{\kappa_{\text{bps}}}{10000} \frac{\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}}}{10000 + \Lambda_{\text{bps}}} E_{\text{tr},1}$ (for $r_{\text{bps}} = 10000$). Thus, temporary reservation griefing is guaranteed to result in a deterministic net deficit whenever:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:griefing_constraint}
\frac{\kappa_{\text{bps}} \cdot \sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}}}{10000 + \Lambda_{\text{bps}}} > \frac{\gamma_{\text{bps}} \cdot (10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}})}{10000} \quad \Big(\text{Baseline}: 3500\text{ bps} > 1900\text{ bps}\Big).
\end{equation}
In particular, even accounting for the integer floor rounding in ceiling settlement $E_{\text{net}} = E_{\text{tr}} - \lfloor \epsilon_{\text{bps}} E_{\text{tr}} / 10000 \rfloor$ (which can differ from the continuous ratio by at most $+1\text{ token}$ unit), the $+1600\text{ bps}$ safety margin at the baseline parameters ensures that the strict integer inequality $S_{\text{burn}}^c > R_{\text{evade}}^{\max}$ holds unconditionally for every positive integer tranche $E_{\text{tr},1} \ge 1$ (for $\Lambda = 1$, $S_c = \lceil E_{\text{tr},1}/2 \rceil$ gives $S_{\text{burn}}^c \ge 0.35\,E_{\text{tr},1}$ while $R_{\text{evade}}^{\max} < 0.19\,E_{\text{tr},1} + 0.2$, so $S_{\text{burn}}^c > R_{\text{evade}}^{\max}$ for all $E_{\text{tr},1} \ge 2$, and $E_{\text{tr},1} = 1$ gives $1 > 0$; this is confirmed by the exhaustive integer scan over $E_{\text{tr}} \in [1, 20000]$ in Section~\ref{sec:sensitivity}).

\subsection{Extension 2: Priority Claim Hold Window (\texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{T}_3$}{T3}) \& Underwriter Market}
When a recipe is punctured during its challenge window, or when sequential batches compete on overlapping data ranges, unrolled bits $d_{\text{unroll}}$ and unrolled escrow $E_{\text{unroll}}$ are placed into a temporary \textbf{Priority Claim Hold Bucket} $(d_{\text{hold}}, e_{\text{hold}} \equiv B_k^{\text{hold}})$ for a duration of $N_{\text{claim}} = 60\text{ blocks}$ (one full batch cycle). At the conclusion of the hold window under transition $\mathcal{T}_3$ (Priority claim hold window expiration \& underwriter absorption, \leanref{stepHoldExpire\_concrete\_preserves\_inv}), a single winning beneficiary recipe $e_{\text{ben}}$ absorbs $d_{\text{abs\_add}} \le d_{\text{hold}}$ bits and receives escrow:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:hold_absorb}
e_{\text{abs\_add}} \equiv \min\left(\left\lfloor \frac{e_{\text{hold}} \cdot d_{\text{abs\_add}}}{d_{\text{hold}}} \right\rfloor, \; \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, d_{\text{abs\_add}})\right),
\end{equation}
while any unabsorbed bits $d_{\text{rewind}} \equiv d_{\text{hold}} - d_{\text{abs\_add}}$ rewind the frontier $\Phi_k^{\text{best}} \mathrel{+}= d_{\text{rewind}}$ and return $e_{\text{ret}} \equiv e_{\text{hold}} - e_{\text{abs\_add}}$ to $B_k^{\text{rem}}$, preserving \texttt{DataInv} identically.

\subsection{Extension 3: RBF Burn Sponsorship \& Epoch-Delayed \texorpdfstring{$\phi_u^{\text{dict}}$}{phi\_u\_dict} Governance (\texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{T}_5$}{T5})}
Because foundational axiom authors may lack the liquid capital to fund $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u)$ upfront, ESM includes a native \textbf{Revenue-Based Financing (RBF) Burn Sponsorship} primitive:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Approved Target Cap \& $2.0\times$ Repayment Ceiling} \leanref{rbf\_lifetime\_cap, rbf\_prorata\_le\_list, slot\_four\_way\_split\_conserves}: An axiom owner sets an approved burn sponsorship target $B_{\text{target}}(v_u)$ after $v_u$ survives $N_{\text{lock}}$ unreduced. To bound on-chain settlement gas to $O(1)$ and prevent dust fragmentation, each axiom is limited to at most $K_{\text{rbf}}^{\max} = 10$ sponsor slots with minimum contribution $b_{u,s} \ge \lceil B_{\text{target}}(v_u) / 10 \rceil$. During $\mathcal{T}_4$ settlement, the aggregate repayment $R_{\text{repay}}(x) \equiv \min(R^{\text{net}}(x), D_{\text{tot}}(v_m))$ is deducted in full from $R^{\text{net}}(x)$ strictly prior to dictionary guarantor dividends (fixing $R^{\text{rem}}(x) \equiv R^{\text{net}}(x) - R_{\text{repay}}(x)$ with zero rounding leakage to the owner, while any integer remainder $R_{\text{repay}}(x) - \sum_s \text{paid}_s(x)$ after Equation~\eqref{eq:rbf_paid} is allocated in $1\text{-token}$ units in commit-priority order to sponsors with $D_{m,s} > 0$); furthermore, under the address-independent \textbf{Universal Debt-Following Waterfall} established in Sections~4.3 and~4.4 (\leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}), on any redirected slot $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ from derivative $v_u$ to root $v_s$, $R^{\text{net}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}})$ universally repays $v_u$'s remaining approved RBF debt $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u)$ in full first ($\text{paid}_u$), next repays $v_s$'s RBF debt $D_{\text{tot}}(v_s)$ ($\text{paid}_s$), and only the residual $R^{\text{rem}}$ flows to dictionary guarantors and $\text{owner}(v_s)$, guaranteeing that axiom owners can neither dilute burn sponsors by raising dictionary dividend rates nor evade RBF debt via single-address or Sybil-split $\mathcal{T}_6$ reduction.
  \item \textbf{Separation of $\phi_i^{\text{rec}}$ and Epoch-Delayed $\phi_u^{\text{dict}}$ Governance} \leanref{tEff\_bounds}: Each recipe fixes its underwriter fee rate $\phi_i^{\text{rec}}$ immutably at creation.   \label{eq:t_eff}

  \end{equation}

  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:t_eff_bounds}
  2 N_{\text{epoch}} \le t_{\text{eff}}(t_{\text{req}}) - t_{\text{req}} < 3 N_{\text{epoch}},
  \end{equation}
  guaranteeing that every dictionary staker has at least two full epochs of advance notice to initiate withdrawal before any rate change becomes active.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Hardware-Accelerated BLC Jets (Precompiles) for Numerical Workloads:} While pure Scott-encoded bit-list arithmetic in untyped $\lambda$-calculus keeps the formal semantics of $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$ minimal and executes symbolic/bitwise laws in $9.0\times 10^2$ to $7.5\times 10^3$ KAM steps (Table~\ref{tab:blc_benchmarks}), dense 64-bit matrix or floating-point emulation in pure $\lambda$-calculus would incur large constant-factor step overhead. Following the established design of verified functional virtual machines, ESM supports \textbf{Hardware-Accelerated BLC Jets}: canonical BLC sub-terms (identified by their exact $\mathcal{T}_0/\mathcal{T}_1$ Merkle hashes, such as 64-bit integer modular multiplication, bitwise XOR, and fixed-point inner products) whose pure $\lambda$-reduction semantics remain unchanged, but which the KAM interpreter and STARK Algebraic Intermediate Representation (AIR) evaluate in a single native word transition. Because a jet's bit-length and input-output relation are identical to its underlying BLC $\lambda$-term, jets preserve exact MDL Kolmogorov/Rissanen semantics while reducing both off-chain KAM execution time and STARK trace height by two to three orders of magnitude.

Table~\ref{tab:agent_sim} reports the exact cumulative net profit (in tokens) across five competing agent strategies (all 25 cells of Table~\ref{tab:agent_sim}, as well as every boundary cell of Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity} in Section~\ref{sec:sensitivity}, are automatically computed from the exact integer waterfall pipeline and asserted as \texttt{PASS} by \texttt{esm\_exp2\_sensitivity.py}):

  \item \textbf{Strategy 1 (Single-Dataset Pre-Embedding Attacker):} Embeds $10^5\text{ bits}$ of $D_1$-specific lookup data ($\ell_E = 10\text{ bits}$), burning $950\text{ tokens}$ plus $10\text{ tokens}$ DA burn. \subsection{Mechanism Ablation and Multi-Agent Equilibrium Comparison}
\label{sec:ablation}
To isolate what each ESM component contributes relative to existing paradigms, we compare five mechanisms under exact-integer, deterministic subgame-perfect equilibrium (SPE) analysis (\texttt{esm\_exp3\_ablation.py}): \textbf{M1} a Hutter-style single-shot prize \cite{hutter2005} (winner-takes-prize on each dataset; no axiom registry, amortization, royalties, or protocol burn); \textbf{M2} a naive free-citation market (citation and royalties as in ESM, but the exemption $\Delta_{\text{amort}} = L_{\text{axiom}}$ is granted without any pre-burn); \textbf{M3} burn-backed amortization without $\mathcal{T}_6$; \textbf{M4$-$} ESM Core (burn-backed amortization plus $\mathcal{T}_6$ reduction and Stage~2 redirection); and \textbf{M4} ESM Full (M4$-$ plus RBF sponsor financing with the Universal $2.0\times$ debt-following waterfall).

\textbf{Model and Assumptions.} All mechanisms face the same stream of $N = 12$ Benchmark-3-type datasets ($\Phi_k^{(0)} = 16,384\text{ bits}$, $B = 10,000\text{ tokens}$, $L_{\text{res}} = 750\text{ bits}$; baseline $\epsilon_{\text{bps}} = 500$, $\gamma_{\text{bps}} = 2000$, $\beta_{D,\text{bps}} = 1000$, $\kappa_{\text{bps}} = 7000$, $B_{\text{finder}} = 6$, $S_{\text{audit}} = 20$). Model assumptions of this stylized analysis are a development cost $c_{\text{dev}} = 20\text{ tokens}$ per independently discovered axiom, base transaction gas $g_{\text{tx}} = 1\text{ token}$, $\mathcal{T}_6$ gas $g_{\mathcal{T}_6} = 2 g_{\text{tx}} + c_{\text{step}} \cdot \text{fuel}_{\max}$ with the execution fuel term normalized to $c_{\text{step}} \cdot \text{fuel}_{\max} = 1\text{ token}$ (yielding $g_{\mathcal{T}_6} = 3\text{ tokens}$ at baseline $g_{\text{tx}} = 1$), search cost $c_{\text{search}} = 1\text{ token}$, and a DA fee of $\lceil 10^{-4}\,\text{bits} \rceil$ tokens. The agents are (i) an honest root-axiom author $H$ ($|p_s| = 85\text{ bits}$, own burn bounded by a capital budget; Stackelberg leader), (ii) a clone plagiarist $Q$ (exact $85\text{-bit}$ copy or $103\text{-bit}$ padded copy of $p_s$ with a free choice of burn), (iii) a pre-embedding attacker $P$ who knows $D_1$ in advance and registers a $16,400\text{-bit}$ lookup axiom, (iv) recipe proposers who, for each dataset, either cite a registered axiom or inline its code so as to maximize $E_{\text{net}}$ minus royalties (the lowest $\Phi_{\text{local}}$ wins the tranche), (v) a watchtower $W$ that submits $\mathcal{T}_6$ against a clone if and only if $B_{\text{finder}} - g_{\mathcal{T}_6} - c_{\text{search}} = 2 > 0$, and (vi) an RBF sponsor that funds the root's burn only if its $2.0\times$ debt claim repays at least its principal. After $H$ commits, $Q$ and $P$ play a simultaneous Nash subgame; every action is drawn from a finite grid and best responses are obtained by exhaustive enumeration (no randomness). Social welfare is $W = V \cdot S_{\text{true}} - \text{real\_costs}$ with $V = B / \Phi_k^{(0)}$ tokens per bit, where $S_{\text{true}}$ is the true corpus-level description-length saving (each distinct axiom content counted once, lookup tables counted in full) and burns, escrow, and royalties are transfers; real costs are development, gas, DA, and watchtower work. Three scenarios are reported: S1 (well-funded author with a $100$-token budget; proposers break exact ties in favor of the earlier-registered root), S2 (capital-constrained author with a $12$-token budget), and S3 (S1 with an adversarial tie-break in which proposers prefer the clone on exact ties).

\textbf{Result 1 (Pre-Embedding Defense: M2 vs.\ M3/M4).} Without burn backing (M2), the pre-embedding attacker $P$ extracts $9,271\text{ tokens}$ at $D_1$ (about $93\%$ of that tranche's escrow $E_{\text{tr}} = 9,962$ tokens) and social welfare degrades from $113,894$ to $104,345\text{ tokens}$ ($-8.4\%$). In M3, M4$-$, and M4, the best pre-embedding attack that wins $D_1$ yields $-238\text{ tokens} < 0$, consistent with the negative-profit bound of Lemma~\ref{lem:single_data_pre_embed}, so extraction is deterred to $0$.

\textbf{Result 2 (Incentive to Publish and Tie-Break Robustness: M1 and M3 vs.\ M4$-$).} Under the Hutter-style prize (M1) there are no citation royalties, so the root axiom is never published and every proposer re-develops it inline: welfare is $113,193\text{ tokens}$ ($701\text{ tokens}$ below ESM) and the author earns nothing. Under the adversarial tie-break of S3, M3, which lacks $\mathcal{T}_6$, collapses: a clone captures the royalties, the author withdraws from the market (published net $0$), and welfare falls to $113,193$. M4$-$ and M4 instead keep the author's publication equilibrium independent of the tie-break rule through $\mathcal{T}_6$ (royalties $108\text{ tokens}$, net $+36\text{ tokens}$, maximal welfare $113,894\text{ tokens}$).

\textbf{Result 3 (Capital-Constrained Author and RBF Synergy: M3 vs.\ M4$-$ vs.\ M4).} In the counterfactual where an author with only $12\text{ tokens}$ of own burn publishes anyway (Table~\ref{tab:ablation_dormant}), M3 lets the clone $Q$ capture $+58\text{ tokens}$ and leaves the author zero royalties (net $-34$). M4$-$ ($\mathcal{T}_6$ alone) cuts the clone's profit to $+32\text{ tokens}$ (about $-45\%$) and returns $12\text{ tokens}$ of royalties to the author, but does not by itself remove the author's capital shortfall (net $-22$). Adding RBF sponsor co-financing (M4, sponsor burn $38\text{ tokens}$) lifts the total burn to $12 + 38 = 50\text{ tokens}$, i.e., full amortization of the $85\text{-bit}$ root: the clone is fully excluded ($Q$ payoff $0$), the sponsor earns $+38\text{ tokens}$, and the author's net payoff, still $-2\text{ tokens}$ at $N = 12$, becomes $+106\text{ tokens}$ at $N = 24$ (welfare $227,861\text{ tokens}$). At $N = 12$, a capital-constrained author does not publish in any burn-backed mechanism, whereas at $N = 24$ only M4 sustains publication.

\textbf{Robustness Across Cost and Information Assumptions (\texttt{esm\_exp3\_ablation.py}).} To verify that these equilibrium separation results are not artifacts of a single parameter point, Experiment~4b sweeps the three auxiliary modeling assumptions and locates exact phase boundaries via monotone bisection and closed-form verification:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Axiom Development Cost ($c_{\text{dev}} \in [5, 80]\text{ tokens}$):} Pre-embedding exploitation under M2 ($+9,271\text{ tokens}$), pre-embedding deterrence under M3/M4$-$/M4 (best winning attack $-238\text{ tokens}$), and clone exclusion via M4 sponsor co-financing hold across the entire tested range. Under both S1 and S3, the well-funded honest author publishes in equilibrium at $N = 12$ if and only if $c_{\text{dev}} \le \mathbf{56\text{ tokens}}$, matching the exact closed form $108\text{ (royalties)} - 50\text{ (burn)} - 1\text{ (DA)} - 1\text{ (gas)} = 56\text{ tokens}$. For the capital-constrained author (own burn $12\text{ tokens}$), equilibrium publication at $N = 24$ is recovered exclusively by M4 (RBF co-financing) across $\mathbf{11 \le c_{\text{dev}} \le 126\text{ tokens}}$ (for $c_{\text{dev}} \le 10\text{ tokens}$, M4$-$ alone suffices to sustain publication at $N = 24$, whereas at $N = 12$ M4 sustains publication for $c_{\text{dev}} \le \mathbf{18\text{ tokens}}$).
  \item \emph{Transaction Gas ($g_{\text{tx}} \in [0, 5]\text{ tokens}$) \& $S_{\text{audit}}$ Scaling Boundary:} If the syntactic-audit deposit is held fixed at its baseline $S_{\text{audit}} = 20\text{ tokens}$ ($B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 0.30 S_{\text{audit}} \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens}$), the watchtower's net profit condition $B_{\text{finder}} - (2 g_{\text{tx}} + 1) - c_{\text{search}} > 0$ holds if and only if $g_{\text{tx}} \le \mathbf{1\text{ token}}$; for $g_{\text{tx}} \ge 2\text{ tokens}$, watchtowers no longer submit $\mathcal{T}_6$ and M4$-$ degrades to M3 in S3 (author net drops to $0$). However, scaling the minimum required audit deposit in accordance with the watchtower profitability condition $\lfloor 0.30 S_{\text{audit}} \rfloor > 2 g_{\text{tx}} + c_{\text{step}}\cdot\text{fuel}_{\max} + c_{\text{search}}$ of Section~4.3 to $S_{\text{audit}} \ge \mathbf{24\text{ tokens}}$ at $g_{\text{tx}} = 2$ ($g_{\mathcal{T}_6} = 5, B_{\text{finder}} = 7$) or $S_{\text{audit}} \ge \mathbf{44\text{ tokens}}$ at $g_{\text{tx}} = 5$ ($g_{\mathcal{T}_6} = 11, B_{\text{finder}} = 13$) completely restores the S3 publication equilibrium for M4$-$ and M4 (yielding author net $\mathbf{+35\text{ tokens}}$ and $\mathbf{+32\text{ tokens}}$, respectively; S1 publication under M3 holds for $g_{\text{tx}} \le 37$ and M4 publication at $N = 24$ holds for $g_{\text{tx}} \le 107$).
  \item \emph{Attacker Advance Knowledge ($p_{\text{know}} = k / 16,384 \in [0.25, 1.00]$):} When the pre-embedding attacker knows $k$ bits of $D_1$ in advance and encodes the remaining $16,384 - k$ unknown bits raw at $1\text{ bit/bit}$ in the recipe residual ($L_{\text{res}} = 34 + (16,384 - k)$, $|p_P| = k + 16$), the closed-form condition for beating the honest recipe ($\Phi_{\text{local}} = 822\text{ bits}$) under M2 is $24 + 4 + 34 + (16,384 - k) < 822$, which holds if and only if $k \ge \mathbf{15,625\text{ bits}}$ ($p_{\text{know}} \ge \mathbf{95.37\%}$). Under M3/M4$-$/M4, the attacker cannot even win $D_1$ unless $k \ge \mathbf{15,643\text{ bits}}$ ($p_{\text{know}} \ge \mathbf{95.48\%}$), and even above that threshold its best winning payoff remains strictly negative across the entire winning range ($\mathbf{-229\text{ tokens}}$ just above the threshold $k = 15,643$ down to $\mathbf{-238\text{ tokens}}$ at $p_{\text{know}} = 1.00$).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Adaptive No-Regret Learning Dynamics (\texttt{esm\_exp3\_ablation.py --learning}).} To test whether boundedly rational agents without prior equilibrium knowledge converge to the subgame-perfect outcomes above, Experiment~4c evaluates a multi-agent multiplicative-weights (full-information Hedge) learning process across all four strategic roles (author $H$, clone plagiarist $Q$, pre-embedding attacker $P$, and watchtower $W$). Every agent initializes at the uniform mixed strategy over its finite action set and simultaneously updates its weights over $T = 200\text{ episodes}$ (averaged over $20$ fixed random seeds, learning rate $\eta = 0.3$, payoff scale $10\text{ tokens}$) via $w_a \leftarrow w_a \exp(\eta u_a / \text{scale})$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{L1 (Baseline Market Convergence \& Exponential Attack Decay):} Under M2 (free citation exemption), $P$'s pre-embedding probability rises from $0.500\,(t=1)$ to $\mathbf{1.000}$ by episode $t = 25$, degrading realized social welfare to $W_{200} = \mathbf{104,345\text{ tokens}}$. Under M4 (ESM Full), by contrast, $P$'s pre-embedding probability decays monotonically as $0.800\,(t=1) \to 0.1034\,(t=25) \to 0.0120\,(t=50) \to 0.0001\,(t=100) \to \mathbf{0.0000}\,(t=200)$ at a fitted exponential rate of $\mathbf{0.0861\text{/episode}}$ between $t = 25$ and $t = 50$---matching the theoretical rate $\eta \cdot 3 / \text{scale} = 0.0900\text{/episode}$ of the slowest-decaying losing lookup action ($-3\text{ tokens}$). Simultaneously, $Q$'s cloning probability decays as $0.923\,(t=1) \to 0.0032\,(t=25) \to \mathbf{0.0000}\,(t \ge 50)$, and the honest author $H$'s publication probability converges to $\mathbf{1.000}$ (modal action: own burn $50$), achieving the exact SPE welfare $W^* = \mathbf{113,894\text{ tokens}}$ across all tested learning rates $\eta \in \{0.1, 0.3, 1.0\}$.
  \item \emph{L2 (Boundary Reproduction \& Simultaneous-Move Watchtower Stagnation):} For the capital-constrained author at $N = 24$ (budget $12\text{ tokens}$), M4$-$ converges to non-publication ($P(\text{publish}) = 0.000$, $W_{200} = 226,386\text{ tokens}$), whereas M4 (RBF co-financing) converges to $P(\text{publish}) = \mathbf{1.000}$ with modal action $(h = 12, r = 38)$ and full SPE welfare $W_{200} = \mathbf{227,861\text{ tokens}}$. Under the adversarial tie-break S3 ($N = 12$, budget $100$), M3 collapses to $P(\text{publish}) = 0.0000$ ($W_{200} = 113,193\text{ tokens}$), whereas simultaneous-move M4 maintains $P(\text{publish}) = \mathbf{0.9500}$ and realized welfare $W_{200} = \mathbf{113,859\text{ tokens}}$ ($+666\text{ tokens}$ above M3) with a small residual cloning probability of $P(Q\text{ clones}) \approx 0.0498$ ($-35\text{ tokens}$ below the pure SPE $W^* = 113,894$). This residual reflects a classic simultaneous-move dynamic of the Verifier's Dilemma \cite{luu2015}: when $Q$ and $W$ update unconditioned mixed strategies simultaneously, as cloning becomes rare, $W$ rarely collects $B_{\text{finder}}$ and its auditing probability plateaus at $P(W\text{ watches}) \approx 0.6250$. Indeed, evaluating the \emph{sequential (conditional) watchtower} setting (\texttt{conditional\_w} in \texttt{esm\_exp3\_ablation.py}, where $W$ observes the on-chain registry and submits $\mathcal{T}_6$ conditionally whenever a clone is registered to collect the deterministic net gain $B_{\text{finder}} - g_{\mathcal{T}_6} - c_{\text{search}} = +2\text{ tokens} > 0$) confirms empirically that in S3-M4, play reaches $P(H\text{ publishes}) = \mathbf{1.0000}$ and $P(Q\text{ clones}) = \mathbf{0.0000}$ by episode $t = 25$, and converges at $t = 200$ to $P(H\text{ publishes}) = \mathbf{1.0000}$ (modal action $(50, 0)$ with mean probability $1.000$), $P(Q\text{ clones}) = \mathbf{0.0000}$, $P(P\text{ pre-embeds}) = \mathbf{0.0000}$, and realized social welfare $W_{200} = \mathbf{113,894\text{ tokens}} = W^*$ ($\vert{}W_{200} - W^*\vert{} = \mathbf{0.0}$), eliminating the simultaneous-move residual completely.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Scope of the Ablation.} This evaluation combines an exact deterministic subgame-perfect equilibrium model with multi-agent no-regret (Hedge) learning over a stylized environment (identical datasets, an attacker with advance knowledge of $D_1$, no inter-attacker collusion, the cost parameters swept above, and RBF financing fixed prior to attacker moves rather than the post-hoc Regime~2 of Proposition~\ref{prop:trilemma}); it isolates incentive structure, exact deterrence boundaries, and learning convergence across mechanisms rather than measuring live mainnet traffic.

\section{Limitations, Open Problems, and Deployment Roadmap}
\label{sec:limitations}

To uphold rigorous scientific transparency, this section explicitly delineates five structural limitations and open engineering challenges of ESM, together with a pragmatic phased mainnet deployment roadmap:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Clean-Room Re-Implementation with Large Edit Distance:} While $\mathcal{T}_6$ Syntactic Reduction Attribution deterministically catches verbatim copying, variable renaming, regular (periodically unfolding) dead-code padding, and bounded algebraic refactoring whenever an adapter $q$ of length $|q| + \ell_\omega(\text{idx}(v_s)) < |p_i|$ synthesizes $p_i = U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}(q(p_s))$ within $T_{\max} = 2^{20}$ steps, deciding semantic equivalence of arbitrary programs is undecidable by Rice's theorem \cite{li2019}. If a well-capitalized adversary performs a complete clean-room re-implementation $p_i$ using a fundamentally different algorithmic representation whose shortest constructive adapter from $p_s$ satisfies $|q| \approx |p_i|$ (or requires $> 2^{20}$ KAM steps to transform), $\mathcal{T}_6$ yields $L_{\text{red}}(v_i) = 0$. In such cases, the protocol cannot force royalty redirection to $v_s$; however, the adversary is still forced to pay the full upfront self-burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_i)$ under Lemma~\ref{lem:single_data_pre_embed} and cannot extract single-dataset pre-embedding profit. Moreover, the bounded B\"ohm-tree verification of Definition~\ref{def:t6_reduction} is deterministic, always halts, and is \emph{sound} (it never accepts a pair of non-equivalent terms), but, by Rice's theorem, necessarily \emph{incomplete}: the boundary test of Section~\ref{sec:empirical} (Benchmark~4) on the $115\text{-bit}$ unoptimized reference form of $v_s$ verifies a clone that $\eta$-expands the recursive call ($122\text{ bits}$, regular unfolding) as \texttt{True} with $\text{kam} = 10$, $\text{beta} = 18$, $\text{nodes} = 7$, $\text{work} = 2,595$ ($\text{fuel}_{\text{total}} = 2,630 \le \text{fuel}_{\max} \le T_{\max}$), whereas a functionally identical clone ($143\text{ bits}$, identical outputs on all $1,024$ records) that stacks an identity application onto an ignored dummy accumulator argument at every step exhausts the budget $T_{\max} = 1,048,576$ ($\text{kam} = 10$, $\text{beta} = 3,093$, $\text{nodes} = 844$, $\text{work} = 1,044,716$ at the aborting charge) and is safely rejected as \texttt{None} [fuel-exhausted]. Such a clone evades redirection to $v_s$ but must still fund its own self-burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_i)$. The residual exposure is quantified in Section~\ref{sec:ablation}: against a fully burned root a clone is unprofitable, whereas against a root with only $12\text{ tokens}$ of burn a clone that $\mathcal{T}_6$ cannot reduce captures $+58\text{ tokens}$ at $N = 12$ (M3 row of Table~\ref{tab:ablation_dormant}), which is why Extension 3 (RBF sponsorship) complements $\mathcal{T}_6$.
  \item \textbf{Protection Ceiling Proportional to Burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$:} By Theorem~\ref{thm:main_theorem_1}(4) (\leanref{capFold\_general\_bound}), the cumulative bits redirected to a root axiom $v_s$ across all derivatives on dataset $D_k$ is bounded by $\text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_s, D_k) \le \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_s, D_k)$. This bound is algebraically necessary: if a root axiom with zero burn ($C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_s) = 0$) could receive unlimited redirected royalties via a high-burn derivative $v_i$, an attacker could bypass the pre-embedding lockup $N_{\text{lock}}$ by registering a zero-cost root stub and channeling payouts through it. While Extension 3 (RBF Burn Sponsorship) mitigates author capital constraints by enabling third-party sponsors to finance $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_s)$, an axiom that attracts neither author burn nor RBF sponsorship remains bounded by its current $\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_s, D_k)$.
  \item \textbf{1-of-$N$ Honest Watchtower Liveness Assumption:} As established in Section~\ref{sec:extensions} (Extension 1), while errors affecting $f \ge 4.02\%$ (exact: $4.018\%$, $\Lambda=1$) or $f \ge 2.65\%$ (exact: $2.644\%$, $\Lambda=0$) of records are deterred at the commit-reveal stage by VDF spot-checking alone ($K_{\text{spot}}=32$), deterring microscopic targeted corruptions ($f < f_{\text{grind}}(\Lambda)$, i.e., $f < 2.65\%$ at $\Lambda=0$ or $f < 4.02\%$ at $\Lambda=1$) relies on post-adoption $\mathcal{T}_2$ monitoring with watchtower detection probability $q_{\text{detect}} \ge 0.576$ ($\Lambda=0$) or $q_{\text{detect}} \ge 0.731$ ($\Lambda=1$). Network censorship or complete watchtower eclipse during the challenge window would weaken Class III deterrence; similarly, as formalized in Proposition~\ref{prop:trilemma}, if total watchtower absence during the $N_{\text{lock}} = 10,000\text{ block}$ pre-RBF window allowed a plagiarist's clone $v_u$ to pass into Regime~2 unreduced and self-sponsor $D_0 = 2b$ before $\mathcal{T}_6$ was submitted, the identity-free waterfall would service $D_0$ prior to redirecting subsequent perpetuity royalties to $v_s$. Likewise, as demonstrated in the gas-sensitivity and no-regret learning analyses of Section~\ref{sec:ablation}, (i) maintaining positive watchtower $\mathcal{T}_6$ profitability $B_{\text{finder}} > 2 g_{\text{tx}} + c_{\text{step}} \cdot \text{fuel}_{\max} + c_{\text{search}}$ under sustained L2 gas spikes ($g_{\text{tx}} \ge 2\text{ tokens}$) requires scaling the syntactic-audit deposit $S_{\text{audit}}$ above its $20\text{-token}$ baseline (e.g., $S_{\text{audit}} \ge 24\text{ tokens}$ at $g_{\text{tx}} = 2$ and $S_{\text{audit}} \ge 44\text{ tokens}$ at $g_{\text{tx}} = 5$), and (ii) whereas unconditioned simultaneous-move learning exhibits a small residual cloning rate ($P(Q\text{ clones}) \approx 0.0498$ under S3 as watchtower auditing probability plateaus at $\approx 0.6250$ once clones become rare), the sequential (conditional) watchtower experiment (\texttt{conditional\_w} in \texttt{esm\_exp3\_ablation.py}) empirically confirms that on-chain sequential observation eliminates this residual completely, reaching $P(H\text{ publishes}) = 1.0000$ and $P(Q\text{ clones}) = 0.0000$ by $t = 25$ and converging to the exact SPE welfare $W_{200} = 113,894\text{ tokens} = W^*$ ($\vert{}W_{200} - W^*\vert{} = 0.0$) at $t = 200$.
  \item \textbf{Full-Scale STARK Prover Memory and GPU Latency for $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$:} Although verifying a STARK proof on-chain requires only polylogarithmic hash operations \cite{bensasson2018} and our concrete KAM benchmarks in Section~\ref{sec:empirical} (Table~\ref{tab:blc_benchmarks}) confirm that symbolic/bitwise laws execute in $9.0 \times 10^2$ to $7.5 \times 10^3$ steps, generating a worst-case STARK proof for $K_{\text{spot}} = 32$ executions at the hard ceiling $T_{\max} = 2^{20}$ ($3.35 \times 10^7$ KAM transitions per reveal) represents a non-trivial off-chain prover workload. Hardware-Accelerated BLC Jets (Section~\ref{sec:empirical}) substantially compress trace height for numerical kernels, while end-to-end GPU prover latency benchmarks for custom pointer-machine AIR circuits remain an important next engineering milestone.
  \item \textbf{Phased L2 Rollup + External Data Availability (DA) Deployment Roadmap:} Storing raw multi-megabyte datasets $D_k$ directly in L1 smart contract storage is economically prohibitive. The deployment roadmap separates execution from data availability in three phases:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Phase 1 (Testnet \& Auditing):} Deploy the exact integer state transitions verified in \texttt{ESM\_verified.lean} on an optimistic/ZK L2 rollup, using content-addressed blobs on dedicated DA layers (such as EIP-4844 blobs, Celestia, or EigenDA) to anchor $D_k$ and MST commitments at low $\epsilon_{\text{DA}}$ cost.
    \item \textbf{Phase 2 (Custom KAM-STARK Prover Integration):} Integrate a dedicated STARK circuit for $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$ ($\mathcal{T}_1$ spot-checks, $\mathcal{T}_2$ punctures, and $\mathcal{T}_6$ syntactic reductions) with VDF beacon synchronization.
    \item \textbf{Phase 3 (Mainnet Launch with RBF \& Underwriter Liquidity):} Activate the full permissionless knowledge market with RBF burn-sponsorship pools and automated watchtower nodes.
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
This paper has presented the \textbf{Epistemic State Machine (ESM)}, a mechanism for oracle-free Minimum Description Length knowledge discovery and burn-backed citation attribution with mechanized discrete accounting. By unifying bounded Binary Lambda Calculus compression with Burn-Backed Amortization ($\Delta_{\text{amort}}$), Syntactic Reduction Attribution ($\mathcal{T}_6$) under bounded B\"ohm-tree $\beta\eta$-equivalence, a two-regime debt-following reservation waterfall with symmetric puncture release, and VDF-seeded STARK spot-checking, ESM addresses validator weight-copying collusion, single-dataset lookup-table gaming, and foundational axiom plagiarism. We formulated an extensive-form game model establishing dominant-strategy deterrence and an endogenous mixed-strategy watchtower inspection equilibrium, complemented by concrete BLC/KAM compression benchmarks, a multi-dataset cash-flow trajectory analysis, and a five-mechanism ablation under subgame-perfect equilibrium, three-axis sensitivity sweeps, and multi-agent no-regret learning dynamics. The discrete natural-number accounting, escrow allocation, reservation waterfall, and supply conservation model has been mechanized in Lean 4.22.0 (\texttt{Init} core, \texttt{ESM\_verified.lean}, 1,111 lines using only the standard axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}) across \textbf{53 \texttt{sorry}-free lemmas and theorems}, confirming the absence of rounding or underflow bugs across the mechanized accounting equations (while analytical components such as the $\mathcal{T}_5$ dictionary sorting algorithm, the $\mathcal{T}_6$ KAM evaluator, and Kraft's inequality are explicitly delineated), providing a mathematically grounded foundation for decentralized epistemic markets.

\subsection{Specification of the 18 Ledger Transaction Schemas (Formalized via 12 Branch Theorems)}
Lines 399--424 and 913--979 of \texttt{ESM\_verified.lean} formalize the master supply state $\texttt{SupplyState} = (\texttt{wallets}, \texttt{locked}, \texttt{burned}, \texttt{minted})$ and verify preservation of $\texttt{SupplyInv}: \texttt{wallets} + \texttt{locked} + \texttt{burned} = \texttt{minted}$ across all 18 ledger transaction schemas (formalized via 12 branch theorems):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Branch 1 ($\mathcal{T}_0$ Dataset Registration):} Locks initial bounty $B_k^{(0)}$ and anchor collateral $S_k$, and burns $\epsilon_{\text{DA}} \Phi_k^{(0)}$ (\texttt{branch01\_T0\_preserves}).
  \item \textbf{Branch 2 ($\mathcal{T}_1$ Blinded Commit):} Locks creator commit collateral $S_c$ (\texttt{branch02\_commit\_preserves}).
  \item \textbf{Branch 3 ($\mathcal{T}_1$ Batch Winner Adoption \& Amortization Burn):} Locks adoption stake $S^{\text{st}}$ (underwriter co-stake $S^{\text{uw}}$ plus syntactic-audit deposit $S_{\text{audit}}$) and burns $\text{burn\_da} + B_{\text{am}}$ ($\epsilon_{\text{DA}} L_{\text{axiom}} + C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$, \texttt{branch03\_adopt\_preserves}).
  \item \textbf{Branch 4 ($\mathcal{T}_1$ Non-Winning Valid Reveal Refund):} Refunds $S_c$ and $S^{\text{st}}$ in full while burning $\text{burn\_da}$ (\texttt{branch04\_reject\_preserves}).
  \item \textbf{Branch 5 ($\mathcal{T}_1$ Unrevealed Commit Forfeiture):} Refunds $30\%$ of $S_c$ and burns $70\%$ (\texttt{branch05\_unrevealed\_preserves}).
  \item \textbf{Branches 6 \& 7 ($\mathcal{T}_2$ Mode II-A Full Slash \& Mode III Partial Puncture Slash):} Splits slashed collateral between hunter reward $S_{\text{hunter}}$ and permanent burn $S_{\text{burn}}^c$ (\texttt{branch06\_07\_puncture\_preserves}).
  \item \textbf{Branches 8a \& 8b ($\mathcal{T}_3$ Underwriter Absorption Top-Up \& Hold Expiry Release):} Locks top-up collateral $dS_{\text{uw}}$ on absorption (\texttt{branch08a\_uw\_topup\_preserves}) and releases excess collateral $dS_{\text{excess}}$ on hold expiration (\texttt{branch08b\_hold\_expire\_preserves}).
  \item \textbf{Branch 9 ($\mathcal{T}_4$ Tranche Maturity Settlement):} Burns $E_{\text{burn}}$, distributes $E_{\text{net}} = R_{\text{anc}} + R_{\text{guar}} + R_{\text{proposer}}$, and unlocks surviving collateral $S_{\text{unlock}}$ (\texttt{branch09\_settle\_preserves}).
  \item \textbf{Branches 10 \& 11 ($\mathcal{T}_4$ Normal \& Penalized Dataset Closure):} Refunds remaining $B_k^{\text{rem}} + S_k$ (\texttt{branch10\_close\_ok\_preserves}) or refunds $B_k^{\text{rem}} + 30\% S_k$ while burning $70\% S_k$ (\texttt{branch11\_close\_pen\_preserves}).
  \item \textbf{Branches 12a, 12b, 12c, 13, 14, \& 15 (RBF Sponsorship Burn, Dictionary Stake Lock/Unlock, $\mathcal{T}_5$ Epoch Update, $\mathcal{T}_6$ Reduction Settlement, \& Block Reward Minting):} Preserves $\texttt{SupplyInv}$ across all auxiliary staking, burn, fee, and minting transitions (\texttt{branch12\_to\_15\_preserves}).
\end{itemize}
