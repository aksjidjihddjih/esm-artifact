\begin{abstract}
Evaluating and financing the epistemic contribution of scientific models, algorithms, and reusable smart-contract abstractions in decentralized environments faces two obstacles: subjective peer evaluation is vulnerable to validator weight-copying and collusion, whereas objective one-shot compression contests lack composable citation attribution and enable free-riding on foundational theories. We introduce the \textbf{Epistemic State Machine (ESM)}, a deterministic cryptographic, smart-contract, and decentralized finance (DeFi) protocol combining Verifiable Delay Functions, zero-knowledge STARK execution proofs, Counted Merkle Sum Tree commitments, and two-phase commit-reveal schemes to adjudicate Minimum Description Length (MDL) lossless compression over a bounded Binary Lambda Calculus universal machine with deductive citation and syntactic reduction attribution. ESM (i) replaces subjective scoring with a telescoping integer escrow rule governed by strict MDL frontier reductions; (ii) bounds an axiom's creditable exemption by its prior irreversible token burn (\textbf{Burn-Backed Amortization}), which makes single-dataset lookup-table pre-embedding unprofitable; (iii) attributes plagiarized axioms through bounded B\"ohm-tree $\beta\eta$-equivalence ($\mathcal{T}_6$), coupled with a Revenue-Based Financing (RBF) $2.0\times$ debt-following waterfall, an underwriter collateral market, staking/slashing splits, and symmetric puncture release; and (iv) is analyzed as an extensive-form game with dominant-strategy deterrence and an endogenous watchtower equilibrium. All accounting is formulated over $\mathbb{N}$ and mechanized in \textbf{53 \texttt{sorry}-free lemmas and theorems} in \textbf{Lean 4.22.0} (1,111 lines, \texttt{Init} core, 18 ledger transaction schemas, classified into Class I/II/III). It is complemented by an executable reference implementation (a \texttt{no\_std} Rust / RV32IM zkVM guest and EIP-170-compliant Solidity contracts whose proof verifier and randomness beacon are mocks) tested across 6 Foundry test suites ($65{,}176$ differential oracle rows, theorem property tests, and stateful invariant fuzzing), alongside KAM benchmarks, cash-flow trajectories, and a five-mechanism SPE ablation with 3-axis sweeps and Hedge learning at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/esm-artifact-C093}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{Motivation: Verifiable Smart Contracts and DeFi Markets for Knowledge Discovery}
Decentralized financial (DeFi) and verifiable smart-contract mechanisms for pricing, financing, and attributing algorithmic discoveries without centralized committees face three limitations across existing paradigms: (1) \textbf{One-Shot Compression Contests Lack Composable Citation \& DeFi Financing:} Minimum Description Length (MDL) and algorithmic probability \cite{solomonoff1964,kolmogorov1965,rissanen1978,li2019} establish that compression is dual to law discovery, yet benchmarks like the Hutter Prize \cite{hutter2005} evaluate monolithic archives without on-chain DAG citation of prior ``axioms'' or DeFi primitives (RBF sponsorship, underwriter leverage) for capital-constrained authors; (2) \textbf{Subjective Peer-Weight Protocols Suffer from Validator Collusion:} Networks such as Bittensor \cite{rao2021} rely on continuous peer-assigned weights, where copying consensus weights is nearly free compared to ground-truth evaluation, inducing validator weight-copying and subjective capture; and (3) \textbf{Optimistic Single-Output Verifiers Cannot Price Generalization or Plagiarism:} TrueBit \cite{teutsch2019} and STARKs \cite{bensasson2018} verify $y = p(x)$ for a single run, but neither measure cross-dataset compression nor prevent an adversary from wrapping an open-source program $p_s$ as $p_i = q(p_s)$ to siphon downstream royalties.

In ESM, Data Anchors post initial bounties $B_k^{(0)}$ to capture external utility $V_{\text{ext}}(\Delta_k)$ across three exogenous demand markets: (i) \emph{cryptographic and PRNG vulnerability auditing / state-recovery bounties}, where compressing an output stream $D_k$ below $\Phi_k^{(0)}$ constructively proves predictability, whereas non-compression provides market-priced empirical evidence of resistance; (ii) \emph{lossless compression of historical rollup and on-chain oracle trajectories} to reduce permanent Data Availability (DA) storage costs; and (iii) \emph{verifiable symbolic regression of physical and financial simulation trajectories} constrained by conservation-law checkers $V_{\text{inv}}$ (Mode~III).

\subsection{Explicit Definition of ``Oracle-Free'' Adjudication}
\begin{definition}[Oracle-Free Epistemic Adjudication]
\label{def:oracle_free}
In ESM, \textbf{Oracle-Free} does \textbf{not} guarantee the physical authenticity or sensor veracity of external off-chain data prior to registration; rather, once a dataset $D_k$ is cryptographically committed on-chain, compression evaluation against $D_k$, citation and syntactic reduction attribution, and DeFi reward, debt-waterfall, and slashing settlements are adjudicated deterministically by the smart-contract state machine without human reviewers, voting committees, or external off-chain arbiters.
\end{definition}

\section{The ESM Core Model (Exact Integer Specification)}
\label{sec:core_model}

\subsection{Base Universal Machine of BLC, Elias Coding, and Merkle Sum Trees}
All computation in ESM is defined relative to $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$ \cite{tromp2007} on the call-by-name Krivine Abstract Machine (KAM) with hard ceiling $T_{\max} = 2^{20} = 1,048,576$ steps per record (full bit-level rules in Appendix~\ref{app:sec3_4}): (i) each record index $j \in \{0,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak n_k - 1\}$ is passed as a width-$b_k$ ($16\text{-bit}$) LSB-first Scott-encoded bit-list, and the output closure is decoded across its first $b_k$ elements via behavioural bit markers $(\texttt{MK0},\allowbreak \texttt{MK1})$ and end-of-list marker $\texttt{MKEND}$ into $\hat{y}_j \in \{0,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak 2^{b_k} - 1\}$; and (ii) the KAM counts $1\text{ step}$ per application push, $\beta$-pop, and de Bruijn lookup, falling back deterministically to $\hat{y}_j \equiv 0^{b_k}$ and charging the declared ceiling $\delta \in \{1,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak T_{\max}\}$ if evaluation exceeds $\delta$ or yields a non-bit/open term. Arithmetic over $\mathbb{N}$ uses saturating subtraction $a \ominus b \equiv \max(a - b,\allowbreak 0)$, floor division $\lfloor a / b \rfloor$ ($\lfloor a / 0 \rfloor \equiv 0$), and ceiling division and integer logarithm ($b \ge 1$):
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ceildiv}
\text{ceilDiv}(a, b) \equiv \left\lceil \frac{a}{b} \right\rceil \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{a + b - 1}{b} \right\rfloor, \quad
\text{clog2}(x) \equiv \min\big\{k \in \mathbb{N} : 2^k \ge \max(x, 1)\big\}.
\end{equation} For dataset $D_k = (y_0,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak y_{n_k - 1}) \in (\{0,1\}^{b_k})^{n_k}$, residual errors $r_j \equiv y_j \oplus \hat{y}_j$ are committed into a binary \textbf{Counted Merkle Sum Tree (MST)} committing child hashes, subtree residual bit-length sums, and outlier counts $k_{\text{err}}$, enabling $O(\log_2 n_k)$ verification of $L_{\text{res}} = \min(L_{\text{res}}^{\text{sparse}},\allowbreak L_{\text{res}}^{\text{dense}})$ with header $c_{\text{hdr}} = 33\text{ bits}$ (Appendix~\ref{app:sec3_4}).

\subsection{Knowledge DAG: Data Nodes, Axiom Nodes, Recipe Edges, and Escrow Allocation}
ESM maintains a typed DAG $\mathcal{G} = (\mathcal{V}_D \cup \mathcal{V}_A,\allowbreak \mathcal{E})$ of Data Nodes $v_k \in \mathcal{V}_D$ ($\Phi_k^{(0)} \equiv n_k b_k \ge 1$, bounty $B_k^{(0)} \ge 1$, frontier $\Phi_k^{\text{best}}$), Axiom Nodes $v_u \in \mathcal{V}_A$ ($L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) \equiv |p_u| \ge 1$, sequence $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_u)$, index $\text{idx}(v_u)$, origin $\text{origin}(v_u)$, reduction $L_{\text{red}}(v_u)$, burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u)$), and Recipe Edges $e_{k \leftarrow i} \in \mathcal{E}$ citing $\mathcal{C}_i \subseteq \mathcal{V}_A$, governed by transitions $\mathcal{T}_0$ (Dataset registration), $\mathcal{T}_1$ (Commit-reveal adoption), $\mathcal{T}_2$ (Boundary puncture), $\mathcal{T}_3$ (Hold expiration \& absorption), $\mathcal{T}_4$ (Maturity settlement \& closure), $\mathcal{T}_5$ (Epoch dictionary update), and $\mathcal{T}_6$ (Syntactic reduction; full tuple specification in Appendix~\ref{app:sec3_4}).

\begin{definition}[Total Local Description Length and Telescoping Escrow Allocation]
\label{def:escrow_alloc}
For recipe $e_{k \leftarrow i}$ on $D_k$, with wrapper overhead $L_{\text{local}}(e_{k \leftarrow i}) \equiv |w_i| + \ell_{\text{ref}} + \ell_\delta$ ($\ell_\delta \equiv \ell_\omega(\delta) \ge 1$, $\ell_{\text{ref}} = 2$ if $|\mathcal{C}_i| = 0$ else $\ell_\omega(|\mathcal{C}_i| + 1)$, ensuring $\Phi_{\text{local}} \ge 1$, \leanref{local\_desc\_ge\_one}), the effective description length $\Phi_{\text{local}}$ and telescoping escrow allocation for frontier reduction $\Delta \equiv \Phi_k^{\text{best}} - \Phi_{\text{new}} \ge 1$ are:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:phi_local}
\begin{aligned}
\Phi_{\text{local}}(e_{k \leftarrow i}) \equiv\; & L_{\text{local}}(e_{k \leftarrow i}) + L_{\text{res}} + d_{\text{punct}} \\
& + \sum_{v_u \in \mathcal{C}_i} \Big(\big(L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) - \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k)\big) + \mathbf{1}_{[\Delta_{\text{amort}} > 0]} \ell_E(v_u)\Big),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:escrow_alloc}
\text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta) \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{B_k^{(0)} \cdot \Delta}{\Phi_k^{(0)}} \right\rfloor \quad \big(\text{where } \Phi_k^{(0)} \equiv n_k b_k \ge 1\big).
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Monotonicity and Superadditivity of Escrow Allocation]
\label{lem:escrow_props}
\leanref{div\_le\_div\_right, add\_div\_le\_div\_add, escrowAlloc\_mono, escrowAlloc\_superadd}
For all $B_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Phi_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Delta_1,\allowbreak \Delta_2 \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Delta_1 \le \Delta_2 \implies \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Phi_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Delta_1) \le \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Phi_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Delta_2)$, and superadditivity $\text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Phi_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Delta_1) + \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Phi_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Delta_2) \le \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Phi_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Delta_1 + \Delta_2)$ holds unconditionally (Equation~\eqref{eq:superadd} and Proof in Appendix~\ref{app:sec3_4}), eliminating tranche-splitting arbitrage.
\end{lemma}

\subsection{Serial Batch Structure and Epoch Synchronization}
To prevent front-running and intra-batch drift, ESM enforces a two-timescale hierarchy:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:batch_cycle}
N_{\text{batch}} = N_{\text{com}}(20) + N_{\text{delay}}(10) + N_{\text{rev}}(30) = 60, \quad
N_{\text{epoch}} = 9,960 \equiv 166 N_{\text{batch}}.
\end{equation} In Phase 1 ($N_{\text{com}} = 20$), proposers submit salted hashes $h_i$ and creator collateral $S_c \equiv S_{k,1}^{\text{creator}}$, capping Phase 2 gross escrow at $E_{\text{tr}} \le \lfloor 10000 (1 + \Lambda) S_c / \sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}} \rfloor$ ($\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}} = 10000\text{ bps}$, leverage $\Lambda \in \{0,\allowbreak 1\}$). During $N_{\text{delay}} = 10$, a VDF \cite{boneh2018} finalizes the spot-check seed $\rho_{\text{batch}}$. During Phase 2 ($N_{\text{rev}} = 30$), proposers reveal recipes and STARK proofs for the $K_{\text{spot}} = 32$ VDF-selected records. Only the valid reveal with strictly lowest $\Phi_{\text{local}} < \Phi_k^{\text{best}}$ (tie-broken by smaller $\sum_{v_u \in \mathcal{C}_i} t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_u)$, then commit order) is adopted; non-winning valid reveals are refunded $S_c$ in full (\leanref{branch04\_reject\_preserves}), while unrevealed commits forfeit $70\%$ of $S_c$ to burn and receive $30\%$ back (\leanref{branch05\_unrevealed\_preserves}). Because $N_{\text{epoch}} = 166 N_{\text{batch}}$, $\mathcal{T}_5$ updates $\ell_E(v_u)$ and $\phi_u^{\text{dict}}$ strictly between batches.

\noindent\textbf{Implementation scaling.} In the contract implementation (\texttt{params.toml}), the batch, epoch and lock windows are scaled by $5\times$ while the ratio $N_{\text{epoch}} = 166 N_{\text{batch}}$ is preserved ($N_{\text{batch}} = 300$, $N_{\text{epoch}} = 49,800$, $N_{\text{lock}} = 50,000$ blocks), in order to leave time for off-chain ZK proof generation; the equations of this paper are stated for the unscaled windows of Equation~\eqref{eq:batch_cycle}.

\section{Burn-Backed Amortization \& Syntactic Reduction Attribution}
\label{sec:burn_and_reduction}

\subsection{Burn-Backed Amortization and Single-Dataset Pre-Embedding Bound}
To prevent an adversary from hardcoding a lookup table for $D_k$ inside $v_u$ and citing it via $\ell_E(v_u)$ to drain $B_k^{(0)}$, ESM requires that an axiom's creditable bit exemption $\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u,\allowbreak D_k)$ be backed by irreversible burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u,\allowbreak < t_k)$ committed at least $N_{\text{lock}} = 10,000\text{ blocks}$ before $D_k$'s registration block $t_k$.

\begin{definition}[Burn-Backed Amortization and Net Citation Savings]
\label{def:burn_amort}
Given settlement burn rate $\epsilon_{\text{bps}} < 10000$ (baseline $500\text{ bps}$) and $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{blk}}(v_u) \le t_k - N_{\text{lock}}$, the amortized description length $\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u,\allowbreak D_k)$ and net epoch savings $\text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_u,\allowbreak D_k) \equiv \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u,\allowbreak D_k) \ominus \ell_E(v_u)$ on $D_k$ ($B_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Phi_k^{(0)} \ge 1$) are defined by:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:delta_amort}
\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u, D_k) \equiv \min\left(L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u), \; \left\lfloor \frac{10000 \cdot C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u, < t_k) \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)}}{(10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}}) \cdot B_k^{(0)}} \right\rfloor\right).
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

Throughout the protocol, basis-point parameters are fixed at baseline settlement burn rate $\epsilon_{\text{bps}} = 500$ ($5\%$, leaving net settlement rate $\text{net\_bps} \equiv 10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}} = 9500\text{ bps}$), ancestor citation royalty rate $\gamma_{\text{bps}} = 2000$ ($20\%$), data-anchor royalty rebate rate $\beta_{D,\text{bps}} = 1000$ ($10\%$), slash burn share $\kappa_{\text{bps}} = 7000$ ($70\%$ burned, $30\%$ to the reporting watchtower), and per-bit Data Availability registration burn rate $\epsilon_{\text{DA}}$ ($\lceil 10^{-4}\text{ tok/bit} \rceil$).

\begin{lemma}[Unconditional Unprofitability of Single-Dataset Pre-Embedding]
\label{lem:single_data_pre_embed}
\leanref{single\_data\_pre\_embedding\_unprofitable}
For any dataset $D_k$ ($\Phi_k^{(0)} > 0$), axiom $v_u$, $\ell_E(v_u) \in \mathbb{N}$, and $\Delta_{\text{saved}} \equiv \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u,\allowbreak D_k) \ominus \ell_E(v_u)$ with $\text{net\_bps} \equiv 10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}}$, the maximum net reward increment obtainable on $D_k$ from $\Delta_{\text{saved}}$ satisfies (Proof in Appendix~\ref{app:sec3_4}):
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:delta_r_net}
\Delta R_{\text{net}} \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{\text{net\_bps} \cdot \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_{\text{saved}})}{10000} \right\rfloor \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u, < t_k).
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{corollary}[Multi-Axiom Split Pre-Embedding Bound]
\label{cor:multi_axiom_pre_embed}
Partitioning a lookup table for $D_k$ across $m \ge 1$ axioms $v_{u_1},\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak v_{u_m} \in \mathcal{C}_i$ with exemptions $\Delta_{\text{saved},j}$ satisfies $\Delta R_{\text{net}}(\sum_{j=1}^m \Delta_{\text{saved},j}) \le \sum_{j=1}^m C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_{u_j},\allowbreak < t_k)$ while increasing pointer and gas overhead (Proof and Equation~\eqref{eq:multi_pre_embed} in Appendix~\ref{app:sec3_4}).
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Canonical Shannon Dictionary Code Length Over Natural Numbers}
\begin{definition}[Stake-Capped Canonical Shannon Dictionary Code Length]
\label{def:shannon_dict}
For each axiom $v_u \in \mathcal{V}_A^{(E)}$ at epoch $E$, with continuously locked creator and guarantor dictionary stakes $\overline{S}_{u,E}^{\text{dict}} \equiv \overline{S}_{u,E}^{\text{dict,c}} + \sum_{s} \overline{S}_{u,s,E}^{\text{dict,st}}$, cap ratio $\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{dict}} \ge 1$ ($10000\text{ bps}$), and smoothing floor $\epsilon_0 \ge 1$, the stake-capped weight $W_E(v_u)$ and integer Shannon code length $\ell_E(v_u)$ (satisfying Kraft's inequality $\sum_{v_u} 2^{-\ell_E(v_u)} < 1$, Appendix~\ref{app:sec3_4}) are:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:weight_cap}
\overline{S}_{u,E}^{\text{dict}} \equiv \overline{S}_{u,E}^{\text{dict,c}} + \sum_{s} \overline{S}_{u,s,E}^{\text{dict,st}}, \;
W_E(v_u) \equiv \min\left(W(v_u), \left\lfloor \frac{10000 \cdot \overline{S}_{u,E}^{\text{dict}}}{\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{dict}}} \right\rfloor\right),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:shannon_len}
\ell_E(v_u) \equiv \min\left\{k \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1} : 2^k \big(W_E(v_u) + \epsilon_0\big) \ge \sum_{v_m \in \mathcal{V}_A^{(E)}} \big(W_E(v_m) + \epsilon_0\big) + \epsilon_0\right\}.
\end{equation}
\end{definition}
Here, the raw citation weight $W(v_u) \in \mathbb{N}$ is initialized to $W(v_u) \leftarrow 0$ upon axiom registration, incremented by the attributed citation bits $a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ (or redirected bits $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$) whenever a tranche settles, and deterministically updated at each epoch boundary ($\mathcal{T}_5$) with decay rate $\lambda_{\text{decay,bps}} \in (0,\allowbreak 10000]$ (baseline $9000\text{ bps}$) via $W(v_u) \leftarrow \lfloor \lambda_{\text{decay,bps}} W(v_u) / 10000 \rfloor$.

\subsection{Syntactic Reduction Transition (\texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{T}_6$}{T6})}
\begin{definition}[Syntactic Reduction Transition $\mathcal{T}_6$ Acceptance Conditions]
\label{def:t6_reduction}
A transaction $\mathcal{T}_6(v_i,\allowbreak v_s,\allowbreak q,\allowbreak \text{fuel}_{\max})$ claiming that axiom $v_i$ ($p_i$) reduces to an earlier axiom $v_s$ ($p_s$) via adapter $q \in \{0,1\}^*$ within declared verification fuel ceiling $\text{fuel}_{\max} \in \{1,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak T_{\max}\}$ is accepted if and only if:
(1) \textbf{Strict Temporal Precedence \& Root Condition:} $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_s) < t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_i)$ and $\text{origin}(v_s) = v_s$ (flat depth-1 attribution forest); (2) \textbf{Bounded Constructive Synthesis (B\"ohm-Tree $\beta\eta$-Equivalence):} evaluating $\text{App}(q,\allowbreak p_s)$ to WHNF on $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$ ($\text{kam}$ steps) and comparing with $p_i$ via stack-based coinductive normal-order B\"ohm-tree comparison ($\text{beta}$ HNF steps, $\text{nodes}$ pair comparisons, $\text{work}$ AST nodes touched) within $\text{fuel}_{\text{total}} = \text{kam} + \text{beta} + \text{nodes} + \text{work} \le \text{fuel}_{\max} \le T_{\max} = 2^{20}$ establishes $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}(q(p_s)) =_{\beta\eta} p_i$ (Equation~\eqref{eq:blc_synthesis}); and (3) \textbf{Syntactic Reduction Length \& Pointer Overwrite Rule:} with candidate reduction length $L_{\text{red}}^{\text{new}} \equiv \vert{}p_i\vert{} \ominus \big(\vert{}q\vert{} + \ell_\omega(\text{idx}(v_s))\big)$, either \textbf{Case A} holds ($\text{origin}(\text{origin}(v_i)) = \text{origin}(v_i)$ and $L_{\text{red}}^{\text{new}} > L_{\text{red}}(v_i)$, or equality $\ge 1$ with earlier root precedence), or \textbf{Case B} holds ($\text{origin}(\text{origin}(v_i)) \neq \text{origin}(v_i)$ orphaned non-root recovery and $L_{\text{red}}^{\text{new}} \ge 1$).
Upon acceptance via commit-reveal ($N_{\text{rev,T6}} = 30\text{ blocks}$, gas $g_{\text{T6}} = 2 g_{\text{tx}} + c_{\text{step}} \cdot \text{fuel}_{\max}$), $\text{origin}(v_i) \leftarrow v_s$ and $L_{\text{red}}(v_i) \leftarrow L_{\text{red}}^{\text{new}}$ update in $O(1)$ writes, and $v_i$'s audit deposit $S_{\text{audit}} = 20\text{ tok}$ pays bounty $B_{\text{finder}} \equiv \lfloor (10000 - \kappa_{\text{bps}}) S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tok}$ to the watchtower and burns $14\text{ tok}$. Under the \textbf{Sybil-Resistant Two-Regime Rule} (\leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}; Appendix~\ref{app:sec3_4}), RBF sponsorship opens only after $v_u$ survives $N_{\text{lock}}$ unreduced (Regime 1), while post-lockup reductions service $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u)$ then $D_{\text{tot}}(v_s)$ via the Universal Debt-Following Waterfall (Regime 2).
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Obfuscation-Compression Duality and Plagiarist Self-Citation Penalty]
\label{prop:obfuscation_duality}
Inflating $|p_i| = |p_s| + K$ ($K \ge 1$) to evade $T_{\max}$-step B\"ohm-tree reduction penalizes unamortized $\Phi_{\text{local}}$ by $+K$ bits or requires extra burn $\Delta C_{\text{amort\_burn}} = \lceil (10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}}) B_k^{(0)} K / (10000 \Phi_k^{(0)}) \rceil + \epsilon_{\text{DA}} K$; once reduced with redirected fraction $\rho \equiv x_{i \to s}^{\text{tr}} / a_{i \to i}^{\text{tr}}$ and citation share $f_{\text{cite}} \equiv a_{i \to i}^{\text{tr}} / \Delta_{\text{tr}}$, self-citation requires a recovery multiplier of $\big(1 - (\gamma_{\text{bps}}/10000)\rho f_{\text{cite}}\big)^{-1} \in (1,\allowbreak 1.25]$ (Proof in Appendix~\ref{app:sec3_4}).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Identity-Free Financing--Attribution Trilemma \& Pre-Lockup Equilibrium Resolution]
\label{prop:trilemma}
\textbf{[Class III Structural Trilemma]} When $v_u$ survives $N_{\text{lock}}$, incurs RBF debt $D_0 = M_{\text{cap}} b$ ($M_{\text{cap}} > 1$, $b > 0$), and is reduced to $v_s$ as $\rho \to 1$, no ex-post rule on $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ can simultaneously satisfy \textbf{(P1) Identity-Freeness}, \textbf{(P2) Sybil-Proof Sponsor Covenant} (up to $M_{\text{cap}} b$ paid under self-reduction), and \textbf{(P3) Post-Lockup Self-Sponsor Premium Elimination} ($\le 1.0b$ extracted by a Sybil self-sponsored clone). ESM resolves this via Regime~1 pre-lockup watchtower deterrence ($B_{\text{finder}} = 6 > c_{\text{search}} + g_{\text{T6}} \implies D_{\text{tot}}(v_u) = 0$ in SPE) and Regime~2 Universal Debt-Following Waterfall enforcing (P1)--(P2) (\leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}; Proof in Appendix~\ref{app:sec3_4}).
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Two-Stage Upfront Attribution at Tranche Creation \& \texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{T}_4$}{T4} Royalty Waterfall}
\begin{definition}[Two-Stage Upfront Tranche Attribution]
\label{def:two_stage_attrib}
When recipe $e_{k \leftarrow i}$ citing $\mathcal{C}_i$ creates a tranche with reduction $\Delta_{\text{tr}} \ge 1$ on $D_k$ in epoch $E$:
(1) \textbf{Stage 1 (Direct Marginal Reservation, \leanref{stage1\_single\_bounds, stage1\_list\_sum\_le\_delta}):} with $\text{Saved}_{i \to u}^{\text{marg}}(D_k) \equiv \text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_u,\allowbreak D_k) \ominus S_{\text{paid}}(v_u,\allowbreak D_k)$ and $\Sigma_{\text{marg}} \equiv \sum_{v_u \in \mathcal{C}_i} \text{Saved}_{i \to u}^{\text{marg}}(D_k)$, reserve $a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}} \equiv \lfloor \text{Saved}_{i \to u}^{\text{marg}}(D_k) \cdot \min(\Sigma_{\text{marg}},\allowbreak \Delta_{\text{tr}}) / \Sigma_{\text{marg}} \rfloor$ ($0$ if $\Sigma_{\text{marg}}=0$) and update $S_{\text{paid}}(v_u,\allowbreak D_k) \mathrel{+}= a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}}$; and (2) \textbf{Stage 2 (Shared Sequential Residual Cap Redirect, \leanref{capStep\_conserves, capFold\_conserves, capFold\_general\_bound}):} independent axioms retain $x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}} \equiv a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}}$, while for each valid root $v_s = \text{origin}(v_u) \neq v_u$ ($\text{origin}(v_s) = v_s$), ESM initializes $R_{\text{cap}}(v_s) \leftarrow \text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_s,\allowbreak D_k) \ominus S_{\text{paid}}(v_s,\allowbreak D_k)$ and iterates over its cited derivatives $\{v_{u_1},\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak v_{u_m}\}$ in ascending $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}$ order, updating $R_{\text{cap}}(v_s) \mathrel{-}= x_{u_j \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ and $S_{\text{paid}}(v_s,\allowbreak D_k) \mathrel{+}= x_{u_j \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ with:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage2_redirect}
x_{u_j \to s}^{\text{tr}} \equiv \min\big(a_{i \to u_j}^{\text{tr}}, \min(L_{\text{red}}(v_{u_j}), R_{\text{cap}}(v_s))\big), \;
x_{u_j \to u_j}^{\text{tr}} \equiv a_{i \to u_j}^{\text{tr}} - x_{u_j \to s}^{\text{tr}}.
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

At $\mathcal{T}_4$ maturity settlement of $E_{\text{tr}}$ ($\Delta_{\text{tr}} > 0$), burn $E_{\text{burn}} \equiv \lfloor \epsilon_{\text{bps}} E_{\text{tr}} / 10000 \rfloor$ leaves $E_{\text{net}} \equiv E_{\text{tr}} - E_{\text{burn}}$. Each slot $x \in \{x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}},\allowbreak x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}\}$ on $v_m$ receives gross royalty $R(x) \equiv \lfloor \gamma_{\text{bps}} E_{\text{net}} x / (10000 \Delta_{\text{tr}}) \rfloor$ (Equation~\eqref{eq:slot_royalty}), split across four tiers (\leanref{slot\_four\_way\_split\_conserves}; Equation~\eqref{eq:rbf_paid} in Appendix~\ref{app:sec3_4}): (1) anchor rebate $R^{\text{anchor}}(x) \equiv \lfloor \beta_{D,\text{bps}} R(x) / 10000 \rfloor$; (2) RBF priority repayment $R_{\text{repay}}(x) \equiv \min(R^{\text{net}}(x),\allowbreak D_{\text{tot}}(v_m))$ with pro-rata $\text{paid}_s(x)$ (repaying $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u)$ then $D_{\text{tot}}(v_s)$ on $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$); (3) dictionary dividends $\text{div}_s(x)$ on $R^{\text{rem}}(x) \equiv R^{\text{net}}(x) - R_{\text{repay}}(x)$; and (4) owner residual $R^{\text{owner}}(x) \equiv R^{\text{rem}}(x) - \sum_s \text{div}_s(x)$. With $R_{\text{anc}} \equiv \sum_x R(x)$, $R_{\text{rem}} \equiv E_{\text{net}} - R_{\text{anc}}$ splits into underwriter $R_{\text{guar}} \equiv \lfloor \phi_{i,\text{bps}}^{\text{rec,eff}} R_{\text{rem}} / 10000 \rfloor$ and proposer $R_{\text{proposer}} \equiv R_{\text{rem}} - R_{\text{guar}}$.

\begin{remark}[Key Structural Invariant of $\mathcal{T}_4$ Settlement (\texttt{settle\_general\_list\_conserves} \& \texttt{slot\_four\_way\_split\_conserves})]
Every integer division rounding dust in the slot waterfall remains inside $R^{\text{owner}}(x)$, and every dust across ancestor slots remains inside $R_{\text{proposer}}$, conserving $R^{\text{anchor}}(x) + \sum_s \text{paid}_s(x) + \sum_s \text{div}_s(x) + R^{\text{owner}}(x) \equiv R(x)$ and $E_{\text{burn}} + R_{\text{anc}} + R_{\text{guar}} + R_{\text{proposer}} \equiv E_{\text{tr}}$.
\end{remark}

\section{Limitations, Open Problems, and Deployment Roadmap}
\label{sec:limitations}
We delineate five structural limitations (full text in Appendix~\ref{app:sec8_ext9}): (1) \textbf{Clean-room re-implementation:} $\mathcal{T}_6$ is sound but necessarily incomplete (Rice's theorem \cite{li2019}); an $\eta$-expanded recursive clone verifies within $\text{fuel}_{\text{total}} = 2,630$, whereas an aperiodic dummy-accumulator clone exhausts $T_{\max}$ and evades redirection, yet must still fund its own burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_i)$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:single_data_pre_embed}); (2) \textbf{Protection ceiling} proportional to the burn, $\text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_s,\allowbreak D_k) \le \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_s,\allowbreak D_k)$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:main_theorem_1}(4)), mitigated by RBF sponsorship; (3) \textbf{1-of-$N$ watchtower liveness} for corruptions below $2.65\%$ ($\Lambda = 0$) or $4.02\%$ ($\Lambda = 1$) and for pre-RBF clone passage, supported by gas-indexed $S_{\text{audit}}$ ($\ge 24$ tok at $g_{\text{tx}} = 2$, $\ge 44$ tok at $g_{\text{tx}} = 5$) and sequential on-chain observation; (4) \textbf{STARK prover memory and latency} at $T_{\max} = 2^{20}$; and (5) a \textbf{phased L2 roadmap}: Phase~1 deploys the reference contracts with external DA, Phase~2 replaces the mocked verifier and beacon by a KAM-STARK verifier and a VDF/drand beacon and integrates the zkVM guest, Phase~3 launches RBF pools and watchtowers.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This paper has presented the \textbf{Epistemic State Machine (ESM)}, a verifiable smart-contract and DeFi mechanism for oracle-free Minimum Description Length knowledge discovery and burn-backed citation attribution with mechanized discrete accounting. By unifying bounded Binary Lambda Calculus compression with Burn-Backed Amortization ($\Delta_{\text{amort}}$), Syntactic Reduction Attribution ($\mathcal{T}_6$) under bounded B\"ohm-tree $\beta\eta$-equivalence, a two-regime $2.0\times$ debt-following RBF reservation waterfall with symmetric puncture release, and VDF-seeded STARK spot-checking, ESM addresses validator weight-copying collusion, single-dataset lookup-table gaming, and foundational axiom plagiarism. Our extensive-form game analysis, BLC/KAM compression benchmarks, an executable reference implementation (RV32IM zkVM guest and EIP-170 Solidity contracts with a mocked verifier and beacon) tested by 6 Foundry test suites (covering property tests across all mechanized theorems, 22 differential tests over $65{,}176$ oracle rows, 8 paper-table tests, 4 range/overflow tests, full-lifecycle integration tests, and handler-based stateful invariant fuzzing), cash-flow trajectory simulations, five-mechanism SPE ablation, three-axis sensitivity sweeps, and multi-agent no-regret learning experiments---together with \textbf{53 \texttt{sorry}-free lemmas and theorems} mechanized in Lean 4.22.0 (\texttt{Init} core, \texttt{ESM\_verified.lean}, 1,111 lines)---provide a mathematically and empirically grounded foundation for decentralized epistemic markets.

\subsection{Canonical BLC/KAM Execution, Behavioural Bit Decoding, and Counted MST Encoding (Section 3.1)}
All computation in ESM is defined relative to a canonical, resource-bounded Binary Lambda Calculus universal machine $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$ \cite{tromp2007} evaluated on the deterministic Krivine Abstract Machine (KAM) with a hard step ceiling of $T_{\max} = 2^{20} = 1,048,576$ reduction steps per record invocation. Every program $p \in \{0,1\}^*$ is a self-delimiting binary encoding of a closed $\lambda$-term. To ensure deterministic consensus across implementations, ESM specifies a canonical execution and I/O semantics: (i) \textbf{Scott-Encoded LSB-First Bit-List I/O:} each record index $j \in \{0,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak n_k - 1\}$ is passed as a width-$b_k$ (baseline $16\text{ bits}$) LSB-first Scott-encoded bit-list ($\text{B0} = \lambda a.\lambda b.\,\allowbreak a$, $\text{B1} = \lambda a.\lambda b.\,\allowbreak b$, $\text{nil} = \text{B1}$, $\text{cons } h\,\allowbreak t = \lambda c.\lambda n.\,\allowbreak c\,\allowbreak h\,\allowbreak t$), and the output closure is lazily decoded bit-by-bit (\texttt{decode\_bits}) across its first $b_k$ elements to reconstruct the predicted integer $\hat{y}_j \in \{0,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak 2^{b_k} - 1\}$ (specifically, the decoder applies each list element $b$ to two opaque markers $(\texttt{MK0},\allowbreak \texttt{MK1})$ and classifies it as bit $0$ if it reduces to $\texttt{MK0}$ or bit $1$ if it reduces to $\texttt{MK1}$, while passing an opaque end-of-list marker $\texttt{MKEND}$ as the list's \texttt{nil} argument so that any output list terminating in fewer than $b_k$ elements or yielding a non-marker term is strictly detected as a non-bit output); and (ii) \textbf{KAM Step Accounting \& Totalized Fallback:} the call-by-name Krivine Abstract Machine (KAM) counts $1\text{ step}$ for each application push (\texttt{App}), $\beta$-reduction pop (\texttt{Lam}), and de Bruijn environment lookup (\texttt{Var(idx)}), summing the WHNF reduction steps and the $b_k$-bit decoding steps as the per-record step count ($1 \le \delta \le T_{\max}$). If evaluation exceeds the proposer's declared per-record step ceiling $\delta \le T_{\max}$ ($\text{steps} > \delta$), encounters an open/unparseable term, or reaches a non-bit output, the evaluator deterministically falls back to $\hat{y}_j \equiv 0^{b_k}$ and charges the fallback record the full declared step ceiling $\delta$ (rendering the evaluation function total over all binary strings, while any mismatch against the committed MST leaf is deterministically rejected or slashed under $\mathcal{T}_1 / \mathcal{T}_2$).


In Phase 1 ($N_{\text{com}} = 20$), proposers submit salted hashes $h_i = \text{Hash}(\text{proposer\_id} \parallel D_k \parallel \text{root}_{\text{MST}} \parallel p_{\text{rec}} \parallel \text{salt})$ and creator collateral $S_c \equiv S_{k,1}^{\text{creator}}$, capping claimable Phase 2 gross escrow at $E_{\text{tr}} \le \lfloor 10000 (1 + \Lambda) S_c / \sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}} \rfloor$ ($\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}} = 10000\text{ bps}$, underwriter leverage $\Lambda \in \{0,\allowbreak 1\}$). During $N_{\text{delay}} = 10$, a VDF \cite{boneh2018} finalizes $\rho_{\text{batch}}$. During Phase 2 ($N_{\text{rev}} = 30$), proposers reveal recipes and STARK proofs for the $K_{\text{spot}} = 32$ VDF-selected records.
In the knowledge DAG $\mathcal{G} = (\mathcal{V}_D \cup \mathcal{V}_A,\allowbreak \mathcal{E})$, $\mathcal{T}_0$ registers dataset $v_k \in \mathcal{V}_D$ ($n_k$ records of $b_k$ bits, $\Phi_k^{(0)} \equiv n_k b_k \ge 1$, initial bounty $B_k^{(0)} \ge 1$, frontier $\Phi_k^{\text{best}} \leftarrow \Phi_k^{(0)}$, optional Mode~III checkers $\Omega_k,\allowbreak V_{\text{inv}}$); $\mathcal{T}_1$ commits/reveals and adopts axiom $v_u \in \mathcal{V}_A$ ($L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) \equiv |p_u| \ge 1$, sequence $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_u)$, reveal index $\text{idx}(v_u)$, block $t_{\text{com}}^{\text{blk}}(v_u)$, origin $\text{origin}(v_u) \leftarrow v_u$, reduction $L_{\text{red}}(v_u) \leftarrow 0$, burn ledger $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u)$) or recipe edge $e_{k \leftarrow i} \in \mathcal{E}$ citing $\mathcal{C}_i \subseteq \mathcal{V}_A$; $\mathcal{T}_2$ executes post-adoption boundary puncture; $\mathcal{T}_3$ executes priority claim hold expiration and underwriter absorption; $\mathcal{T}_4$ settles mature tranches and closes datasets; $\mathcal{T}_5$ executes epoch weight decay, Shannon dictionary updates, and delayed $\phi_u^{\text{dict}}$ activation; and $\mathcal{T}_6$ executes syntactic reduction attribution.

In Equation~\eqref{eq:ceildiv}, $\text{clog2}(x)$ provides the exact integer definition of both the binary Merkle tree depth $\text{clog2}(n_k)$ and the integer floor logarithm $\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor \equiv \text{clog2}(n + 1) - 1$ for $n \ge 1$. Positive integers $n \ge 1$ (including execution step counts $\delta \ge 1$ and the sequential registration index $\text{idx}(v_s) \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$ assigned to each axiom $v_s$ in order of reveal adoption) are encoded via Elias $\omega$ coding \cite{elias1975} with recursive bit-length $\ell_\omega(1) = 1$ and $\ell_\omega(n) = 1 + \lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor + \ell_\omega(\lfloor \log_2 n \rfloor)$ for $n \ge 2$. When a recipe predicts $\hat{y}_j = U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}(p_{\text{rec}}(j))$ for record index $j \in \{0,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak n_k - 1\}$ of dataset $D_k = (y_0,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak y_{n_k - 1}) \in (\{0,1\}^{b_k})^{n_k}$, the residual error $r_j \equiv y_j \oplus \hat{y}_j$ (mapped bijectively to $\mathbb{N}_{\ge 0}$ via unsigned integer interpretation or ZigZag encoding with $\text{biject}(0) = 0$, so that $\text{biject}(r_j) + 1 \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$) is committed into a binary \textbf{Merkle Sum Tree (MST)}. Here, $p_{\text{rec}}$ denotes the composite recipe evaluation term binding the cited axioms $\mathcal{C}_i$ and the local wrapper/witness parameters $w_i$ (and the general formulation incorporating an explicit validity domain specification $\Omega_{k,i}$ or non-zero tolerance thresholds reduces identically to the same MST residual bit-length sum $L_{\text{res}}$). Each internal node of the MST commits both the cryptographic hash of its children and the exact integer sum of their residual bit-lengths, making the total residual bit-length $L_{\text{res}} = \sum_{j=0}^{n_k-1} \ell_{\text{res}}(r_j)$ verifiable at the root and enabling $O(\log_2 n_k)$ range-sum proofs during spot-checking and boundary punctures. Specifically, for a dataset with $k_{\text{err}} \in \{0,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak n_k\}$ non-zero residual leaves at sorted record indices $0 \le j_1 < j_2 < \dots < j_{k_{\text{err}}} < n_k$, the sparse MST root encoding defines the total residual description length as $L_{\text{res}} \equiv \ell_\omega(k_{\text{err}} + 1) + c_{\text{hdr}} + \sum_{m=1}^{k_{\text{err}}} \big(\ell_\omega(\Delta j_m) + b_k\big)$, where $\ell_\omega(k_{\text{err}} + 1)$ is the Elias $\omega$ outlier-count header ($1\text{ bit}$ when $k_{\text{err}} = 0$), $c_{\text{hdr}} = 33\text{ bits}$ is the fixed metadata header ($1\text{ bit}$ sparse/dense mode flag $+ 16\text{ bits}$ tree-depth and record-count framing $+ 16\text{ bits}$ residual bit-width descriptor $= 33\text{ bits}$, yielding $L_{\text{res}} = 1 + 33 = 34\text{ bits}$ when $k_{\text{err}} = 0$), $\Delta j_m \ge 1$ ($\Delta j_1 \equiv j_1 + 1$ and $\Delta j_m \equiv j_m - j_{m-1}$ for $m \ge 2$) is the index gap encoded via Elias $\omega$, and $b_k$ is the residual amplitude width. Furthermore, because each internal MST node also commits the exact integer count of non-zero residual leaves (outlier count) in its subtree as a \textbf{Counted Merkle Sum Tree}, the index gap $\Delta j_m$ to the preceding non-zero leaf is deterministically verifiable in $O(\log_2 n_k)$ Merkle path steps during Mode~II-A and Mode~III single-leaf verification. Conversely, in the dense mode ($1\text{ bit}$ mode flag $= 1$, used when outliers are frequent), index-gap codes are omitted and all $n_k$ residuals are encoded sequentially as $L_{\text{res}}^{\text{dense}} \equiv c_{\text{hdr}} + \sum_{j=0}^{n_k-1} \ell_\omega(\text{biject}(r_j) + 1)$, allowing each recipe to select the mode achieving $L_{\text{res}} = \min(L_{\text{res}}^{\text{sparse}},\allowbreak L_{\text{res}}^{\text{dense}})$.

\subsection{Proofs of Lemma~\ref{lem:escrow_props}, Lemma~\ref{lem:single_data_pre_embed}, Corollary~\ref{cor:multi_axiom_pre_embed}, and Kraft's Inequality}

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:superadd}
\begin{aligned}
\text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_1) &+ \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_2) \\
&\le \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Delta_1 + \Delta_2).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

\begin{proof}[of Lemma~\ref{lem:escrow_props}]
By integer division properties, $\lfloor x / c \rfloor \cdot c \le x$ and $\lfloor y / c \rfloor \cdot c \le y$. Summing both yields $(\lfloor x/c \rfloor + \lfloor y/c \rfloor) \cdot c \le x + y$, which implies $\lfloor x/c \rfloor + \lfloor y/c \rfloor \le \lfloor (x+y)/c \rfloor$ (\texttt{add\_div\_le\_div\_add}). Substituting $x = B_k^{(0)} \Delta_1$, $y = B_k^{(0)} \Delta_2$, and $c = \Phi_k^{(0)}$ with distributivity $B_k^{(0)}\Delta_1 + B_k^{(0)}\Delta_2 = B_k^{(0)}(\Delta_1 + \Delta_2)$ immediately yields Equation~\eqref{eq:superadd}. Specifically, superadditivity guarantees that splitting a compression improvement $\Delta_1 + \Delta_2$ across multiple sequential recipes never extracts more from $B_k^{(0)}$ than a single combined jump, completely eliminating tranche-splitting arbitrage.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[of Lemma~\ref{lem:single_data_pre_embed}]
By definition of saturating subtraction and minimum, $\Delta_{\text{saved}} \le \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u,\allowbreak D_k) \le \lfloor 10000 \cdot C_{\text{amort\_burn}} \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)} / (\text{net\_bps} \cdot B_k^{(0)}) \rfloor$. Multiplying by $\text{net\_bps} \cdot B_k^{(0)}$ and applying $\lfloor X / Y \rfloor \cdot Y \le X$ yields $\Delta_{\text{saved}} \cdot \text{net\_bps} \cdot B_k^{(0)} \le 10000 \cdot C_{\text{amort\_burn}} \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)}$. Since $\text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Phi_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Delta_{\text{saved}}) \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)} \le B_k^{(0)} \cdot \Delta_{\text{saved}}$, multiplying by $\text{net\_bps}$ and dividing by $\Phi_k^{(0)} > 0$ via \texttt{div\_le\_div\_right} gives $\text{net\_bps} \cdot \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Phi_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Delta_{\text{saved}}) \le 10000 \cdot C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$. Dividing both sides by $10000$ yields $\Delta R_{\text{net}} \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$. Note also that the difference between $\Delta R_{\text{net}}$ in Equation~\eqref{eq:delta_r_net} and the ceiling $\mathcal{T}_4$ net settlement payout $E_{\text{net}} \equiv E_{\text{tr}} - \lfloor \epsilon_{\text{bps}} E_{\text{tr}} / 10000 \rfloor \le \Delta R_{\text{net}} + 1 \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_u,\allowbreak < t_k) + 1$ (for $E_{\text{tr}} = \text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Phi_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Delta_{\text{saved}})$) is at most $1\text{ token unit}$ due to integer floor rounding. Consequently, whenever mandatory Data Availability (DA) registration burn and transaction gas satisfy $\epsilon_{\text{DA}} L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) + g_{\text{tx}} \ge 2\text{ token units}$ (plus capital opportunity cost over $N_{\text{lock}} = 10,000\text{ blocks}$), the net profit of pre-embedding data for any single dataset $D_k$ is unconditionally strictly negative ($< 0$) under both floor and ceiling settlement forms.
\end{proof}


\begin{equation}
\label{eq:multi_pre_embed}
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R_{\text{net}}(\Sigma_\Delta) &\equiv \left\lfloor \frac{(10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}}) \cdot \text{EscrowAlloc}\big(B_k^{(0)}, \Phi_k^{(0)}, \Sigma_\Delta\big)}{10000} \right\rfloor \\
&\le \sum_{j=1}^m C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_{u_j}, < t_k) \quad \Big(\text{where } \Sigma_\Delta \equiv \sum_{j=1}^m \Delta_{\text{saved},j}\Big).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

\begin{proof}[of Corollary~\ref{cor:multi_axiom_pre_embed}]
For each $j \in \{1,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak m\}$, $\Delta_{\text{saved},j} \cdot \text{net\_bps} \cdot B_k^{(0)} \le 10000 \cdot C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_{u_j},\allowbreak < t_k) \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)}$. Summing over $j = 1,\allowbreak \dots,\allowbreak m$ yields $(\sum_{j=1}^m \Delta_{\text{saved},j}) \cdot \text{net\_bps} \cdot B_k^{(0)} \le 10000 \cdot (\sum_{j=1}^m C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_{u_j},\allowbreak < t_k)) \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)}$. Applying $\text{EscrowAlloc}(B_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Phi_k^{(0)},\allowbreak \Sigma_\Delta) \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)} \le B_k^{(0)} \Sigma_\Delta$ and dividing by $10000 \cdot \Phi_k^{(0)} > 0$ via \texttt{div\_le\_div\_right} yields Equation~\eqref{eq:multi_pre_embed}, while splitting across $m$ axioms strictly increases pointer overhead $\sum_{j=1}^m \ell_E(v_{u_j}) \ge m$ and gas fees.
\end{proof}

\textbf{Kraft's Inequality for Definition~\ref{def:shannon_dict}:} Defining the total smoothed dictionary weight $\Sigma_W \equiv \sum_{v_m \in \mathcal{V}_A^{(E)}} (W_E(v_m) + \epsilon_0)$, note that Equation~\eqref{eq:shannon_len} requires zero floating-point logarithms: $k$ is simply the smallest positive integer for which $2^k (W_E(v_u) + \epsilon_0)$ meets or exceeds $\Sigma_W + \epsilon_0$. Summing $2^{-\ell_E(v_u)} \le \frac{W_E(v_u) + \epsilon_0}{\Sigma_W + \epsilon_0}$ over all $v_u \in \mathcal{V}_A^{(E)}$ yields $\sum_{v_u} 2^{-\ell_E(v_u)} \le \frac{\Sigma_W}{\Sigma_W + \epsilon_0} < 1$, strictly satisfying Kraft's inequality for prefix-free decodability.

\subsection{Detailed Mechanics of \texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{T}_6$}{T6} Finder's Bounty, Two-Regime Rule, Propositions~\ref{prop:obfuscation_duality}--\ref{prop:trilemma}, and \texorpdfstring{$\mathcal{T}_4$}{T4} Priority Waterfall (Sections 4.3--4.4)}

\textbf{One-Time Deposit-Backed $\mathcal{T}_6$ Finder's Bounty ($B_{\text{finder}}$) \& Sybil-Resistant Universal Debt-Following Waterfall (\leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}):} Because on-chain wallet addresses are pseudonymous in a permissionless environment, conditioning waterfall priority on address equality ($\text{owner}(v_u) = \text{owner}(v_s)$ vs.\ $\text{owner}(v_u) \neq \text{owner}(v_s)$) would be vulnerable to trivial two-wallet Sybil splitting, while paying a perpetual royalty share to the $\mathcal{T}_6$ finder would both delay watchtower incentives until future citations occur and allow a clone creator to self-submit $\mathcal{T}_6$ as their own finder. Instead, ESM achieves address-independent protection via two complementary rules:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{One-Time Deposit-Backed $\mathcal{T}_6$ Finder's Bounty ($B_{\text{finder}}$):} Upon reveal adoption ($\mathcal{T}_1$, Branch~3), each registered axiom $v_i$ locks a syntactic-audit deposit $S_{\text{audit}}$ (baseline $S_{\text{audit}} = 20\text{ tokens}$, included in the Branch~3 locked stake $S^{\text{st}}$ and refunded in full to $\text{owner}(v_i)$ if $v_i$ remains unreduced at dataset closure or maturity). When a watchtower $h$ submits the first valid $\mathcal{T}_6(v_i,\allowbreak v_s,\allowbreak q,\allowbreak \text{fuel}_{\max})$ transaction reducing $v_i$ to root $v_s$, $h$ does not receive a perpetual cut of future royalties; instead, $S_{\text{audit}}$ is immediately slashed using the exact same integer split structure as Branches~6/7 and~14, paying $h$ a one-time fixed cap bounty $B_{\text{finder}} \equiv \lfloor (10000 - \kappa_{\text{bps}}) S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens}$ (under the parameter regime $c_{\text{search}} + g_{\text{T6}} \le 5$ over $\mathbb{N}$, so that $B_{\text{finder}} = 6\text{ tokens} > c_{\text{search}} + g_{\text{T6}}$) and permanently burning the remaining $S_{\text{audit}} - B_{\text{finder}} = 14\text{ tokens}$ ($70\%$). This design yields two immediate game-theoretic guarantees: (a) \emph{Subgame-Perfect Pre-Lockup Deterrence:} because $B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens} > c_{\text{search}} + g_{\text{T6}}$ is paid immediately upon $\mathcal{T}_6$ acceptance regardless of whether $v_i$ is ever cited, third-party watchtowers have a strict best response to reduce any plagiarized clone $v_i$ during the $N_{\text{lock}} = 10,000\text{ block}$ inspection window before $v_i$ can open RBF sponsorship; and (b) \emph{Elimination of Self-Finder Arbitrage:} if the clone creator self-submits $\mathcal{T}_6$ as finder, they merely recover $B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens}$ of their own locked $S_{\text{audit}}$ while permanently burning $S_{\text{audit}} - B_{\text{finder}} = 14\text{ tokens}$ (net loss $-(S_{\text{audit}} - B_{\text{finder}}) - g_{\text{T6}} = -14 - g_{\text{T6}} < 0$) and capture zero tokens from the redirected royalty stream.
  \item \textbf{Sybil-Resistant Two-Regime Rule (Pre-Lockup RBF Eligibility Gate \& Universal $2.0\times$ Debt-Following Waterfall, \leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}):}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{Regime 1 (Pre-Lockup RBF Eligibility Gate):} An axiom $v_u$ is permitted to open external RBF sponsorship ($B_{\text{target}}(v_u) > 0$) only after surviving the $N_{\text{lock}} = 10,000\text{ block}$ post-reveal inspection window without any $\mathcal{T}_6$ reduction ($\text{origin}(v_u) = v_u$). Any plagiarized clone reduced during $N_{\text{lock}}$ by a watchtower (incentivized by the immediate bounty $B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens}$) has $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u) = 0$, so $100\%$ of redirected post-anchor royalties on $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ flow directly to root $v_s$'s waterfall.
    \item \emph{Regime 2 (Identity-Free Post-Lockup Debt-Following Waterfall):} If $v_u$ survives $N_{\text{lock}}$, raises RBF burn $b_{u,s}$ ($D_{\text{tot}}(v_u) > 0$ under the $M_{\text{cap,bps}} = 20000$, i.e., $2.0\times$ repayment cap), and is subsequently reduced via $\mathcal{T}_6$ to an earlier root $v_s$ ($t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_s) < t_{\text{com}}^{\text{seq}}(v_u)$), the protocol applies the \textbf{Universal Debt-Following Waterfall} (\leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}) on $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ \emph{unconditionally and without referencing address identity}: $R^{\text{net}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}})$ repays $v_u$'s remaining approved RBF debt $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u)$ in full first ($\text{paid}_{u,p}$), then $v_s$'s RBF debt $D_{\text{tot}}(v_s)$ ($\text{paid}_{s,q}$), and distributes the residual $R^{\text{rem}}$ to dictionary guarantors (prioritizing any locked dictionary guarantors $\sum_s \overline{S}_{u,s,E}^{\text{dict,st}} > 0$ of $v_u$ during the $2\text{--}3$ epoch withdrawal grace window $t_{\text{eff}}(t_{\text{req}})$) and $\text{owner}(v_s)$. Once $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u)$ is retired, all subsequent perpetuity royalties on the redirected slot $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ accrue exclusively to $v_s$'s waterfall ($\text{owner}(v_s)$ and $v_s$'s guarantors), while only the residual on the non-reduced retained slot $x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ (corresponding to genuine non-reduced wrapper/extension code) remains with $\text{owner}(v_u)$.
  \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Detailed Derivation of Proposition~\ref{prop:obfuscation_duality} \& Proposition~\ref{prop:trilemma}:} Under Condition~(2) of Definition~\ref{def:t6_reduction} (Equation~\eqref{eq:blc_synthesis}), bounded $\beta\eta$-normalization within $T_{\max}$ steps strips trivial $\beta/\eta$ redex padding, regular dead-code wrappers, and mechanical continuation-passing style (CPS) transforms via an $O(1)$-bit normalizer adapter $q$. When a plagiarist registers and burns $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_i)$ for a clone $v_i$ and submits winning recipes citing $v_i$ themselves, once $\mathcal{T}_6$ reduces $v_i \to v_s$ with redirected slot fraction $\rho \equiv x_{i \to s}^{\text{tr}} / a_{i \to i}^{\text{tr}} \in (0,\allowbreak 1]$ and tranche citation share $f_{\text{cite}} \equiv a_{i \to i}^{\text{tr}} / \Delta_{\text{tr}} \in (0,\allowbreak 1]$, a fraction $(\gamma_{\text{bps}}/10000) \rho f_{\text{cite}}$ of net tranche escrow is redirected to $v_s$'s waterfall. Compared to the hypothetical baseline in which $v_i$ remained an unreduced independent axiom retaining $100\%$ of its self-citation payouts, the plagiarist requires a dataset recovery multiplier of $\frac{1}{1 - (\gamma_{\text{bps}}/10000) \rho f_{\text{cite}}} \in (1,\allowbreak \; 1.25]$ under $\gamma_{\text{bps}} = 2000\text{ bps}$ (reaching $1 / (1 - 0.20) = 1.25\times$ under full reduction $\rho = f_{\text{cite}} = 1$, and $1 / (1 - 0.20 \times 0.90) \approx 1.22\times$ when $\rho = 0.90,\allowbreak f_{\text{cite}} = 1$ as in Strategy~5 of Section~\ref{sec:agent_sim}) to recover their sunk burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_i)$.

For Proposition~\ref{prop:trilemma}, under (P1), a post-lockup state with an honest axiom owner $\text{owner}(v_u)$ funded by an independent RBF sponsor $s$ who later self-reduces $v_u \to v_s$ is state-indistinguishable from a plagiarist $\text{owner}(v_u)$ who self-sponsors $v_u$ via a Sybil wallet $s'$ and is later reduced to an honest root $v_s$. Satisfying (P2) requires paying $v_u$'s sponsors up to $M_{\text{cap}} b$ from $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$, whereas satisfying (P3) requires capping payouts to $v_u$'s sponsors at $\le b$, which is impossible whenever $M_{\text{cap}} b > b$ ($M_{\text{cap}} > 1$). ESM resolves this trilemma by separating prevention and debt protection across two temporal stages: (i) on the equilibrium path, because the one-time deposit-backed bounty $B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 3000 S_{\text{audit}} / 10000 \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens} > c_{\text{search}} + g_{\text{T6}}$ makes watchtower reduction strictly dominant during Regime~1 ($N_{\text{lock}} = 10,000\text{ blocks}$ prior to RBF eligibility), any clone $v_u$ of an existing public axiom $v_s$ is reduced before RBF sponsorship can open, enforcing $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u) = 0$ in subgame-perfect equilibrium; and (ii) off the equilibrium path (Regime~2, after $N_{\text{lock}}$), the Universal Debt-Following Waterfall strictly enforces (P1) and (P2) (\leanref{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves}), guaranteeing that bona fide RBF sponsors can never be defaulted on via self-reduction. \qed

\textbf{Rationale for Case B in Definition~\ref{def:t6_reduction}:} Suppose $v_3$ was initially reduced to $v_2$ ($\text{origin}(v_3) = v_2$), and later someone reduces $v_2$ to an even earlier root axiom $v_1$ ($\text{origin}(v_2) = v_1 \neq v_2$). To guarantee $O(1)$ gas complexity per transaction and avoid unbounded recursive pointer-chasing loops on-chain, $\mathcal{T}_6$ does not recursively traverse all descendants of $v_2$ when $v_2$ is reduced. Instead, Stage 2 attribution only redirects royalties to $\text{origin}(v_u)$ if $\text{origin}(\text{origin}(v_u)) = \text{origin}(v_u)$, and Case B allows any watchtower or $v_1$'s owner to re-point $v_3$ directly to root $v_1$ whenever $L_{\text{red}}^{\text{new}} \ge 1$, even if the two-hop composition adapter $q_{1 \to 3}$ is slightly longer than the single-hop adapter $q_{2 \to 3}$.

\textbf{Four-Step $\mathcal{T}_4$ Slot Waterfall Specification (Section 4.4):} Inside each slot $x \in \{x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}},\allowbreak x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}\}$ with gross royalty $R(x) \equiv \lfloor \gamma_{\text{bps}} E_{\text{net}} x / (10000 \Delta_{\text{tr}}) \rfloor$ (\leanref{slot\_four\_way\_split\_conserves}): (1) \emph{Data Anchor Rebate:} $R^{\text{anchor}}(x) \equiv \lfloor \beta_{D,\text{bps}} R(x) / 10000 \rfloor$ (baseline $1000\text{ bps} = 10\%$) goes to the anchor of $D_k$, leaving $R^{\text{net}}(x) \equiv R(x) - R^{\text{anchor}}(x)$. (2) \emph{RBF Burn-Sponsor Priority Repayment:} On a direct slot $x = x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ ($v_m = v_u$), aggregate repayment $R_{\text{repay}}(x) \equiv \min(R^{\text{net}}(x),\allowbreak D_{\text{tot}}(v_m))$ is deducted into $v_m$'s RBF pool, fixing $R^{\text{rem}}(x) \equiv R^{\text{net}}(x) - R_{\text{repay}}(x)$ with zero rounding leakage. Each sponsor $s$ receives:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:rbf_paid}
\begin{aligned}
\text{paid}_s(x) &\equiv \left\lfloor \frac{R_{\text{repay}}(x) \cdot D_{m,s}}{D_{\text{tot}}(v_m)} \right\rfloor, \quad
\text{div}_s(x) \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{\phi_{m,\text{bps}}^{\text{dict}} \cdot \overline{S}_{m,s,E}^{\text{dict,st}} \cdot R^{\text{rem}}(x)}{10000 \cdot \overline{S}_{m,E}^{\text{dict}}} \right\rfloor,
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
where the right-hand side defines the Step~3 dictionary guarantor dividend (Equation~\eqref{eq:rbf_paid}), and any integer remainder $R_{\text{repay}}(x) - \sum_s \text{paid}_s(x)$ is allocated in $1\text{-token}$ units in commit-priority order (FIFO / Largest-Remainder) to sponsors with $D_{m,s} > 0$ ($K_{\text{rbf}}^{\max} = 10$ max slots). On a redirected slot $x = x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$, the Universal Debt-Following Waterfall deducts $\text{paid}_u = \min(R^{\text{net}}(x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}),\allowbreak D_{\text{tot}}(v_u))$ first ($0$ if reduced during $N_{\text{lock}}$), leaving $R^{\text{mid}} \equiv R^{\text{net}} - \text{paid}_u$, and next deducts $\text{paid}_s = \min(R^{\text{mid}},\allowbreak D_{\text{tot}}(v_s))$, leaving $R^{\text{rem}} \equiv R^{\text{mid}} - \text{paid}_s$. (3) \emph{Dictionary Guarantor Dividend:} External stakers receive $\text{div}_s(x)$ per Equation~\eqref{eq:rbf_paid} (prioritizing $v_u$'s locked dictionary guarantors on $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$ during the $2\text{--}3$ epoch withdrawal grace window $t_{\text{eff}}(t_{\text{req}})$). (4) \emph{Axiom Owner Residual:} $R^{\text{owner}}(x) \equiv R^{\text{rem}}(x) - \sum_s \text{div}_s(x)$ accrues to $\text{owner}(v_u)$ on $x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}}$ and in full to $\text{owner}(v_s)$ on $x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}}$.

\begin{proof}[of Theorem~\ref{thm:main_theorem_1}]
For Part (1), let $d_{\text{used}} \equiv (d_{\text{init}} + d_{\text{abs}}) + d_{\text{hold}} + d_{\text{settled}}$ and $e_{\text{used}} \equiv (e_{\text{init}} + e_{\text{abs}}) + e_{\text{hold}} + e_{\text{settled}}$. Moreover, because $\Phi_k^{\text{best}} \ge 1$ (\texttt{local\_desc\_ge\_one}), $B_k^{\text{rem}} \ge 1$ whenever $B_k^{(0)} \ge 1$, ensuring the bounty pool can never be prematurely exhausted to zero while any compression potential remains. Part (2) follows from Lemma~\ref{lem:single_data_pre_embed}. For Part (3), each full-cap citation on a dataset $D_k$ where $v_u$ is cited by a third-party proposer yields net royalty $(1 - \beta_{D,\text{bps}}/10000)(\gamma_{\text{bps}}/10000)(1 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}}/10000) \frac{B_k^{(0)} \Delta_{\text{amort}}}{\Phi_k^{(0)}} \approx 0.90 \times 0.20 \times C_{\text{amort\_burn}} = 0.18 C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$, requiring $\lceil 1 / 0.18 \rceil = 6$ datasets to exceed $1.0 C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$. Part (4) follows by structural induction on the derivative list $l$ using \texttt{capStep\_conserves} ($(R - x) + (\text{acc} + x) = R + \text{acc}$) and subadditivity of floor division $R(x_s) + R(x_u) \le R(x_s + x_u)$ (\texttt{syntactic\_reduction\_waterfall\_valid}).
\end{proof}

\begin{example}[Analytical Numerical Example: Pre-Embedding vs.\ Multi-Dataset Reuse]
\label{ex:numerical_amort}
Consider baseline parameters $\epsilon_{\text{bps}} = 500$ ($5\%$), $\gamma_{\text{bps}} = 2000$ ($20\%$), $\beta_{D,\text{bps}} = 1000$ ($10\%$), and a dataset $D_k$ with $\Phi_k^{(0)} = 10^6\text{ bits}$ and $B_k^{(0)} = 10,000\text{ tokens}$.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Case 1 (Single-Dataset Pre-Embedding Attack):} An attacker embeds $L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u) = 10^5\text{ bits}$ of $D_k$-specific lookup data and burns $C_{\text{amort\_burn}} = 950\text{ tokens}$. By Equation~\eqref{eq:delta_amort}, $\Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_u,\allowbreak D_k) = \min\big(10^5,\allowbreak \lfloor \frac{10000 \times 950 \times 10^6}{9500 \times 10000} \rfloor\big) = 10^5\text{ bits}$. With dictionary code $\ell_E(v_u) = 10\text{ bits}$, $\Delta_{\text{saved}} = 99,990\text{ bits}$. The escrow carved out is $E_{\text{tr}} = \text{EscrowAlloc}(10000,\allowbreak 10^6,\allowbreak 99990) = 999\text{ tokens}$, and after the $5\%$ settlement burn, the net payout increment is $\Delta R_{\text{net}} = \lfloor 0.95 \times 999 \rfloor = 949\text{ tokens}$ (and even under the ceiling settlement form $E_{\text{tr}} - \lfloor 0.05 \times 999 \rfloor = 999 - 49 = 950\text{ tokens} \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}} = 950\text{ tokens}$, it never exceeds $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$ in either case). Subtracting DA registration burn $\epsilon_{\text{DA}} L_{\text{axiom}}(v_u)$ and gas fees $g_{\text{tx}}$ leaves the attacker with a strict net deficit.
  \item \textbf{Case 2 (Honest Universal Axiom Cited Across $N_{\text{cite}} = 8$ Datasets):} An honest researcher publishes a general scientific parser $v_s$ of length $10^5\text{ bits}$ backed by $C_{\text{amort\_burn}} = 950\text{ tokens}$ (possibly co-financed via RBF sponsors). Across each of $N_{\text{cite}} = 8$ independent datasets of comparable reward density ($E_{\text{net}} = 950\text{ tokens}$ per dataset), $v_s$ earns gross royalty $R = \lfloor 0.20 \times 950 \rfloor = 190\text{ tokens}$ and net royalty after the $10\%$ anchor rebate of $R^{\text{net}} = 190 - 19 = 171\text{ tokens}$ per dataset. Over $8$ datasets, cumulative net royalties total $8 \times 171 = 1,368\text{ tokens} > 950\text{ tokens}$, yielding a $+44\%$ net return above burn.
\end{itemize}
\end{example}

\subsection{Extensive-Form Game Strategy Spaces and Proofs of Theorem~\ref{thm:game_equilibria} and Proposition~\ref{prop:endogenous_watchtower} (Section 5.4)}
In $\Gamma_{\text{ESM}} = \big(\mathcal{N},\allowbreak (\Sigma_i)_{i \in \mathcal{N}},\allowbreak (u_i)_{i \in \mathcal{N}}\big)$, the strategy spaces $\Sigma_i$ are: (1) $\Sigma_{\text{Anchor}}$: register an authentic dataset $D_k$ with $V_{\text{ext}}(\Delta_k) \ge B_k^{(0)}$, or execute a synthetic wash-trading cycle; (2) $\Sigma_{\text{AxiomAuthor}}$: publish a reusable foundational axiom $v_s$ funded via self-burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$ or RBF sponsorship, register a single-dataset pre-embedded lookup table $v_{\text{pre}}$, or attempt a self-reduction $\mathcal{T}_6$ attack to evade RBF debt $D_{\text{tot}}(v_u) > 0$; (3) $\Sigma_{\text{Proposer}}$: submit an honest compression recipe citing amortized axioms $v_s$, inline an unamortized clone of $p_s$ to evade ancestor royalty $\gamma_{\text{bps}}$, submit a corrupted MST with error fraction $f \in (0,1)$ (Mode II-A grinding, with reveal/abort choice after observing VDF seed $\rho_{\text{batch}}$), or execute a two-recipe temporary reservation griefing attack $(e_1,\allowbreak e_2)$; (4) $\Sigma_{\text{Watchtower}}$: select post-adoption auditing effort determining $q_{\text{detect}} \in [0,1]$ for Mode II-A / Mode III falsification witnesses and $\mathcal{T}_6$ reductions; and (5) $\Sigma_{\text{Sponsor}}$: contribute RBF burn $b_{u,s} \le B_{\text{target}}(v_u)$ or underwriter collateral $S_{k,1}^{\text{uw}} \le \Lambda S_c$.

\begin{proof}[of Theorem~\ref{thm:game_equilibria}]
Theorems 49--53 in \texttt{ESM\_verified.lean} establish the exact natural-number payoff inequalities underlying each game-theoretic claim: Part (1) follows from \texttt{single\_data\_pre\_embedding\_unprofitable} ($\Delta R_{\text{net}} \le C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$) and \texttt{game\_pre\_embedding\_strictly\_dominated} ($\Delta R_{\text{net}} < C_{\text{amort\_burn}} + \text{burn\_da} + g_{\text{tx}}$ for $\text{burn\_da} + g_{\text{tx}} > 0$). Part (2) follows from the conservation and priority identity $\text{paid}_u = \min(R^{\text{net}},\allowbreak D_{\text{tot}}(v_u))$ in \texttt{m1\_redirect\_rbf\_priority\_conserves} (where the priority ordering is specified via sequential \texttt{let}-bindings in the waterfall definition and Lean mechanically proves exact sum conservation $R^{\text{anchor}} + \text{paid}_u + \text{paid}_s + \text{div} + R^{\text{owner}} = R$ under that specification). Part (3) follows from the cross-multiplied numerator inequality in \texttt{game\_griefing\_strictly\_dominated}. Part (4) combines the binomial pass probability $(1-f)^{32}$ with the scaled payoff inequality $((10000 - q_{\text{bps}})\text{net\_bps}) R_{\text{gross}} \le (q_{\text{bps}} \kappa_{\text{bps}}) S_c$ in \texttt{game\_watchtower\_deterrence\_equilibrium}. Part (5) follows from \texttt{game\_honest\_citation\_dominates\_unamortized\_clone}, which proves $\Delta E_{\text{net}} - \lfloor \gamma_{\text{bps}} \Delta E_{\text{net}} / 10000 \rfloor > 0$ for $\gamma_{\text{bps}} < 10000$ and $\Delta E_{\text{net}} > 0$.
\end{proof}

\textbf{Self-Front-Running Slash Accounting and Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:endogenous_watchtower}:} When an independent watchtower detects a Mode~II-A fraud, the proposer forfeits $100\%$ of creator collateral ($1.00 S_c$), of which $\kappa S_c = 0.70 S_c$ ($\kappa \equiv \kappa_{\text{bps}}/10000 = 0.70$) is permanently burned and $(1 - \kappa) S_c = 0.30 S_c$ is paid to the watchtower as hunter reward. If a cheating proposer who observes an incoming watchtower challenge can front-run their own puncture transaction with probability $\alpha \in [0,\allowbreak 1)$, the proposer's expected net collateral loss upon detection and the independent watchtower's expected hunter reward are:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:slash_alpha_accounting}
\begin{aligned}
L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha) &\equiv \big(1 - (1 - \kappa)\alpha\big) S_c \in [0.70 S_c, \; 1.00 S_c], \\
R_{\text{hunter}}(\alpha) &\equiv (1 - \alpha)(1 - \kappa) S_c = (1 - \alpha) 0.30 S_c.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
An independent watchtower is indifferent between auditing (expected net payoff $p_{\text{cheat}} R_{\text{hunter}}(\alpha) - c_{\text{audit}}$) and not auditing ($0$) if and only if $p_{\text{cheat}}^* = c_{\text{audit}} / R_{\text{hunter}}(\alpha)$. Given $q_{\text{detect}}$, a cheating proposer selects $f^* \in \arg\max_{f \in (0,\allowbreak f_{\text{grind}}(\Lambda)]} \Delta R_{\text{fraud}}(f)$ to maximize incremental gain $\Delta R_{\text{fraud}}$ and is indifferent between cheating at scale $\Delta R_{\text{fraud}}$ (expected incremental payoff $(1 - q_{\text{detect}}) \Delta R_{\text{fraud}} - q_{\text{detect}} L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha)$ over honest reporting) and reporting honestly ($0$) if and only if $q_{\text{detect}}^* = \Delta R_{\text{fraud}} / (\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} + L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha))$. Substituting $\Delta R_{\text{fraud}} \le 0.95 R_{\text{gross}}$, $L_{\text{slash}}(\alpha) \ge 0.70 S_c$, and $S_c \ge R_{\text{gross}} / (1 + \Lambda)$ recovers the worst-case upper bound $q_{\text{detect}}^* \le 0.95(1+\Lambda)/(0.70 + 0.95(1+\Lambda))$. \qed

\subsection{Derivations of Grinding Thresholds, Symmetric Puncture Release (Lemma~\ref{lem:release_symmetric}), and Griefing Margin (Section 6)}
\textbf{Grinding and Watchtower Threshold Derivations:} Under Mode II-A, an adversary submitting a corrupted MST with error fraction $f \in (0,1)$ passes the $K_{\text{spot}} = 32$ VDF spot-check with probability $P_{\text{pass}}(f) = (1 - f)^{32}$. With $\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}} = 10000$, $\epsilon_{\text{bps}} = 500$, unrevealed forfeiture $0.70 S_c$, and leverage $\Lambda \in \{0,\allowbreak 1\}$ ($S_c \ge R_{\text{gross}} / (1 + \Lambda)$):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Pre-Reveal Grinding Threshold ($q_{\text{detect}} = 0$):} Aborting whenever the sample hits a corrupted leaf yields non-positive expected profit whenever $P_{\text{pass}}(f) \cdot 0.95 R_{\text{gross}} - (1 - P_{\text{pass}}(f)) \cdot 0.70 \frac{R_{\text{gross}}}{1 + \Lambda} \le 0 \iff (1 - f)^{32} \le \frac{0.70}{0.70 + 0.95(1 + \Lambda)}$, giving $(1-f)^{32} \le 0.70/1.65 \implies \mathbf{f \ge 2.65\%}$ (exact: $2.644\%$) at $\Lambda = 0$, and $(1-f)^{32} \le 0.70/2.60 \implies \mathbf{f \ge 4.02\%}$ (exact: $4.018\%$) at $\Lambda = 1$.
  \item \emph{Post-Reveal Watchtower Threshold ($f \to 0^+$):} Revealing on-chain exposes the recipe to Mode II-A slashing of $100\%$ of $S_{k,1}^{\text{creator}} = R_{\text{gross}}/(1+\Lambda)$. Even under worst-case self-front-running ($\alpha \to 1$, net slash $0.70 S_c$), $(1 - q_{\text{detect}}) \cdot 0.95 R_{\text{gross}} - q_{\text{detect}} \cdot 0.70 \frac{R_{\text{gross}}}{1 + \Lambda} \le 0$ holds for all $f > 0$ whenever $q_{\text{detect}} \ge 0.95(1+\Lambda)/(0.70 + 0.95(1+\Lambda))$, yielding $0.95/1.65 \approx \mathbf{0.576}$ ($\Lambda=0$) and $1.90/2.60 \approx \mathbf{0.731}$ ($\Lambda=1$) (or $0.487$ and $0.655$ when $\alpha = 0$).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Symmetric Puncture Release (Lemma~\ref{lem:release_symmetric}) and Temporary Reservation Griefing:} When a tranche is punctured from $\Delta_{k,1}^{\text{tr}} > 0$ to $\Delta_{\text{rem}}^{\text{tr}}$, slots scale via:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:puncture_slot_scale}
x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr,new}} \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}} \Delta_{\text{rem}}^{\text{tr}}}{\Delta_{k,1}^{\text{tr}}} \right\rfloor, \qquad
x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr,new}} \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}} \Delta_{\text{rem}}^{\text{tr}}}{\Delta_{k,1}^{\text{tr}}} \right\rfloor,
\end{equation}
with $\delta x_s \equiv x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr}} - x_{u \to s}^{\text{tr,new}}$, $\delta x_u \equiv x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr}} - x_{u \to u}^{\text{tr,new}}$, and symmetric release:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:symmetric_release}
S_{\text{paid}}(v_s, D_k) \mathrel{-}= \delta x_s, \qquad
S_{\text{paid}}(v_u, D_k) \mathrel{-}= (\delta x_s + \delta x_u).
\end{equation}
In Lemma~\ref{lem:release_symmetric}, because Stage 1 added $a_{i \to u}^{\text{tr}} = x_s + x_u$ to $S_{\text{paid}}(v_u,\allowbreak D_k)$, subtracting $(\delta x_s + \delta x_u)$ restores $\text{base} + \lfloor x_s r / d \rfloor + \lfloor x_u r / d \rfloor$ identically (\texttt{release\_symmetric}), whereas subtracting only $\delta x_u$ leaves an un-released phantom reservation of $\delta x_s > 0$ whenever $\lfloor x_s r / d \rfloor < x_s$ (mechanized counterexample in \texttt{old\_rule\_leaks}: for $\text{base}=0,\allowbreak x_s=10,\allowbreak x_u=10,\allowbreak r=1,\allowbreak d=2$, $(0+10+10)-(10-5) = 15 \neq 10$). Under temporary reservation griefing, slashing splits creator stake via:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:slash_split}
\begin{aligned}
S_{\text{slash}}^c &\equiv \left\lceil \frac{r_{\text{bps}} S_{k,1}^{\text{creator}}}{10000} \right\rceil, \quad
S_{\text{hunter}} \equiv \left\lfloor \frac{(10000 - \kappa_{\text{bps}}) S_{\text{slash}}^c}{10000} \right\rfloor, \\
S_{\text{burn}}^c &\equiv S_{\text{slash}}^c - S_{\text{hunter}} \ge \left\lceil \frac{\kappa_{\text{bps}} S_{\text{slash}}^c}{10000} \right\rceil.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Because bogus recipe $e_1$ locks at most $x \le \Delta_{\text{tr},1}$ bits of $v_s$'s cap, the maximum royalty evaded by colluding recipe $e_2$ is bounded by $\frac{\gamma_{\text{bps}}(10000 - \epsilon_{\text{bps}})}{10000^2} E_{\text{tr},1}$, while permanent burn satisfies $S_{\text{burn}}^c \ge \frac{\kappa_{\text{bps}}}{10000} \frac{\sigma_{\text{bps}}^{\text{col}}}{10000 + \Lambda_{\text{bps}}} E_{\text{tr},1}$ ($r_{\text{bps}}=10000$), ensuring $S_{\text{burn}}^c > R_{\text{evade}}^{\max}$ for every $E_{\text{tr},1} \ge 1$ under Equation~\eqref{eq:griefing_constraint} ($3500\text{ bps} > 1900\text{ bps}$ at baseline).

\textbf{Hardware-Accelerated BLC Jets (Precompiles) for Numerical Workloads:} While pure Scott-encoded bit-list arithmetic in untyped $\lambda$-calculus keeps the formal semantics of $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$ minimal and executes symbolic/bitwise laws in $9.0\times 10^2$ to $7.5\times 10^3$ KAM steps (Table~\ref{tab:blc_benchmarks}), dense 64-bit matrix or floating-point emulation in pure $\lambda$-calculus would incur large constant-factor step overhead. Following the established design of verified functional virtual machines, ESM supports \textbf{Hardware-Accelerated BLC Jets}: canonical BLC sub-terms (identified by their exact $\mathcal{T}_0/\mathcal{T}_1$ Merkle hashes, such as 64-bit integer modular multiplication, bitwise XOR, and fixed-point inner products) whose pure $\lambda$-reduction semantics remain unchanged, but which the KAM interpreter and STARK Algebraic Intermediate Representation (AIR) evaluate in a single native word transition. Because a jet's bit-length and input-output relation are identical to its underlying BLC $\lambda$-term, jets preserve exact MDL Kolmogorov/Rissanen semantics while reducing both off-chain KAM execution time and STARK trace height by two to three orders of magnitude.

\subsection{Extended Protocol for Mechanism Ablation, 3-Axis Sweeps, and Adaptive Learning (Section 8.4)}\label{app:sec8_d4}
\textbf{Model and Assumptions (\texttt{esm\_exp3\_ablation.py}).} All five mechanisms (M1, M2, M3, M4$-$, M4) face the same stream of $N = 12$ Benchmark-3-type datasets ($\Phi_k^{(0)} = 16,384\text{ bits}$, $B = 10,000\text{ tokens}$, $L_{\text{res}} = 750\text{ bits}$; baseline $\epsilon_{\text{bps}} = 500$, $\gamma_{\text{bps}} = 2000$, $\beta_{D,\text{bps}} = 1000$, $\kappa_{\text{bps}} = 7000$, $B_{\text{finder}} = 6$, $S_{\text{audit}} = 20$). Model assumptions of this stylized analysis are a development cost $c_{\text{dev}} = 20\text{ tokens}$ per independently discovered axiom, base transaction gas $g_{\text{tx}} = 1\text{ token}$, $\mathcal{T}_6$ gas $g_{\mathcal{T}_6} = 2 g_{\text{tx}} + c_{\text{step}} \cdot \text{fuel}_{\max}$ with the execution fuel term normalized to $c_{\text{step}} \cdot \text{fuel}_{\max} = 1\text{ token}$ (yielding $g_{\mathcal{T}_6} = 3\text{ tokens}$ at baseline $g_{\text{tx}} = 1$), search cost $c_{\text{search}} = 1\text{ token}$, and a DA fee of $\lceil 10^{-4}\,\text{bits} \rceil$ tokens. The agents are (i) an honest root-axiom author $H$ ($|p_s| = 85\text{ bits}$, own burn bounded by a capital budget; Stackelberg leader), (ii) a clone plagiarist $Q$ (exact $85\text{-bit}$ copy or $103\text{-bit}$ padded copy of $p_s$ with a free choice of burn), (iii) a pre-embedding attacker $P$ who knows $D_1$ in advance and registers a $16,400\text{-bit}$ lookup axiom, (iv) recipe proposers who, for each dataset, either cite a registered axiom or inline its code so as to maximize $E_{\text{net}}$ minus royalties (the lowest $\Phi_{\text{local}}$ wins the tranche), (v) a watchtower $W$ that submits $\mathcal{T}_6$ against a clone if and only if $B_{\text{finder}} - g_{\mathcal{T}_6} - c_{\text{search}} = 2 > 0$, and (vi) an RBF sponsor that funds the root's burn only if its $2.0\times$ debt claim repays at least its principal. After $H$ commits, $Q$ and $P$ play a simultaneous Nash subgame; every action is drawn from a finite grid and best responses are obtained by exhaustive enumeration (no randomness). Social welfare is $W = V \cdot S_{\text{true}} - \text{real\_costs}$ with $V = B / \Phi_k^{(0)}$ tokens per bit, where $S_{\text{true}}$ is the true corpus-level description-length saving (each distinct axiom content counted once, lookup tables counted in full) and burns, escrow, and royalties are transfers; real costs are development, gas, DA, and watchtower work. Three scenarios are reported: S1 (well-funded author with a $100$-token budget; proposers break exact ties in favor of the earlier-registered root), S2 (capital-constrained author with a $12$-token budget), and S3 (S1 with an adversarial tie-break in which proposers prefer the clone on exact ties).

\textbf{Result 1 (Pre-Embedding Defense: M2 vs.\ M3/M4).} Without burn backing (M2), the pre-embedding attacker $P$ extracts $9,271\text{ tokens}$ at $D_1$ (about $93\%$ of that tranche's escrow $E_{\text{tr}} = 9,962$ tokens) and social welfare degrades from $113,894$ to $104,345\text{ tokens}$ ($-8.4\%$). In M3, M4$-$, and M4, the best pre-embedding attack that wins $D_1$ yields $-238\text{ tokens} < 0$, consistent with the negative-profit bound of Lemma~\ref{lem:single_data_pre_embed}, so extraction is deterred to $0$.

\textbf{Result 2 (Incentive to Publish and Tie-Break Robustness: M1 and M3 vs.\ M4$-$).} Under the Hutter-style prize (M1) there are no citation royalties, so the root axiom is never published and every proposer re-develops it inline: welfare is $113,193\text{ tokens}$ ($701\text{ tokens}$ below ESM) and the author earns nothing. Under the adversarial tie-break of S3, M3, which lacks $\mathcal{T}_6$, collapses: a clone captures the royalties, the author withdraws from the market (published net $0$), and welfare falls to $113,193$. M4$-$ and M4 instead keep the author's publication equilibrium independent of the tie-break rule through $\mathcal{T}_6$ (royalties $108\text{ tokens}$, net $+36\text{ tokens}$, maximal welfare $113,894\text{ tokens}$).

\textbf{Result 3 (Capital-Constrained Author and RBF Synergy: M3 vs.\ M4$-$ vs.\ M4).} In the counterfactual where an author with only $12\text{ tokens}$ of own burn publishes anyway (Table~\ref{tab:ablation_dormant}), M3 lets the clone $Q$ capture $+58\text{ tokens}$ and leaves the author zero royalties (net $-34$). M4$-$ ($\mathcal{T}_6$ alone) cuts the clone's profit to $+32\text{ tokens}$ (about $-45\%$) and returns $12\text{ tokens}$ of royalties to the author, but does not by itself remove the author's capital shortfall (net $-22$). Adding RBF sponsor co-financing (M4, sponsor burn $38\text{ tokens}$) lifts the total burn to $12 + 38 = 50\text{ tokens}$, i.e., full amortization of the $85\text{-bit}$ root: the clone is fully excluded ($Q$ payoff $0$), the sponsor earns $+38\text{ tokens}$, and the author's net payoff, still $-2\text{ tokens}$ at $N = 12$, becomes $+106\text{ tokens}$ at $N = 24$ (welfare $227,861\text{ tokens}$). At $N = 12$, a capital-constrained author does not publish in any burn-backed mechanism, whereas at $N = 24$ only M4 sustains publication.

\textbf{Robustness Across Cost and Information Assumptions (\texttt{esm\_exp3\_ablation.py}, Experiment~4b):}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Axiom Development Cost ($c_{\text{dev}} \in [5,\allowbreak 80]\text{ tokens}$):} Pre-embedding exploitation under M2 ($+9,271\text{ tokens}$), pre-embedding deterrence under M3/M4$-$/M4 (best winning attack $-238\text{ tokens}$), and clone exclusion via M4 sponsor co-financing hold across the entire tested range. Under both S1 and S3, the well-funded honest author publishes in equilibrium at $N = 12$ if and only if $c_{\text{dev}} \le \mathbf{56\text{ tokens}}$, matching the exact closed form $108\text{ (royalties)} - 50\text{ (burn)} - 1\text{ (DA)} - 1\text{ (gas)} = 56\text{ tokens}$. For the capital-constrained author (own burn $12\text{ tokens}$), equilibrium publication at $N = 24$ is recovered exclusively by M4 (RBF co-financing) across $\mathbf{11 \le c_{\text{dev}} \le 126\text{ tokens}}$ (for $c_{\text{dev}} \le 10\text{ tokens}$, M4$-$ alone suffices to sustain publication at $N = 24$, whereas at $N = 12$ M4 sustains publication for $c_{\text{dev}} \le \mathbf{18\text{ tokens}}$).
  \item \emph{Transaction Gas ($g_{\text{tx}} \in [0,\allowbreak 5]\text{ tokens}$) \& $S_{\text{audit}}$ Scaling Boundary:} If the syntactic-audit deposit is held fixed at its baseline $S_{\text{audit}} = 20\text{ tokens}$ ($B_{\text{finder}} = \lfloor 0.30 S_{\text{audit}} \rfloor = 6\text{ tokens}$), the watchtower's net profit condition $B_{\text{finder}} - (2 g_{\text{tx}} + 1) - c_{\text{search}} > 0$ holds if and only if $g_{\text{tx}} \le \mathbf{1\text{ token}}$; for $g_{\text{tx}} \ge 2\text{ tokens}$, watchtowers no longer submit $\mathcal{T}_6$ and M4$-$ degrades to M3 in S3 (author net drops to $0$). However, scaling the minimum required audit deposit in accordance with the watchtower profitability condition $\lfloor 0.30 S_{\text{audit}} \rfloor > 2 g_{\text{tx}} + c_{\text{step}}\cdot\text{fuel}_{\max} + c_{\text{search}}$ of Section~4.3 to $S_{\text{audit}} \ge \mathbf{24\text{ tokens}}$ at $g_{\text{tx}} = 2$ ($g_{\mathcal{T}_6} = 5,\allowbreak B_{\text{finder}} = 7$) or $S_{\text{audit}} \ge \mathbf{44\text{ tokens}}$ at $g_{\text{tx}} = 5$ ($g_{\mathcal{T}_6} = 11,\allowbreak B_{\text{finder}} = 13$) completely restores the S3 publication equilibrium for M4$-$ and M4 (yielding author net $\mathbf{+35\text{ tokens}}$ and $\mathbf{+32\text{ tokens}}$, respectively; S1 publication under M3 holds for $g_{\text{tx}} \le 37$ and M4 publication at $N = 24$ holds for $g_{\text{tx}} \le 107$).
  \item \emph{Attacker Advance Knowledge ($p_{\text{know}} = k / 16,384 \in [0.25,\allowbreak 1.00]$):} When the pre-embedding attacker knows $k$ bits of $D_1$ in advance and encodes the remaining $16,384 - k$ unknown bits raw at $1\text{ bit/bit}$ in the recipe residual ($L_{\text{res}} = 34 + (16,384 - k)$, $|p_P| = k + 16$), the closed-form condition for beating the honest recipe ($\Phi_{\text{local}} = 822\text{ bits}$) under M2 is $24 + 4 + 34 + (16,384 - k) < 822$, which holds if and only if $k \ge \mathbf{15,625\text{ bits}}$ ($p_{\text{know}} \ge \mathbf{95.37\%}$). Under M3/M4$-$/M4, the attacker cannot even win $D_1$ unless $k \ge \mathbf{15,643\text{ bits}}$ ($p_{\text{know}} \ge \mathbf{95.48\%}$), and even above that threshold its best winning payoff remains strictly negative across the entire winning range ($\mathbf{-229\text{ tokens}}$ just above the threshold $k = 15,643$ down to $\mathbf{-238\text{ tokens}}$ at $p_{\text{know}} = 1.00$).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Adaptive No-Regret Learning Dynamics (\texttt{esm\_exp3\_ablation.py --learning}, Experiment~4c):} Every agent ($H,\allowbreak Q,\allowbreak P,\allowbreak W$) initializes at the uniform mixed strategy over its finite action set and simultaneously updates its weights over $T = 200\text{ episodes}$ ($20$ fixed random seeds, learning rate $\eta = 0.3$, payoff scale $10\text{ tokens}$) via $w_a \leftarrow w_a \exp(\eta u_a / \text{scale})$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{L1 (Baseline Market Convergence \& Exponential Attack Decay):} Under M2 (free citation exemption), $P$'s pre-embedding probability rises from $0.500\,(t=1)$ to $\mathbf{1.000}$ by episode $t = 25$, degrading realized social welfare to $W_{200} = \mathbf{104,345\text{ tokens}}$. Under M4 (ESM Full), by contrast, $P$'s pre-embedding probability decays monotonically as $0.800\,(t=1) \to 0.1034\,(t=25) \to 0.0120\,(t=50) \to 0.0001\,(t=100) \to \mathbf{0.0000}\,(t=200)$ at a fitted exponential rate of $\mathbf{0.0861\text{/episode}}$ between $t = 25$ and $t = 50$---matching the theoretical rate $\eta \cdot 3 / \text{scale} = 0.0900\text{/episode}$ of the slowest-decaying losing lookup action ($-3\text{ tokens}$). Simultaneously, $Q$'s cloning probability decays as $0.923\,(t=1) \to 0.0032\,(t=25) \to \mathbf{0.0000}\,(t \ge 50)$, and the honest author $H$'s publication probability converges to $\mathbf{1.000}$ (modal action: own burn $50$), achieving the exact SPE welfare $W^* = \mathbf{113,894\text{ tokens}}$ across all tested learning rates $\eta \in \{0.1,\allowbreak 0.3,\allowbreak 1.0\}$.
  \item \emph{L2 (Boundary Reproduction, Simultaneous-Move Stagnation, \& Sequential Watchtower Verification):} For the capital-constrained author at $N = 24$ (budget $12\text{ tokens}$), M4$-$ converges to non-publication ($P(\text{publish}) = 0.000$, $W_{200} = 226,386\text{ tokens}$), whereas M4 (RBF co-financing) converges to $P(\text{publish}) = \mathbf{1.000}$ with modal action $(h = 12,\allowbreak r = 38)$ and full SPE welfare $W_{200} = \mathbf{227,861\text{ tokens}}$. Under the adversarial tie-break S3 ($N = 12$, budget $100$), M3 collapses to $P(\text{publish}) = 0.0000$ ($W_{200} = 113,193\text{ tokens}$), whereas simultaneous-move M4 maintains $P(\text{publish}) = \mathbf{0.9500}$ and realized welfare $W_{200} = \mathbf{113,859\text{ tokens}}$ ($+666\text{ tokens}$ above M3) with a small residual cloning probability of $P(Q\text{ clones}) \approx 0.0498$ ($-35\text{ tokens}$ below the pure SPE $W^* = 113,894$). This residual reflects a classic simultaneous-move dynamic of the Verifier's Dilemma \cite{luu2015}: when $Q$ and $W$ update unconditioned mixed strategies simultaneously, as cloning becomes rare, $W$ rarely collects $B_{\text{finder}}$ and its auditing probability plateaus at $P(W\text{ watches}) \approx 0.6250$. Evaluating the \emph{sequential (conditional) watchtower} setting (\texttt{conditional\_w} in \texttt{esm\_exp3\_ablation.py}, where $W$ observes the on-chain registry and submits $\mathcal{T}_6$ conditionally whenever a clone is registered to collect the deterministic net gain $B_{\text{finder}} - g_{\mathcal{T}_6} - c_{\text{search}} = +2\text{ tokens} > 0$) confirms empirically that in S3-M4, play reaches $P(H\text{ publishes}) = \mathbf{1.0000}$ and $P(Q\text{ clones}) = \mathbf{0.0000}$ by episode $t = 25$, and converges at $t = 200$ to $P(H\text{ publishes}) = \mathbf{1.0000}$ (modal action $(50,\allowbreak 0)$ with mean probability $1.000$), $P(Q\text{ clones}) = \mathbf{0.0000}$, $P(P\text{ pre-embeds}) = \mathbf{0.0000}$, and realized social welfare $W_{200} = \mathbf{113,894\text{ tokens}} = W^*$ ($\vert{}W_{200} - W^*\vert{} = \mathbf{0.0}$), eliminating the simultaneous-move residual completely.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Scope of the Ablation.} This evaluation combines an exact deterministic subgame-perfect equilibrium model with multi-agent no-regret (Hedge) learning over a stylized environment (identical datasets, an attacker with advance knowledge of $D_1$, no inter-attacker collusion, the cost parameters swept above, and RBF financing fixed prior to attacker moves rather than the post-hoc Regime~2 of Proposition~\ref{prop:trilemma}); it isolates incentive structure, exact deterrence boundaries, and learning convergence across mechanisms rather than measuring live mainnet traffic.


\subsection{Extended Discussion of Limitations and Phased L2 Deployment Roadmap (Section 9)}\label{app:sec8_ext9}
We delineate five structural limitations and a phased L2 deployment roadmap: (1) \textbf{Clean-Room Re-Implementation with Large Edit Distance:} While $\mathcal{T}_6$ catches verbatim copying, variable renaming, regular dead-code padding, and bounded refactoring within $T_{\max} = 2^{20}$, arbitrary semantic equivalence is undecidable by Rice's theorem \cite{li2019}: on the $115\text{-b}$ unoptimized reference form of $v_s$, an $\eta$-expanded recursive clone ($122\text{ b}$) verifies as \texttt{True} in $\text{fuel}_{\text{total}} = 2,630$ ($\text{kam} = 10,\allowbreak \text{beta} = 18,\allowbreak \text{nodes} = 7,\allowbreak \text{work} = 2,595$), whereas an aperiodic dummy-accumulator clone ($143\text{ b}$) exhausts $T_{\max} = 1,048,576$ ($\text{work} = 1,044,716$) and is rejected as \texttt{None} [fuel-exhausted], though it must still fund its own upfront self-burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}(v_i)$ under Lemma~\ref{lem:single_data_pre_embed} (and Extension~3 RBF co-financing eliminates residual exposure; Table~\ref{tab:ablation_dormant}); (2) \textbf{Protection Ceiling Proportional to Burn $C_{\text{amort\_burn}}$:} By Theorem~\ref{thm:main_theorem_1}(4) (\leanref{capFold\_general\_bound}), cumulative redirected bits to root $v_s$ on $D_k$ are bounded by $\text{Saved}^{(E)}(v_s,\allowbreak D_k) \le \Delta_{\text{amort}}(v_s,\allowbreak D_k)$, mitigated via Extension~3 RBF sponsorship; (3) \textbf{1-of-$N$ Honest Watchtower Liveness \& Gas-Indexed $S_{\text{audit}}$:} Deterring microscopic corruptions ($f < 2.65\%$ at $\Lambda=0$ or $4.02\%$ at $\Lambda=1$) and pre-RBF clone passage during $N_{\text{lock}}$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:trilemma}) relies on 1-of-$N$ watchtower liveness, supported by gas-indexed $S_{\text{audit}} \ge 24\text{ tok}$ ($g_{\text{tx}}=2$) or $44\text{ tok}$ ($g_{\text{tx}}=5$) and sequential on-chain observation (\texttt{conditional\_w}, converging to $P(H)=1.0000$, $P(Q)=0.0000$, $W_{200} = 113,894 = W^*$); (4) \textbf{STARK Prover Memory and GPU Latency for $U_{\text{BLC}}^{T_{\max}}$:} Symbolic/bitwise laws execute in $9.0 \times 10^2$ to $7.5 \times 10^3$ KAM steps ($1.0 \times 10^7$ RV32IM instructions and $\approx 300\text{ KiB}$ peak heap for Benchmark~1 at $K_{\text{spot}} = 32$ in \texttt{esm-guest}; Table~\ref{tab:blc_benchmarks}), while worst-case STARK proving at $T_{\max} = 2^{20}$ benefits from Hardware-Accelerated BLC Jets (Appendix~\ref{app:sec8}); and (5) \textbf{Phased L2 Rollup + External DA Deployment Roadmap:} \textbf{Phase 1} deploys the verified EIP-170 Solidity contracts (\texttt{ESM.sol} / \texttt{ESMOps.sol}) on an L2 rollup anchoring $D_k$ and MST blobs on external DA layers (EIP-4844, Celestia, EigenDA), \textbf{Phase 2} integrates the RV32IM zkVM guest (\texttt{esm-guest}) and custom KAM-STARK circuit ($\mathcal{T}_1,\allowbreak \mathcal{T}_2,\allowbreak \mathcal{T}_6$) with VDF beacons, and \textbf{Phase 3} launches mainnet DeFi RBF sponsorship pools and watchtowers.
